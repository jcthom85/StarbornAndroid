# Privacy Policy for Starborn

**Last updated:** September 3, 2026

**Junewire Games** ("we", "our", or "us") develops and publishes **Starborn** ("the Game"), a single-player narrative sci-fi role-playing game.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with Starborn on Android devices.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

**Starborn does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or sensitive user data.**

- **No Personal Data:** We do not collect names, email addresses, physical addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, or any other personal identifiers.
- **No Device Identifiers or Tracking:** We do not track Advertising IDs, Android IDs, IMEI numbers, IP addresses, or device fingerprints.
- **No Analytics or Telemetry:** We do not integrate third-party analytics SDKs, crash trackers, or behavior profiling tools.
- **No Account Creation:** Starborn does not require an account, sign-in, or registration of any kind to play.

---

### 2. Device Permissions

Starborn requests only the minimum necessary system permissions to function:

- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Used exclusively to provide haptic feedback during in-game physical impacts, explosions, and UI interactions.

No camera, microphone, location, storage, or network permissions are requested or used by the game.

---

### 3. Local Game Saves

All game progress, inventory, character statistics, dialogue choices, and settings are stored **locally on your device** within the application's private sandboxed data directory. 

- This data never leaves your device.
- You can permanently delete this data at any time by clearing the app's storage via your device's Settings or by uninstalling the application.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

- **No Advertising:** Starborn contains **no advertisements**, banner ads, rewarded video ads, or commercial tracking networks.
- **No Microtransactions:** Starborn does not offer in-app purchases, loot boxes, or financial transactions.

---

### 5. Children’s Privacy

Starborn does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected by the application whatsoever, Starborn complies with the requirements of the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international privacy standards.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.
