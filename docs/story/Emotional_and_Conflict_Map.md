# Starborn emotional pacing map

Status: Supporting editorial reference, reconciled October 14, 2026. An editing lens, not a second plot specification or script. [Master Story](Starborn_Master_Story.md) owns events; [Character Arcs](Character_Arcs.md) owns personal development.

| Sequence | Pressure and contrast | Relationship work | Payoff to protect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mines | Exhaustion and repairs become administrative threat, discovery, then hunted escape | Nova and Jed argue about survival; Zeke moves from procedure to personal risk | Jed holds the failing mechanism; Nova leaves with Zeke and a loss she cannot repair |
| Crash | Escape becomes loss of control and physical danger | Zeke takes responsibility for his unverified splice without blaming Nova | Survival makes them dependent on each other |
| Sector 9 | Beauty, isolation, unfamiliar ruins, and ship repairs | Orion's displacement; Gh0st's voluntary turn; early trust with limits | The crew earns a way out and a place to return |
| Spire | Improvised heist confidence meets class exclusion, exposure, and pursuit | Zeke confronts old access and shame; Nova coordinates people | They escape with the Lens, aware of how precarious success was |
| Foundry | Heat, connected machinery, worker danger, and Gh0st's production history | Gh0st determines his response to Rylos; Nova supports his choice | Engine recovery and autonomy land before Vale's coup escalates the threat |
| Orbital Ring | Polished comfort conceals confinement and coercion | Elara's reunion; lost access tests Zeke; Nova's power proves insufficient | Vale executes Thorne and opens the Tear; the crew follows |
| Source | Familiar histories become disturbing spaces; cooperation overcomes isolation | Companions demonstrate changed choices under pressure | Nova relinquishes Source access and retains herself; a regional opening is won |

## Editing questions

- What immediate problem or want makes the conversation necessary?
- Which companions disagree, and what legitimate priority does each protect?
- Does a quiet beat offer rest, food, a habit, humor, or conversation rather than more exposition?
- Does behavior change from an earlier comparable situation?
- Does the player participate meaningfully in the turn?
- Is dialogue explaining a lesson the scene already showed?

Give grief and uncertainty time without turning every pause into a thesis. Source imagery can serve a particular discovery; the whole party need not share Orion's metaphors.

## Retired claims

Earlier versions described the colony as safe, Gh0st as an Elara backup, routine strain as memory loss, and the ending as the end of the war. Current canon preserves Gh0st's independent identity and Nova's memories; the wider Dominion survives. Verify specific breather placement and access gates against the live route before calling them mandatory scenes.
