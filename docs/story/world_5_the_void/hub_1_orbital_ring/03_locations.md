## Hub Layout
The halo of the planet. Vacuum silence, recycled air, sterile white light.

**Progression Model:** Combat Entry → Propaganda/Paradise Split → Descent.

## Stage 1
### Node 1: Executive Dock
*   **Icon:** ⚓
*   **Type:** Start / Combat
*   **Description:** The airlock hisses open to a red carpet that floats slightly off the floor. Bodies of Thorne’s loyalists float in zero-G pockets.
*   **Internal Content:** Airlock.
*   **Purpose:** Combat Entry.
*   **Unlocks:** Stage 2 (Grand Concourse AND Solarium).

## Stage 2 (Choice)
### Node 2A: Grand Concourse
*   **Icon:** 🏨
*   **Type:** Choice A / Lore
*   **Description:** A wide hallway lined with golden statues of the Architects. Propaganda flickers on every screen.
*   **Internal Content:** Statues.
*   **Purpose:** Lore / Exploration.
*   **Unlocks:** Stage 3 (Security Hub).

### Node 2B: Solarium
*   **Icon:** 🏖️
*   **Type:** Choice B / Breather
*   **Description:** A massive biodome mimicking a tropical beach. The "sun" is a fusion lamp. The fruit is rotting.
*   **Internal Content:** Fake Beach.
*   **Purpose:** Breather.
*   **Unlocks:** Stage 3 (Security Hub).

## Stage 3
### Node 3: Security Hub
*   **Icon:** 🛡️
*   **Type:** Combat
*   **Description:** The fortified checkpoint guarding the lift to the core.
*   **Internal Content:** Armory.
*   **Purpose:** Combat: Elite Guards.
*   **Unlocks:** Stage 4 (Service Shaft).

## Stage 4
### Node 4: Service Shaft
*   **Icon:** ⬇️
*   **Type:** Gate
*   **Description:** A vertical shaft where gravity is disabled. You pull yourself along cables.
*   **Internal Content:** Zero-G Drop.
*   **Purpose:** Transition to Hub 5.2 (Deep Ring).
*   **Unlocks:** Hub 5.2 (Deep Ring).
