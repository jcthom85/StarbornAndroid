# Starborn — Main Characters

**Status:** Supporting character reference; subordinate to the Master Story, Lore Bible, and Character Arcs. Combat details may express canon but may not override narrative causality or artifact rules.

## Nova

**Role:** Protagonist / The Spark (Accelerates Team Tempo - Cooldown Reduction)
**Age:** 22
**Archetype:** Striker (High-Speed Ranged)
**Opening Tool/Weapon:** Illegally modified Dominion mining cutter
**Combat Weapon:** Dual Laser Guns
**Key Relics:** **The Tuning Fork** (Mines) & **The Anchor** (Void)

Nova is a scrappy young adult colony scavenger with a rogue vibe—steals, hacks, survives. She’s funny, sharp, reckless in a controlled way, and initially equates usefulness with safety. Story-wise, she’s the "small person vs huge system" lens. She has no Aethel ancestry, chosen status, or genetic compatibility. Her modified cutter wakes the Tuning Fork; her manual counter-tune and Jed's repaired safety gear let her survive the handshake. When she becomes **imprinted** by its protocol, she acts as the **Spark**—the one who opens the channel while trusting a crew of learned specialists to share the load.

**Lie:** "No one is coming. If I stay useful and owe nobody, I can survive alone."

**Truth:** "Survival is shared. Needing people does not make me owned by them."

**Narrative Arc:**
- World 1: Removes her cutter governor, wakes and counter-tunes the Tuning Fork, loses Jed to his chosen sacrifice, and is forced beyond solitary survival.
- World 2: Learns she is a conduit, not a god. "Ground first."
- World 3: Leads the Lens heist.
- World 6: Rejects perfect control and learns to **close the circuit**, sharing the load to stop the Great Silence. Tuning burns out her Source connection but preserves her memories and identity; signal tinnitus and a hand tremor remain.

**Combat Kit (The Spark)**
**Note: Skills are governed by Cooldowns.**
Nova focuses on high-speed ranged attacks and mobility. She accelerates team tempo by reducing ally cooldowns.
*   **Core Skills (6 Total):**
    *   *Scrap Shot:* Basic laser fire. High speed.
    *   *Slide Kick:* Low damage, high stagger, breaks guard.
    *   *Overclock:* Self-buff (Speed up, Defense down).
    *   *Pulse Grenade:* AoE physical damage.
    *   *Chain Lightning:* Multi-target energy damage.
    *   *Hydraulic Kick:* Heavy melee strike. High guard break and stagger.
*   **Source Arts (6 Relic Skills):**
    *   *Blast Wave (Tuning Fork - Force):* A massive directional push. Shatters armor.
    *   *Phase Dash (The Bridge - Connection):* Instantly teleports to strike an enemy (ignoring cover).
    *   *Laser Focus (The Lens - Vision):* Next attack ignores 100% of enemy Armor/Guard.
    *   *Shard Storm (The Anvil - Creation):* Constructs 3 floating hard-light daggers that fire when she attacks.
    *   *Stasis Bolt (The Anchor - Stability):* Completely freezes one target in time for 1 turn.
    *   *Source Burst (The Source):* Clears all cooldowns for the entire party.

## Zeke

**Role:** The Insider
**Archetype:** Melee Brawler / Monk
**Weapon:** Combat Gauntlets
**Key Relics:** **The Lens** (Spire)

Zeke talks like corporate HR software—buzzwords, relentless optimism—because that’s how he coped inside the Dominion machine. Underneath, he’s got suppressed rage. He is the party's eyes, using his Dominion clearance and tech to find weaknesses.

**Narrative Arc:**
- World 1: Defects from Dominion.
- World 2: Owns his decision to splice the Chime into navigation and his failure to override the crash route; he does not blame Nova.
- World 3: Stops hiding behind the "HR voice" when he wields the Lens.
- World 6: Accepts he is valuable without being a tool.

**Combat Kit (The Analyst)**
Zeke fights up close, using data to exploit weak points and destabilize enemies.
*   **World 1 onboarding note:** Zeke joins at the very end of World 1, so his tactical "deny/setup" layer (Silence/Jam + Mark setup/payoff) should be tutorialized in World 2, not World 1.
*   **Core Skills (6 Total):**
    *   *Target Lock:* Marks an enemy (crit setup / lowers evasion).
    *   *Adrenaline Surge:* Self target Haste/Attack buff.
    *   *Flashbang:* AoE blind/stun.
    *   *Corrosive Strike:* Melee damage over time (Acid).
    *   *Data Siphon:* Steals enemy buffs on hit.
    *   *Logic Bomb:* Detonates "Target Lock" marks for massive damage.
*   **Source Arts (6 Relic Skills):**
    *   *Signal Jammer (Tuning Fork - Force):* AoE Silence. Prevents enemies from using Tech/Arts.
    *   *Network Patch (The Bridge - Connection):* Spreads a single ally's buffs to the entire party.
    *   *True Sight (The Lens - Vision):* Reveals all weaknesses + Party deals Guaranteed Crits for 1 turn.
    *   *Auto-Turret (The Anvil - Creation):* Constructs a drone that deals passive damage every turn.
    *   *Lag Spike (The Anchor - Stability):* Massively reduces Speed/Turn Order for all enemies.
    *   *System Reboot (The Source):* Revives a fallen ally with full HP and no cooldown penalty.

## Orion

**Role:** The Ancient
**Archetype:** Mage / Caster (Source Channeling)
**Weapon:** Focus Jewel
**Key Relics:** **The Bridge** (Wilds)

Orion is the last physical Aethel. He is introspective, earnest, and haunted by the "silence" of his people. He functions as the crew's conscience and medic. His Chime is an identity key and beacon, never a battery. It authenticates him to the Sanctuary, where the party recovers the separate Bridge Echo and installs it aboard the Astra.

**Narrative Arc:**
- World 2: Wakes from stasis, becomes the teacher.
- World 5: Recognizes the "Great Silence" protocol.
- World 6: Learns the Chorus is still there and offers a real ancestral continuity; answers "not yet" and remains with his living family.

**Combat Kit (The Guide)**
Orion channels the Source through his Jewel to damage enemies and heal the team.
*   **Core Skills (6 Total):**
    *   *Mend:* Single target heal.
    *   *Aftertone Pulse:* Light AoE damage + self-heal.
    *   *Purge:* Removes debuffs.
    *   *Barrier Field:* Reduces incoming damage for one ally.
    *   *Chorus Echo:* Heals repeat for 2 turns.
    *   *Silence Field:* Zone that dampens enemy Source attacks.
*   **Source Arts (6 Relic Skills):**
    *   *Shock Pulse (Tuning Fork - Force):* Knocks enemies away from low-HP allies (Disengage).
    *   *Soul Link (The Bridge - Connection):* Links party HP. Damage is split evenly; Healing is shared.
    *   *Triage Scan (The Lens - Vision):* Cleanses all Debuffs from party + Heals based on debuffs removed.
    *   *Sanctuary (The Anvil - Creation):* Constructs a zone on the floor; allies inside regenerate HP.
    *   *Undo (The Anchor - Stability):* Reverses all damage taken by one ally in the last turn.
    *   *Symphony (The Source):* Applies "Invulnerable" to the whole party for 1 turn.

## Gh0st

**Role:** The Hunter
**Archetype:** Tank / Knight (Melee)
**Weapon:** Heavy Blade
**Key Relics:** **The Anvil** (Foundry)

Gh0st is a defected corporate assassin (Phantom-class). He is cool, efficient, and humorless. Dominion built him from a **partial derivative of Elara's neural map**, but he is a distinct person, not a complete copy or biological sibling. Elara interacted with and trained him, forming the relationship that she later affirms by calling him **brother**. He starts as a weapon and learns to be a shield.

**Narrative Arc:**
- World 2: Nova's Fork mark trips the Elara-calibrated Source Lock, which only pauses his attack. Protecting the crew from the Source Beast is his first voluntary act.
- World 4: Confronts his creator and Dominion's "backup" label at the Foundry. Claims the Anvil and refuses an ordered execution.
- World 5-6: Elara names him her brother before she is pulled into the Tear; Gh0st becomes a protector on purpose.

**Combat Kit (The Wall)**
Gh0st holds the line with his blade and shields, forcing enemies to focus on him.
*   **Core Skills (6 Total):**
    *   *Taunt:* Forces enemies to attack Gh0st.
    *   *Suppression Strike:* Low damage, lowers enemy attack.
    *   *Harden:* Drastically increases defense for 1 turn.
    *   *Shock Blade:* Melee range stun/damage.
    *   *Intercept:* Takes damage meant for another ally.
    *   *Vengeance:* Deals damage based on missing HP.
*   **Source Arts (6 Relic Skills):**
    *   *Seismic Stomp (Tuning Fork - Force):* AoE Stun centred on self. Punishes swarming.
    *   *Tether (The Bridge - Connection):* Leashes an enemy to Gh0st. They cannot target others or move away.
    *   *Threat Assessment (The Lens - Vision):* Enters a stance that Auto-Counters the next physical hit with massive damage.
    *   *Barricade (The Anvil - Creation):* Constructs physical cover (Wall) with its own HP bar.
    *   *Gravity Well (The Anchor - Stability):* Pulls all enemies to one point and Roots them.
    *   *Iron Curtain (The Source):* Prevents all allies from falling below 1 HP for 1 turn.
