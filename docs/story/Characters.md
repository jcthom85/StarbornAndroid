## Nova

A 22-year-old human miner and mechanic accustomed to earning survival through work. She watches machinery and people closely, resists being owned, and takes risks when acceptable procedure leaves her trapped. Jed's care matters because it is specific and habitual.

She speaks practically under pressure. With trusted people she can be warmer, uncertain, impatient, or amused. Mechanical competence does not make her an expert on every alien system. Her Source access comes from the cutter/Fork incident and subsequent learning. Leadership requires trusting others and making room for their choices.

## Zeke

A Dominion insider who has helped an abusive system function. Procedure initially protects him and excuses him. Helping Nova costs that protection; joining the crew does not immediately erase fear, habits, or guilt.

His humor comes from precise familiarity with absurd rules. Under danger he can act decisively. Show growth through what he risks, admits, and accepts from others when he has nothing useful to offer. Avoid an HR joke or declaration of self-worth in every exchange.

## Orion

An Aethel survivor awakened after twenty years in stasis. He has knowledge, grief, incomplete information, and reasons to avoid the present. Teaching Nova draws him into living relationships.

He can use cultural or musical imagery more readily than the others, while practical instructions stay clear. He does not know every Source mystery. Learning the Chorus endures gives him a real choice about belonging; he remains with the living.

## Gh0st

Unit 734, a manufactured person trained to obey and minimize himself. Dominion used a partial derivative of Elara's neural map; his memories and identity are his own. Their relationship developed through time together.

Early brevity reflects training and self-protection. Trust brings questions, preferences, volunteered detail, disagreement, and freely chosen protection. A failsafe creates the opportunity for his first protective choice. Avoid reducing him to an Elara copy, a humorless wall, or a constant declaration of personhood.

## Jed

A mechanic who protects through maintenance, food, preparation, and attention. His conflict with Nova concerns how she spends her tools and body to survive. His sacrifice is his deliberate response to a physical trap. Establish the practical relationship that makes the loss matter.

## Elara, Thorne, and Vale

- Elara is connected to Dominion's living-interface research and to Gh0st through their relationship. Preserve her agency; her importance extends beyond motivating others.
- Mara Thorne wants monopoly and continued power. Express her justifications through institutional consequences and decisions. Vale deposes her in the Foundry and executes her on the Ring.
- Arden Vale treats individual suffering as a defect he is entitled to eliminate. Imposing a single will is the final threat. Build his argument through conduct before extended philosophy.
