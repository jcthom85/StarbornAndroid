# Character Arcs

**Status:** Canon Authority — Character Beliefs, Choices, Relationships, and Emotional Progression (Locked July 2026)
**Domain:** This document ranks below the Master Story and Lore Bibles and above the Writer's Handbook, world documents, and assets. It cannot alter plot causality or artifact rules.

This document outlines the emotional journeys and structural development of the main cast. Each character is built around a "Lie" (their starting false belief) and a "Truth" (the realization that completes their arc).

---

## 1. Nova — The Catalyst

*   **Age:** 22.
*   **The Lie:** "No one is coming. If I stay useful and owe nobody, I can survive alone."
*   **The Truth:** "Survival is shared. Needing people does not make me owned by them."

| World | State of Mind | Key Beat / Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1: The Mines** | Survival through usefulness. Pushes her machinery and herself past safe limits rather than admit need. | **Inciting Incident:** Her illegal cutter modification wakes the Fork. She chooses to counter-tune it as a mechanical problem and survives because Jed's repairs hold. Jed's death is his decision under structural pressure, never punishment for Nova being "not ready." |
| **2: The Wilds** | Overwhelmed. Tries to "brute force" the Source (panic-channelling). | **The Lesson:** Orion forces her to stop running. She learns to listen to the Source, not just scream at it. |
| **3: The Spire** | Competent. Starts to enjoy the power. Feels like a "cool" rebel. | **The Test:** Leads the heist. Confidence borders on arrogance. She thinks she can outsmart the Administrator effortlessly. |
| **4: The Foundry** | Responsibility. Realizes her power has consequences for others. | **The Pivot:** She creates room for Gh0st to confront his origin and make his own choice, even when a faster mission-first answer is available. She leads without authoring him. |
| **5: The Void** | Failure. Tries to fix everything alone with "Stasis." Arrogant. | **Low Point:** Vale defeats her easily because she tries to control him. She realizes brute force (even Source power) can’t fix a broken soul. |
| **6: The Source** | Acceptance. Rejects "Perfect Control." | **Climax:** She trusts the crew's living circuit, gives up root access, and burns out her Source connection to keep every mind distinct. Her memories remain hers. |

## 2. Zeke — The Insider

*   **The Lie:** "I’m a coward. I can only fix things from the inside by following the rules."
*   **The Truth:** "I am brave. Rules are just code, and I can rewrite them. I refuse to be silenced."

| World | State of Mind | Key Beat / Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1: The Mines** | Complicit. Watching Nova on screens. "Just doing my job." | **The Turn:** Deletes the footage of Nova. Physically leaves his safe booth to help her, burning his entire career/life in 5 minutes. |
| **2: The Wilds** | Guilty and useless. He blames himself for splicing the Chime into navigation and failing to override the crash route; he feels like dead weight in the jungle. | **The Growth:** Owns the mistake without turning it on Nova. His knowledge of systems and history helps them understand the ruins, but the crew's care is not conditional on that utility. |
| **3: The Spire** | Haunted. Back in civilization, he’s terrified of being recognized. | **The Confrontation:** Has to bluff a Dominion guard using his old "HR voice." He realizes he can use his past as a weapon, not just a shame. |
| **4: The Foundry** | Angry. Sees how the sausage is made (Gh0st’s creation). | **The Bond:** He connects with Gh0st—the "software guy" understanding the "hardware guy." He hacks the assembly line to save Gh0st. |
| **5: The Void** | Regret. Sees the full scale of what he helped build. | **Low Point:** He is locked out of the system by Vale. His "Insider" status means nothing. He feels he has nothing left to offer. |
| **6: The Source** | Resolved. He stops looking for a system to join. | **Climax:** With every interface failing, he manually keeps the living circuit's ground path open. The action, not a thematic speech, proves that he is present by choice. |

## 3. Gh0st — The Weapon

*   **The Lie:** "I am a thing. I have no name, only a function. I follow orders."
*   **The Truth:** "I am a person. I choose who I protect. I cannot be owned."

| World | State of Mind | Key Beat / Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1: The Mines** | Off-screen. | **Active Threat:** He is deployed by Thorne to hunt the Anomaly (Nova). |
| **2: The Wilds** | The Haunting. Confused when Nova's Echo mark trips the Elara-calibrated Source Lock. | **The First Choice:** The lock only pauses his attack. When the Source Beast strikes, he protects the party without a protection directive, then stays to understand why. |
| **3: The Spire** | The Watchdog. Protective of Nova, but claims it's "mission critical." | **The Crack:** He takes a hit meant for Nova without calculating it first. Logic says "dodge," body says "protect." |
| **4: The Foundry** | The Reckoning. Learns he was built from a partial derivative of Elara's neural map. Dominion's "backup" label makes him fear he is only a replacement. | **The Choice:** Rylos invokes command codes and demands an execution. Gh0st refuses to kill on command, proving difference through choice rather than through a technical loophole. |
| **5: The Void** | The Brother. Desperate to reach the person who once trained and knew him. | **Recognition and Loss:** Elara deliberately calls him "brother," affirming that shared origin does not erase distinct personhood. When she is pulled into the Tear, he loses a real relationship—not an imagined sister he never knew. |
| **6: The Source** | The Shield. No more orders. | **Climax:** He steps into the force aimed at the crew and contains it because he chooses who to protect. No ownership declaration is needed to explain the act. |

## 4. Orion — The Ancient

*   **The Lie:** "My time is over. The silence of my people is my punishment. I should just fade away."
*   **The Truth:** "Life is for the living. We are Starborn."

| World | State of Mind | Key Beat / Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1: The Mines** | Sleeping. | **Passive:** Stasis in the Wilds. Dreaming of the Chorus. |
| **2: The Wilds** | Grief. Wakes up to a dead world. Wants to go back to sleep. | **The Awakening:** Sees Nova struggling with the Source. He can't let her die. He steps up as a reluctant teacher. |
| **3: The Spire** | Judgment. Looks at the Dominion city with disgust. | **The Perspective:** He sees Architect tech—built through the exploitation of his people—repurposed by Dominion. He has to move past contempt for its human operators to help the crew use it without repeating either empire's logic. |
| **4: The Foundry** | Connection. Bonding with the "Artificial" (Gh0st). | **The Wisdom:** He teaches Gh0st that having a "designed purpose" doesn't mean you lack a soul. |
| **5: The Void** | Horror. Recognizes the "Great Silence" protocol. | **Low Point:** Realizes his people were exploited and silenced. The Chorus offers him a real ancestral continuity he could join now, not death or a deceptive lure. |
| **6: The Source** | Life. Accepts that home can exist in more than one direction. | **Climax:** He answers the Chorus "not yet," holds the counter-tone for the living circuit, and remains embodied with the people who need him now. |

## Implementation Strategy: World 1

To effectively establish these arcs, World 1 needs to emphasize the starting "Lies" for Nova and Zeke:

1.  **Nova (The Solitary Provider):** Show her refusing help, measuring safety by usefulness, and accepting dangerous work to avoid debt to another person. She does not keep her head down—her unsafe cutter modification is the flaw in action.
2.  **Zeke (The Complicit Employee):** Include dialogue where Zeke parrots corporate policy or enforces rules he clearly dislikes, highlighting his internal struggle and his reliance on the system for safety.
