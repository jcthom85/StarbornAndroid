## Hub Layout
Inside the mountain. Massive pistons, conveyor belts, and the heat of the core.

**Progression Model:** Combat/Lore → Relic → Boss.

## Stage 1
### Node 1: Conveyor Belt
*   **Icon:** ⚙️
*   **Type:** Start / Combat
*   **Description:** Conveyor belts carry half-assembled droids... and sometimes "biological components."
*   **Internal Content:** Assembly.
*   **Purpose:** Combat Entry.
*   **Unlocks:** Stage 2 (Conditioning AND The Forge).

## Stage 2 (Choice)
### Node 2A: Conditioning
*   **Icon:** 🧠
*   **Type:** Choice A / Lore
*   **Description:** Sterile white tiles stained with oil. Rows of empty chairs with restraints.
*   **Internal Content:** The Chair.
*   **Purpose:** Lore (Gh0st's Origin).
*   **Unlocks:** Stage 3 (Power Core).

### Node 2B: The Forge
*   **Icon:** 🔨
*   **Type:** Choice B / Relic
*   **Description:** A massive hammer smashing raw ore.
*   **Internal Content:** Anvil Mount.
*   **Purpose:** Obtain Relic (Anvil).
*   **Unlocks:** Stage 3 (Power Core).

## Stage 3
### Node 3: Power Core
*   **Icon:** 🔋
*   **Type:** Objective
*   **Description:** The pulsating heart of the factory.
*   **Internal Content:** Engine Mount.
*   **Purpose:** Steal Engine (for Ship).
*   **Unlocks:** Stage 4 (Titan Dock).

## Stage 4
### Node 4: Titan Dock
*   **Icon:** 🤖
*   **Type:** BOSS
*   **Description:** A circular platform suspended over the volcano's primary vent.
*   **Internal Content:** Catwalks.
*   **Purpose:** BOSS FIGHT: Rylos.
*   **Unlocks:** World 5 (The Void).
