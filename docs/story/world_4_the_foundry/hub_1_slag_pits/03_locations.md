## Hub Layout
Sector 7. The planet's open wound. Unbearably hot. Ash falls like snow.

**Progression Model:** Landing → Hazard/Lore Split → Entry.

## Stage 1
### Node 1: Obsidian Shelf
*   **Icon:** 🌋
*   **Type:** Start / Base
*   **Description:** A shelf of black glass overlooking a river of magma. *The Astra*’s heat shields are glowing cherry-red.
*   **Internal Content:** Landing Zone.
*   **Purpose:** Park Ship.
*   **Unlocks:** Stage 2 (Slag River AND Cooling Springs).

## Stage 2 (Choice)
### Node 2A: Slag River
*   **Icon:** 🔥
*   **Type:** Choice A / Hazard
*   **Description:** Broken stepping stones over flowing lava. Source flares erupt randomly.
*   **Internal Content:** Lava Flow.
*   **Purpose:** Hazard / Puzzle.
*   **Unlocks:** Stage 3 (Waste Intake).

### Node 2B: Cooling Springs
*   **Icon:** 🧖
*   **Type:** Choice B / Breather
*   **Description:** A rare maintenance pocket where condensed steam forms pools of hot, clear water.
*   **Internal Content:** Pool.
*   **Purpose:** Breather / Lore.
*   **Unlocks:** Stage 3 (Waste Intake).

## Stage 3
### Node 3: Waste Intake
*   **Icon:** 🏭
*   **Type:** Combat
*   **Description:** Massive pistons deafen the senses. Where the raw material enters the factory.
*   **Internal Content:** Compactor.
*   **Purpose:** Combat: Golems.
*   **Unlocks:** Stage 4 (Service Airlock).

## Stage 4
### Node 4: Service Airlock
*   **Icon:** 🚪
*   **Type:** Gate
*   **Description:** A heavy, pressurized door separating the volcanic exterior from the sterile factory interior.
*   **Internal Content:** Decon.
*   **Purpose:** Transition to Hub 4.2 (Assembly Line).
*   **Unlocks:** Hub 4.2 (Assembly Line).
