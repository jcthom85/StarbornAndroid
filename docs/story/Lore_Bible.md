# I. The Cosmology: The Skin and The Ocean

The universe of Starborn operates on two layers of existence.

### 1. The Skin (Physical Reality)
This is the world of matter, atoms, gravity, and corporations. It is rigid, cold, and follows the laws of physics. It is where the Dominion rules. Think of it as **ice**—frozen, solid, and separate.

### 2. the Source (The Ocean)
Beneath the physical layer flows **the Source**. It is a psionic current of pure information and consciousness.
*   **Nature:** It is not a "soul" in the religious sense, but a "carrier wave" of existence. It is an ocean of memory and presence.
*   **The Flow:** When things die, they **melt**. Their water returns to the ocean. When they are born, a scoop of water freezes into a shape (an ego/body).
*   **Visuals:** Represented as vibrating starlight, waveform distortions, and "singing" geometry where it bleeds through.
*   **Names:** Dominion calls it **the Source**. The Aethel call it **Veyra**. Orion calls it **the music**.

### Dominion Engineering Model: Hardened vs. Tunable Reality
Dominion doesn’t treat the Source like mysticism. They treat it like **acoustics**.
*   **Solid-State (Normal Physics):** Steel is steel. Bullets stop in armor. Walls are walls.
*   **Phase-State (Dominion term):** With the right **Source alignment**, matter becomes *tunable*—fields can harden air into barriers, “curtains” can become solid, and specialized impacts can shear through shields that would otherwise be impenetrable.

This is the foundation of Dominion Source-tech: force reality into a controlled phase-state, then profit from the control.

# II. The Ancients & The Relics

Thousands of years ago, a species known only as **The Architects** learned to modulate the Source. They didn't build with stone; they built with sound and frequency. They vanished, leaving behind tools—**Relics** (or Echoes)—that serve as mixing decks for reality.

### The Six Echoes (MacGuffins)
Dominion is digging for these. Each Relic controls a specific aspect of reality.

| Relic | Name | Function | Dominion's Goal | Nova's Power |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1 (Mines)** | **The Tuning Fork** | Opens and destabilizes boundaries between matter and the Source. | To break the barrier between Skin and Vein. | **Blast** (Force/Destruction) |
| **2 (Wilds)** | **The Bridge** | Connects distinct minds without merging them. | To network human minds together. | **Link** (Heal/Buff Party) |
| **3 (Spire)** | **The Lens** | Perceives Source structures and phase corridors. | To monitor the colony and control every route. | **Scan (True Sight)** (Reveal secrets/weak points) |
| **4 (Foundry)** | **The Anvil** | Shapes matter. | To manufacture infinite resources. | **Construct** (Create barriers/turrets) |
| **5 (Void)** | **The Anchor** | Stabilizes edits to reality. | To make their changes permanent. | **Stasis** (Time stop/CC) |
| **6 (Source)** | **The Key** | Grants root-level authority over the Source. | To seize root access and lock in Dominion’s monopoly. | **Ascension** (God Mode) |

### Architects Reveal Ladder (Player-Facing)
This is the intended drip-feed of truth about the Architects. Each reveal should change what the player believes they’re fighting.
*   **World 1 (Mines):** The Echo Relic “speaks” in non-Dominion language ("Sync established"). The chamber geometry is older than the colony. Dominion didn’t invent this—they found it.
*   **World 2 (Wilds):** Sanctuary murals show the Architects using the Bridge to connect minds. The Bridge sealed and masked the Sanctuary during the breach; the crew recovers it and installs it aboard the Astra. Orion recognizes the pattern: “Harmony” is not a Dominion invention—it is an ancient weapon.
*   **World 3 (Spire):** Archive fragments show that the internal sector curtains and the separate outer Planetary Shield are distinct installations derived from Architect **phase-curtain** technology. The Lens perceives timing windows through both. The player learns the cage has moving locks.
*   **World 4 (Foundry):** Dominion manufacturing and Project Phantom logs show they are copying Architect logic (Anvil fabrication, chordprint locks) while pretending it’s “progress.” The player learns the machine behind the machine.
*   **World 5 (Void):** The Ring mainframe reveals Project Harmony is a reboot of an Architect-era protocol (the first “Great Silence”). Thorne thinks it’s a product; Vale thinks it’s mercy.
*   **World 6 (Source):** The Key isn’t a treasure—it’s root access. The final question becomes: do you *own* reality, or do you protect it and let go?

### The Architects (Canon Truth)
The Architects were not gods. They were engineers who tried to solve suffering with control.
*   They built the six Echoes as admin instruments for the Source—tools that can network minds, reshape matter, and stabilize edits.
*   They used Aethel consciousness as living material while developing the Great Silence. The Aethel are exploited victims and survivors of the system, never the Echoes' creators.
*   Their “Great Silence” was the first forced harmony attempt. It did not create peace; it erased people.
*   The relic suite survived as scattered Echoes. Dominion is rebuilding the same ladder with a different logo.

### Chimes (Non-Echo Artifacts)
Chimes are portable identity keys and beacons keyed to a specific chordprint. They can carry resonance imprints, but they are not Echoes, batteries, engines, or general-purpose Source tools.
*   **Dominion designation:** Ghost Signal Cell.
*   **Aethel term:** Chime.

They can authenticate to locks and paired systems that know their identity, but they do not generate sustained power, provide thrust, or grant reality-editing.
*   **Orion's Chime:** Twenty years ago, when a Source Beast surfaced in Orion’s lab, he **jettisoned** his Chime as both a distress beacon and a decoy. The Beast turned toward the departing signature; Orion entered stasis to mute his remaining signal.
*   **How it reaches Nova:** Dominion later recovered it as salvage and it eventually filtered down to the Mining Colony as scrap, where Jed found it.
*   **Ore pod:** Dominion launch hardware recognizes Orion's old authorization signature, so the Chime spoofs the pod's launch lock. Once connected to navigation, its pairing signal pulls the pod toward the Sanctuary. It never enters the power core.
*   **Sanctuary:** The Chime authenticates Orion to the paired Sanctuary. Sanctuary reserve power releases him from stasis; the Chime does not power the release.
*   **The Astra:** The Bridge is a separate Echo recovered from the Sanctuary and installed aboard the Astra. The Chime neither becomes nor reboots the Bridge.
*   **Chorus Response:** A reactivated Chime is audible inside Veyra. The Chorus may strengthen the beacon's direction like wind on water, but cannot steer the ship, supply thrust, or override a living pilot.

### Signalprints (Dominion: Spectral ID)
Direct contact with the Source (especially via Relics) leaves a measurable Source fingerprint in the way a person "rings" when they act, panic, fight, or channel power.
*   **Dominion term:** Spectral ID / Spectral Profile.
*   **Orion's term:** Chordprint.
*   **How it shows up in tech:** Locks, sensors, and weapons can key off the chordprint instead of DNA.
*   **Project Phantom failsafe:** Gh0st-class units carry a **Source Lock** keyed to relay architecture calibrated from Elara’s chordprint. A threshold match interrupts lethal action long enough to prevent accidental friendly fire. It does not issue a protection order or recruit the unit.
*   **Why Nova can trip it:** The Tuning Fork's protocol signature is burned into Nova's nervous system. When that signature passes through the Elara-calibrated relay architecture, it creates a partial protocol match and pauses Gh0st's attack. Gh0st's later choice to protect the party is his own.

# III. The Imprinted: Anomalies

**The Unknown**
To the wider universe, "Relics" are rumors or folklore. Only the Dominion's top-level black program (Project Harmony) understands they are Architect-made artifacts. The public has no words for them.

**Why Nova?**
Nova is **not** a prophesied hero, genetically compatible, or selected by a conscious relic. She survives because a visible chain of machinery, preparation, judgment, and risk happens to form a viable interface.

### The Tuning Fork Handshake (Exact Mechanism)

1. Architect Echoes expect a stable call-and-response signal from a calibrated interface and grounding cradle. A bare nervous system normally overloads before it can complete the response.
2. Dominion mining cutters descend from crude Architect phase technology. Their safety governors block the frequency bands that destabilize machinery, nerves, and phase boundaries.
3. To make quota, Nova removes her cutter's governor. Jed has just repaired its **Cryo-Inductor** and patched the **Flux Liner** in her suit.
4. The cutter's first uncontrolled sweep enters old conduits, wakes the dormant Fork, opens its damaged chamber, and flags Nova's file.
5. In the chamber, Nova treats the Fork's destructive repetition as a feedback problem. She uses the cutter diagnostics to generate a counter-frequency and manually adjusts it as the Fork answers.
6. When the signals enter phase, the damaged interface interprets the cutter–suit–operator loop as a calibration rig and opens a neural handshake.
7. The Cryo-Inductor absorbs the thermal spike and fuses; the Flux Liner throws most of the discharge into chamber ground, burning open its sacrificial ground strip. The armor remains wearable, but that protection is spent until repaired. Nova survives neurological feedback and receives an **Echo mark**: an Architect protocol signature written into her nervous system.

### Why This Has Not Happened Before

*   The chamber was dormant, sealed, and physically inaccessible until Nova's opening surge reached its buried conduit.
*   Standard cutters cannot enter the required band, and local workers read the response as breaker trouble and magnetic interference.
*   Harmony researchers use remote probes designed to suppress feedback. Their own safety systems prevent the uncontrolled call-and-response that Nova permits.
*   Nova is not biologically unique. Another skilled operator with an ungoverned phase tool, functioning thermal protection and grounding, and the same willingness to answer could theoretically survive.

### Later Echoes and the Living Circuit

*   Later Echoes recognize the Fork's **protocol signature**, not Nova's blood. The mark permits a handshake; it never guarantees safety, mastery, or obedience.
*   Each Echo demands its own learned interaction. Orion teaches Nova technique, while the entire crew engineers a living circuit through practice and trust.
*   Nova opens the channel, Zeke manages the ground path, Gh0st contains physical spillover, and Orion modulates the counter-tone because those are skills they developed—not cosmic roles assigned at birth.
*   If the circuit breaks, everyone can be injured. Its power comes from cooperation that remains voluntary and reversible.
*   Echo marks let Nova reach into the Source and allow pressure, dreams, and resonance imprints to travel back. They do not alter her ancestry or replace her identity.

### Source Cost (Canon)

*   Ordinary channeling causes pain, sensory crossover, lost seconds, exhaustion, and accumulating neurological instability.
*   Personal memories are endangered only by catastrophic, uncontrolled failure. They are never a routine fuel, a spell price, or a menu of sacrifices.
*   In the finale, Nova deliberately burns out her Source connection. Her memories and identity remain intact; persistent signal tinnitus and an intermittent hand tremor are the lasting physical cost.

# IV. The Antagonist: Dominion's "Project Harmony"

**Director Mara Thorne is greed in a clean suit.**
She sells Harmony as mercy because it plays well in boardrooms and recruitment ads. But underneath, Thorne’s drive is acquisition: to **own** the Source, to **monopolize** Source-harnessing tech, and to turn an entire planet into a closed, profitable system she controls forever.

Thorne isn’t trying to “save” people. She’s trying to **corner a market** nobody else understands yet.
*   **The Commodity:** the Source is the first new resource since the invention of off-world travel: energy, computation, authentication, surveillance, and—at the top—reality edits.
*   **The Business Model:** Keep the planet sealed behind the outer Shield, isolate sectors with internal phase curtains, extract Echoes, reverse-engineer patterns into scalable products, then sell “Harmony” as a subscription: fewer strikes, fewer riots, fewer leaks.
*   **The Personal Goal:** Money, legacy, and control. A Director who never has to answer to a board again.
*   **Her Moral Line:** She has one. It’s just drawn around *herself* and the company’s survival, not around workers. If a hundred “assets” die to protect the monopoly, she calls it a write-off.
*   **Why she’s not cartoon evil:** She doesn’t enjoy cruelty. She enjoys *winning*. If the numbers go up and the sector stays quiet, she sleeps fine.
*   **The Constraint:** Dominion controls facilities built around several Echoes but cannot safely activate or combine them. Remote probes suppress the feedback required for a valid handshake.
*   **The Nova Strategy:** Once Nova survives the Fork, Thorne changes her status from disposable liability to capture-only mobile interface. She tracks the crew and allows Nova to expose otherwise inaccessible Echoes, intending to take both operator and suite at the end.

**Lieutenant Arden Vale is a radical idealist.**
Vale does not want profit—he wants *purity*. His single governing belief is that separate selves inevitably create suffering, so one coordinating will must eliminate variance. He seeks to claim the Chorus and force every consciousness in the Source into that directed will. He appoints himself because he mistakes certainty for moral qualification; in his mind, becoming the Soloist is responsibility rather than conquest.

### Phase-Curtain Travel Systems
The **internal sector curtains** and the outer **Dominion Planetary Shield** are separate installations derived from the same Architect phase technology.
*   **Internal curtains:** Facility, ground-route, and low-atmosphere barriers that isolate sectors. The Astra can fly at low altitude, but cannot cross these curtains reliably until the crew has the Lens.
*   **Outer Planetary Shield:** A hard deck anchored and managed from the Orbital Ring. It seals the labor world, controls off-world traffic, and prevents leaks of Echo research.
*   **Timed corridors:** Authorized craft pass through moving micro-windows keyed to clearance transponders. Outside a corridor, either system can shred or deflect a craft.
*   **Lens:** Reveals corridor timing through internal curtains and the outer Shield. World 3 ends with an internal crossing to the Foundry, not an orbital escape.
*   **Deep-Core Engine:** Supplies the velocity required to reach orbit.
*   **Phase-Cutter Arrays:** Hold a Lens-revealed corridor open long enough for the Astra to ascend. They do not replace the Engine or the Lens.

### Thorne’s Pitch: Project Harmony (A Product)
Thorne wants to use the Six Echoes to build the first scalable Source-exploit: a system that turns minds into managed infrastructure—and turns managed minds into profit.
1.  Crack the boundary between Skin and Source (The Tuning Fork) to access the “resource.”
2.  Network minds (The Bridge) so dissent becomes a solvable engineering problem.
3.  Monitor and contain the planet (The Lens + the Phase Curtain corridors).
4.  Manufacture at will (The Anvil) and stabilize edits (The Anchor).
5.  Take root access (The Key) so Dominion owns the Source outright.

### Dominion Source-Tech (Corporate Reality)
Dominion already builds technology that harnesses the Source in many ways—imperfect, dangerous, and scalable enough to be profitable.
This is their “magitek” ladder: infrastructure → industry → personal weapons.

#### Pillar 1: Logistics (Relay Network)
*   **What it is:** Orbital relays and Source repeaters that keep Dominion commerce and compliance connected.
*   **What it does:** Turns distance into a managed variable—shipping windows, communications priority, and “accidental” shortages that can be blamed on weather.
*   **Story function:** Dominion doesn’t just own labor. It owns the **frequency** that food, medicine, and evacuation rides on.

#### Pillar 2: Defense & Industry (Phase-Tech)
*   **Phase Curtains:** Planetary and facility-scale barriers with timed corridors (the Shield as logistics + prison).
*   **Source Barriers:** Corridor shutters, kill-walls, and containment fields keyed to **chordprints** (Spectral ID).
*   **Phase Tools:** “Drills” and cutters that hum rock into dust instead of spinning—fast, quiet, and brutal.

#### Pillar 3: Warfare (Virtuoso Program)
Machines can generate huge standing fields, but they’re clumsy at *improvisation*. Combat requires microsecond tuning—defense to offense to containment without overheating.
*   **The solution:** A living brain as the processor: a human conduit synced with relay augmentation.
*   **Project Virtuoso (Elara):** Proof that a tuned human + a focus can do what Dominion hardware alone cannot.

#### Project Phantom (Gh0st and Elara)

*   Dominion built Unit 734, later **Gh0st**, from a **partial derivative of Elara's neural map**. He is neither a complete copy nor a biological sibling.
*   Elara interacted with and trained 734, and they formed a sibling relationship before he understood the process that made him. Dominion's label **backup** is its dehumanizing interpretation, not the narrative truth.
*   The derivative gave his relay architecture compatibility with systems calibrated from Elara's chordprint; it did not give him her memories, identity, or destiny.
*   In the Foundry, learning the method makes Gh0st fear that he is a replacement. On the Ring, Elara deliberately calls him **brother**, affirming that a shared origin does not erase distinct personhood.

#### Source Foci (Elara-Era Prototypes)
Dominion built “foci” that are inert in normal hands and catastrophic in a conduit’s.
*   **Prism-Saber (Melee):** A hilt that projects a blade of hardened phase-noise—destabilizes bonds instead of cutting by edge.
*   **Caster Gauntlets (Ranged):** Amplify Source signal into shaped Source discharge (concussive / heat / EMP variants).
*   **Vector Harness (Utility):** An inertial rig that locks position for short bursts—wall-walk, micro-blinks, and recoil-cancel tricks.

These prototypes can become late-game “legendary gear,” but **not as destiny locks**: Echo-marked users can ignite them, Orion can stabilize them, Zeke can calibrate them, and Gh0st can operate dampened modes.

The Echoes are the difference between *tools* and *root*. Thorne wants root for monopoly. Vale wants root for ideology.

### Vale’s Variant: The Great Silence (A Crusade)
Vale calls it mercy: remove dissonance by removing individuals. A choir can still fracture; the Soloist cannot.

**The Twist:**
Thorne thinks Harmony is controllable because it’s *her* product. Vale thinks Thorne is a coward playing god for profit. Vale makes his move early: a compliance coup in the Foundry (World 4), then full ascension on the Ring (World 5).

Stopping Vale collapses the forced network and reopens the planetary phase curtain. It does **not** dissolve the wider Dominion. Thorne's death and Harmony's destruction create a regional power vacuum; broadcasting the evidence gives this labor world and neighboring sectors a chance to organize their own freedom.

# V. Alien Species

### The Aethel (Orion's People)
*   **Origins:** Native to Veyra. They didn't live on planets; they evolved in the Ocean. They aren't "people" in the human sense; they are **currents** that hold their shape.
*   **The Fall:** The Architects tried to bottle them as batteries. To survive, the Aethel fled into physical bodies (creating the first “aliens”) to hide their signal. Being physical was painful—like being frozen.
*   **Ascension (the Chorus):** Over time, many Aethel learned to slip back into the Source without dissolving. They learned to hold their "shape" without a body.
*   **Limitations:** The Chorus can’t simply walk into the Skin. Dominion damping, phase curtains, and the nature of Veyra make direct manifestation unstable. They can only **nudge** through anchored interfaces: Chimes, Echo Relics, and Echo-marked nervous systems.
*   **How they help (without destiny):** They don’t “choose” heroes. They answer what’s *loud enough to hear*—a reactivated Chime, a Relic handshake, a person risking themselves for someone else. Their influence is wind-on-water: direction, not control.
*   **Orion:** He is one of the last “Physical Aethel.” After stasis, he thinks the music went silent and that his people left him. Near the end, he learns the truth: they aren't dead. They're just underwater. They’ve been screaming his name for 20 years, but he was too "solid" to hear it.

# VI. Glossary & Terminology

### Starborn
Derogatory slang used by the Dominion for things that "fell from the sky" (Aliens/Unauthorized breaches).
*   **Usage:** First a slur for Orion and unauthorized Aethel signals; later applied to Nova after her impossible launch and crash across controlled boundaries.
*   **Reclamation:** Only after the crew chooses one another do they reclaim it to mean a "Constellation" (a stable group of stars). They are not one hero; they are a system.

### Aethel Ascension
The process by which an Aethel consciousness sheds its physical form ("melting the ice") and returns to the Source ("the Ocean") as a coherent, self-sustaining pattern. Unlike death, where the pattern dissolves, Ascension allows the individual to join the Chorus while retaining their essence.

### Relics as Anchors
Relics are not merely tools for manipulating reality; they act as heavy "sinkers" or anchors that bridge the gap between the Source (Ocean) and the Physical World (Skin). Without an anchor, Source energy dissipates or burns uncontrollably in the physical realm. The Relics stabilize the flow, allowing specific aspects of reality to be tuned safely.
