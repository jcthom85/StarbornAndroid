## Creative direction

A grounded, colorful science-fiction adventure about work, ownership, escape, and a crew learning to trust each other. Humor grows from people and circumstances. Unfamiliar technology and the Source bring wonder and danger.

Sound can accompany Source contact, and Orion can draw on musical traditions. It is an occasional motif. Establish human stakes and observable consequences before metaphysics. Theme should emerge through decisions and relationships; planning summaries below are not dialogue.

## Causal constraints

- Nova is a 22-year-old human miner and mechanic, with no Aethel ancestry, prophecy, genetic compatibility, or prior Chime exposure.
- Her ungoverned cutter wakes the Fork through a buried conduit. Her cutter-suit-operator calibration lets her survive contact. The current opening's authored settings are 87 kHz phase sweep, 68% cold loop, and 180 degrees ground phase; preserve their puzzle contract until deliberately redesigned.
- The Fork writes an Architect protocol signature into her nervous system. The Cryo-Inductor fuses; only the Flux Liner's sacrificial ground strip is spent. Its armor remains equipped.
- The Chime is Orion's identity key, paired beacon, and imprint carrier. It supplies no sustained power, thrust, attack, Bridge startup current, or Shield bypass.
- The Bridge is separate Architect Echo #2. It connects distinct willing minds without merging them.
- Architects built the Echoes and exploited Aethel consciousness. Aethel survived, adapted, preserved, and operated some systems.
- Gh0st is a distinct person built around a partial derivative of Elara's neural map. He has his own identity and no inherited memories. An Elara-calibrated Source Lock pauses one lethal action and supplies no protective command.
- Source use causes bodily and neurological strain. Memory is never routine fuel.
- World 2 travel is low-altitude to the Spire; World 3 crosses an internal curtain to the Foundry. Lens, engine, and arrays enable the outer-Shield breach in Worlds 4-5.
- The ending burns out Nova's Source access, preserving identity and memory. Vale and Harmony fall locally; a regional opening forms and the wider Dominion survives.

## Premise and antagonists

Nova works under quota penalties and debt in a Dominion mining colony. A dangerous tool modification wakes an ancient artifact and makes her a target. Escape draws her into a struggle over technology that can alter matter and consciousness.

Director Mara Thorne wants to monopolize the Source and turn control into a permanent business. Lieutenant Arden Vale takes over her project because he believes eliminating individual difference will eliminate suffering. He intends to impose his will on everyone. The crew must stop that attempt while preserving the people it claims to save.

## Prologue

At the Sector 9 facility, a containment breach threatens Orion. He launches his Chime as a paired identity beacon and enters stasis. The artifact eventually passes through Dominion salvage to Jed. The prologue establishes danger and a concrete object whose function becomes clearer later; it does not need to explain the whole cosmology.

## World 1: Mines

Nova has too little sleep, food, and clearance to absorb another quota penalty. Jed repairs the cutter's Cryo-Inductor and Flux Liner; Nova deliberately confirms the governor bypass. Her live test sends a prohibited sweep through a buried Architect conduit, blows a sector breaker, wakes the Fork, and damages its chamber seal. Logs retain her operator and cutter identity.

Zeke alters Nova's classification enough to prevent immediate recycling without erasing the underlying fault. Boggs sends her to Sector 4 as expendable labor to clear the interference. The Fork repeats her cutter pulse. Nova reads the feedback and uses the repaired tool, suit cooling, and physical ground to stabilize contact. The incident marks her nervous system and alerts Dominion sensors.

During the escape Jed holds the failing mechanism so Nova can reach the pods. He gives her the salvaged Chime because old launch systems recognize its authorization. Zeke leaves his booth and helps her escape. Jed's loss follows from a physical problem and his own choice; no convenient relic intervention resolves it.

## Crash and World 2: Sector 9

Zeke splices the Chime into the pod's authorization/navigation bus. Its beacon forces a Sanctuary route; the pod supplies its own power, misses the safe Shield corridor, and crashes.

The Chime authenticates Orion. Sanctuary reserve cells release him after twenty years in stasis. The party recovers the separate Bridge from its cradle and finds the wrecked Astra.

At Canopy Ridge Nova's Fork mark trips an Elara-calibrated Source Lock, pausing Gh0st's lethal action. He confirms no command accompanies the pause. When the Beast attacks he chooses to protect the party. Only after that victory does Orion lead a grounded Link drill: Zeke clips a lead to Nova's bracer, Gh0st offers his hand, and Orion regulates the connection. Distinct participants remain willing and separate.

The crew powers the Bridge/Astra installation with salvaged conduits and ship current. The Chime authenticates Orion. Repair permits a low-altitude service route to the Spire. Nova learns that power requires practice and help; quiet moments should let the recruits become people to each other.

## World 3: Spire

The Astra can fly, but Dominion barriers restrict its route. Reconnaissance, disguises, credentials, favors, and service access build the Lens heist. Zeke's history complicates his usefulness and fear; Nova must coordinate rather than solve every problem herself.

The Lens responds to the Fork's protocol mark and reveals phase structures, including a seam through the internal curtain toward the Foundry. Contact brings risk and exposure rather than instant expertise.

Thorne anticipates the theft. Vale intrudes on her authority and escalates the lockdown. Thorne vents the room; the crew uses the Lens to find a dangerous exit and escapes. The power struggle remains unresolved.

## World 4: Foundry

The Astra needs a Deep-Core engine and ascent equipment to reach the Ring. This is also Gh0st's production site. Evidence and Rylos's commands confront him with what Dominion intended him to be; Nova must leave his response to him.

The Anvil detects Nova's protocol mark. Its damaged installation amplifies unstable input and her first Construct fails. Engine theft physically destabilizes the Foundry and disrupts production. Consequences for workers and manufactured units belong in the route, not only in a log.

Land Gh0st's choice and the crew's escape before Vale's compliance coup relieves Thorne and puts the wider network under his control.

## World 5: Orbital Ring

The Ring is polished, controlled, and hunting the crew. They reach Elara and take the Anchor while discovering the scale of Harmony. Thorne, already deposed, reveals the commercial purpose of her project. Vale's plan exceeds it.

Nova cannot solve the confrontation simply by applying a newly acquired power. Vale executes Thorne, ascends, and opens the Tear; Elara is pulled into it. The crew follows. Specific optional-area access and breather placement must match production gates; the old blanket Solarium lockout claim is retired pending route reconciliation.

## World 6: Source and ending

Distorted places from earlier worlds make the crew's histories physically present. Rescue and reunion should show how their choices have changed. Preserve the Chorus revelation and Orion's decision to remain with the living, without requiring every companion to explain that choice musically.

Vale attempts to overwrite their minds. The crew's practiced cooperation preserves their separation and enables resistance. The resolution depends on their actions and Nova relinquishing control, not a sequence of declarations that magically stabilizes reality.

The current final interaction is named Tune World. Its proposed replacement remains in the [puzzle refinement plan](../design/SOURCE_LANGUAGE_AND_PUZZLE_REFINEMENT_PLAN.md). Keep existing completion gates until that redesign is implemented. Nova loses Source access and retains her identity and memories, with tinnitus and an intermittent tremor. Life continues through a newly opened regional corridor while Dominion survives beyond it.
