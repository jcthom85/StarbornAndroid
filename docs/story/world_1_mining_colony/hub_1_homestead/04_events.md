# Events — World 1: The Mines

## EVT_W1_00: Volunteer Compliance Committee
*   **Trigger:** Early Hub exploration (before Mine access).
*   **Visuals:**
    *   A nervous administrator (Zeke) flags Nova down near the bulletin board. He’s holding a datapad like a shield.
    *   **Zeke:** "Citizen! You have a high mobility score. Have you considered joining the Volunteer Compliance Committee?"
    *   **Zeke:** "We get extra rations. And a sash. It’s a really nice sash."
    *   **Nova:** "I'm busy surviving, suit. Go find someone who likes rules."
    *   **Zeke:** "Right. Survival. Efficient. I’ll just... put you down as 'maybe'."
    *   He watches her walk away—impressed, not offended.
    *   **Nova (Internal):** Great. Now I’m on a list.

## EVT_W1_01: The Discovery
*   **Trigger:** Nova enters `echo_heart`, approaches the broadcasting **Tuning Fork**, and starts the cutter-frequency interaction.
*   **Visuals:**
    *   The chamber seal was forced apart by the opening surge. Its angles are too clean and intentional for Dominion construction; a buried conduit pulses between the mine and the Fork.
    *   The Fork's unstable call shakes dust from the supports. Nova recognizes the behavior as harmonic stress, the same principle her cutter uses on rock.
    *   **Nova:** "Not magnetic. Harmonic. And it's answering my cutter."
    *   Nova opens the cutter diagnostic instead of touching the Relic.
*   **Player Interaction — Counter-Tune:**
    *   Sweep through the widened frequency band created by the MQ01 governor bypass.
    *   Each closer adjustment makes the Fork answer and steadies the room; a poor match produces painful feedback but allows an immediate retry.
    *   The final match creates a stable call-and-response loop.
*   **Handshake Cinematic:**
    *   **Overlay:** **CALIBRATION LOOP: ACCEPTED.** Then: **SYNC: ESTABLISHED.**
    *   Ambient sound drops out. The Fork's hum becomes pressure in Nova's teeth and sternum.
    *   The damaged Fork treats the cutter-suit-operator circuit as an old calibration rig and opens a direct neural handshake.
    *   The repaired **Cryo-Inductor** screams as it dumps heat. The **Flux Liner** flashes from wrist to boot, shunting the discharge into the chamber floor.
    *   The Cryo-Inductor fuses, and only the Flux Liner's sacrificial ground strip burns open. The liner remains equipped; the connection snaps before it kills her.
    *   Nova wakes holding the **Echo Relic**, shivering and nosebleeding, with signal tinnitus, a hand tremor, and several unperceived seconds. Her memories and identity remain intact.
    *   **Overlay:** **UNKNOWN PROTOCOL SIGNATURE: WRITTEN.** The **Echo mark** now lives in her nervous system.
    *   Somewhere far above, a Project Harmony sensor recognizes a live Architect handshake and triggers the intact-capture alert.
*   **Gameplay Result:** Fuses the Cryo-Inductor and burns open the Flux Liner's sacrificial ground strip while leaving the liner equipped, persists Nova's **Echo mark**, unlocks **"Blast Wave"** (Nova Source Art), and triggers `EVT_W1_02`.

## EVT_W1_02: The Lockdown
*   **Trigger:** Immediately after `EVT_W1_01` writes the Echo mark, before Nova leaves the Echo Chamber.
*   **Visuals:**
    *   All lights in the Hub turn RED.
    *   Sirens blare.
    *   Giant holographic screens descend.
    *   **The Warden (On Screen):** "Security Breach in Sector 4. Biological contaminant detected. Initiate containment protocol. Purge all organic assets."
    *   **System Override:** The red alert flickers to a cool, sterile blue for a split second.
    *   **PA (Orbital Compliance):** "Correction, Warden. The asset is now project material. Contain it intact."
    *   The screen glitches back to red. The Warden pauses, visibly irritated, then resumes.
    *   **The Warden:** "...Seize the assets. Alive."
*   **Gameplay Result:** Dominion changes Nova's order from disposal to capture. Hub becomes a hostile zone. Guards spawn in Trade Row.

## EVT_W1_03: The Sacrifice
*   **Trigger:** Reaching the Cargo Lift at the end of Maintenance Tunnels.
*   **Visuals:**
    *   Nova and Jed reach the lift.
    *   Warden's Enforcers (Elites) breach the door behind them.
    *   The Lift controls are jammed. Jed runs *back* to the manual override panel on the wall.
    *   **Jed:** "Get out of here, Nova! Find the sky!"
    *   Jed presses a humming Chime into Nova's hand. "Found it in scrap. Dominion called it a Ghost Signal Cell. Doesn't hold a charge, but old launch boards recognize its handshake. Find a pod and it'll clear you."
    *   Jed smashes the panel. The lift door slams shut between them.
    *   Through the window, Nova sees Jed charge the Enforcers with his wrench as the lift rockets upward.
*   **Gameplay Result:** Nova receives the Chime identity key/beacon. Launch Bay access opens. The escape continues.
