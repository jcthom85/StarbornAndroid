# World 1: Mining Colony — Hub 1: Homestead Quarter

## Overview
This is the starting hub for Nova. It serves as the onboarding zone where the player learns movement, interaction, tinkering, and cutter-frequency controls. At Jed's Workshop, Nova repairs her Cryo-Inductor and Flux Liner, bypasses the cutter's safety governor, and causes the surge that wakes the buried Tuning Fork. The atmosphere is "working class sci-fi" — industrial, lived-in, but relatively safe compared to the mines.

## Key Themes
- **Normalcy before the storm:** Showing Nova's daily life before the cosmic plot kicks in.
- **Corporate Presence:** Dominion is here, but currently just "the boss," not "the villain."
- **Community:** The people here know each other.
- **Consequences come from work:** The cosmic plot begins through Nova's visible mechanical choice, not ancestry or a relic selecting her.

## Transition Points
- **To Trade Row:** Open access.
- **To Stellarium Mine:** Initially gated/guarded until tutorial steps are complete.
