# Campaign availability and economy audit

The latest [World 5 pass](PUBLIC_CAMPAIGN_WORLD_5.md) removes
the room-position shortcut, reduces hidden action calls to 5 and synthetic
victories to 7, and completes Worlds 1-5 without test assistance. Counts and economic
observations below describe the earlier audit snapshot.

The follow-up [snack and public-route pass](SNACK_ABILITIES_PUBLIC_ROUTES.md)
executes a procurement, crafting, cooking and Astra-selling slice through public
controller actions and adds two snack ability trials.

The audit follows the existing six-world campaign runner and observes the
production state after each settled action. It records 233 distinct checkpoints,
10,485 observations of 45 recipes, and 17 fishing/market scenarios with 300 casts
each. These reports are regenerated by `LegalRouteCampaignRunnerTest` under
`app/build/reports/campaign-systems/`.

## Fixed content gap

Fiery Pepper had no authored source, although Pulse Citrus requires two peppers
and Spicy Ramen requires one. Added it to Sentinel Scraps in World 2 and Noodle
Row in World 3, using the existing item price and each merchant's markup.
Recipe costs, output effects and fishing rewards are unchanged.

Regression coverage now checks that every cooking ingredient has an external
source or a recipe chain rooted in external sources. A production shop purchase
test buys peppers, preserves the Pulse Citrus discovery flag after leaving the
ridge station, cooks the snack and verifies ingredient consumption.

## Recipe timing

The side-trip model can reach the Trail Mix and Pulse Citrus discovery stations
in World 2, Comet Gummies in World 3, Foundry Smoked Brisket in World 4, Orbital
Glimmer Tonic in World 5, and Source Astral Feast in World 6. This establishes
available station candidates at observed campaign states, not automatic
discovery: the player must visit and open the cooking station.

The minimal runner does not fish, purchase optional ingredients or open these
stations, so its actual inventory never becomes ready to cook those snacks.
That is an intentionally limited route policy, not evidence that crafting is
impossible. Reports distinguish held ingredients, current source candidates,
and hypothetical procurement carried forward from earlier regions.

## Upgrade bootstrap

The Resin Rod base is sold by David's Provisions for 184 credits in the sampled
World 2 state. A dedicated integration test buys that base, then uses only the
starter Field Rod and plain lure to catch wiring and a circuit board in Spire
runoff. It successfully crafts the Reinforced Resin Rod, consumes the purchased
base, and preserves both starter items. The upgrade does not require itself or
an advanced lure to obtain its components.

Potential-source closure also identifies routes to the four authored fishing
gear recipes without letting a recipe supply its own missing component or tool.
Those plans can include purchased bases, optional loot, chance drops and clean
collection rewards. They are source plans, not guaranteed quantities or a fully
executed acquisition route. Generated arcade quest items are outside this
source model and may remain unresolved in unrelated repair recipes.

## Fishing income along unlocked routes

Movement probes use the production direction evaluator in read-only CHECK mode,
including named special exits. Hub entry uses the production node evaluator.
When a reachable exit permits returning to the hub after
`ms_w2_mq05_complete`, the model includes the Astra's production list of unlocked
destinations and eligible nodes in their arrival hubs. This matters: catches
from a later region can be sold to an earlier merchant.

Shop stock, rotation, milestone gates, prices, allowed sale types and blacklists
come from production ShopController output. Fishing uses the starter gear
actually held by the minimal runner, real weighted encounters, fight timing and
secured clean-catch rewards. The model never grants simulated catches to the
campaign inventory.

| Sample | Gross credits per fishing minute | Basic medkit fishing time |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| World 2 stream, regional shops | 64.6 | 53 seconds |
| Spire runoff, unlocked return trips | 105.7 | 32 seconds |
| Foundry runoff, unlocked return trips | 163.9 | 21 seconds |
| Orbital false tide, unlocked return trips | 183.7 | 19 seconds |
| Source ether well, unlocked return trips | 438.5 | 8 seconds |

The basic medkit costs 57 credits and restores 75 HP. Upgraded Medkit+ stock and
its fishing-time equivalent are also recorded in `income.csv`; in the Source
sample, 156 credits for 150 HP takes about 21 seconds of fishing. These rates
exclude travel, reaction, menus and selling, and assume attentive clean catches.
They are gross earning potential rather than measured end-to-end farming time.
The Source scenario deserves balance review: return travel means the earlier
global-best-shop income estimate cannot be dismissed solely because the Source
has no local merchant. No fishing price or mastery change was made here.

## Evidence boundaries and validation

The runner completed all six worlds and passed its existing save/reload
checkpoints. The new audit passed alongside source availability, production
purchase/cooking, starter-gear upgrade, catalog, progression, provision and
shop-funded crafting-arbitrage coverage: 25 Android tests. Six affected desktop
crafting/fishing flow tests also passed.

The rebuilt Windows portable package passed its smoke check from the temporary
directory, including title-music decoding. All 1,328 packaged assets matched
their source bytes, including the updated shop stock.

The runner records all assistance: one room-position shortcut, 49 reflective
action calls, and 23 scripted victories. `assistance.txt` names each one. This
is not a strict proof of every player's legal route, combat affordability or UI
access. Optional side trips are read-only topology/gate probes; they do not
execute all entry events, consume route keys, play battles, collect every item,
or solve optional puzzles. Potential sources may need several trips and compete
for limited materials or credits. The runner starts with its own zero-credit
fixture, so its wallet is not a claim about the game's configured starting cash.

The next stronger automated check would execute specific procurement plans and
return trips through public actions, reducing the recorded assistance. Physical
input feel and audible playback still need hands-on testing.
