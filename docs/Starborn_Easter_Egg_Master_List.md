**Personal Easter Egg Master List**

*People, pets, places, memories, and implementation ideas*

**Purpose.** A living reference for personal details Jason wants woven
into Starborn. The goal is not to force every reference into the game or
make them all the same kind of Easter egg. NPCs, dialogue, side quests,
creatures, props, item descriptions, environmental details, jokes,
music, locations, and hidden areas are all fair game. The best
references should feel native to Starborn first and personally
recognizable second.

# 1. Immediate Family

## Jason

**Who he is:** Builder and creator. Loves computers, music, games, and
making creative projects. Dedicated dad and husband; treats his pets as
family. Lifelong music lover and guitarist. Grew up loving JRPGs. Cares
about nutrition.

**Character notes:** Almost always making something: software, games,
music, websites, designs, or experiments. Nostalgic and sentimental
about people, pets, places, music, old games, and earlier periods of
life. Likes figuring out how things work and bringing other people into
the things he loves. Has a goofy, irreverent streak. Relationships are
one of the strongest recurring themes in his creative work.

**Strong implementation idea:** HIDDEN JASON WORKSHOP. A completely
optional hidden room containing a Jason-like creator who cannot stop
making things. The room is littered with half-finished inventions,
computer-like equipment, music gear, an old game-like device, tools,
mementos, and the next project already underway. He is competent and
building genuinely cool things; the joke is that he cannot stop
creating. Possible exchange: Nova asks what he is making and he
effectively responds, \'Which thing?\' A subtle meta-layer could imply
he is building a tiny simulated world without explicitly saying he is
making Starborn.

## Megan

**Who she is:** Jason\'s wife and the heart of the household. Dental
hygienist. Very supportive wife and mom. Helps Jason with side projects
such as Great Frontier and Starborn, even while giving him grief about
them.

**Interests and traits:** Likes investigative crime drama, live court
cases, the beach and pool, and now especially spring and fall after
being more of a summer person when younger. Very pretty. A good daughter
to her mother and helps when she can.

**Animal connection:** Penny is attached to her hip, as Kodi and Bella
were. The family dogs strongly gravitate toward her.

**Implementation possibilities:** A Megan-inspired character could have
an animal or creature that refuses to leave her side. Dental/teeth,
investigation/courtroom themes, beach/coastal themes, or her role as the
emotional center of a home could all provide subtler hooks.

## Tyson

**Who he is:** Jason and Megan\'s oldest son. A builder, creator, and
dreamer. A good big brother even though Ian can drive him nuts.

**Interests and traits:** Loves retro video games, horror such as Killer
Klowns from Outer Space, Ninja Turtles, art, engineering, and building
things. Used to make plushies from paper, cotton balls, and tape. Often
seems to be playing out an imagined scene in his mind. Loves chocolate.

**Implementation possibilities:** The handmade paper/cotton/tape
creations are a particularly personal hook. A young inventor, artist,
builder, imaginative NPC, handmade creature, or improvised construction
could carry his spirit without simply using his name.

## Ian

**Who he is:** Jason and Megan\'s youngest son. Definitely a momma\'s
boy. Curious, social, quick-thinking, and increasingly interested in
soccer.

**Interests and traits:** Loves YouTube, VR and games in general,
especially UG, Hero X Hero, and Gorilla Tag. Loves science at school,
dinosaurs, and space. Wants to be an astronaut when he grows up, but
does not want to have to move away from his family. Plays soccer as a
defender.

**Implementation possibilities:** The aspiring astronaut who does not
want to leave his family is an especially strong Starborn-compatible
detail. Science, space, dinosaurs/ancient creatures, defense/protection,
and social gaming are other hooks.

**Traits and details:** Older, very nice, quirky, and notably forgetful.

**Traits and memories:** Had many small strokes during his life, so

Worked as an engineer at the Newport News shipyard. Grew up country but

**Implementation status:** Open. Engineering, mathematics,
shipyard/industrial work, travel, and grandfatherly affection are better

# 5. Music and Creative History

## Great Frontier

**Context:** Jason\'s solo music/story project. Ten songs are
plot-driven and include voice acting and sound effects.

**Existing Starborn integration:** The songs already exist in Starborn
as VHS-like media discovered by the player and experienced in a
screening room. Because Starborn presents them as something like movies,
the player can watch a visual while hearing the song/story.

**Alex and Carly:** The main Great Frontier characters are deliberately
NOT on the master Easter-egg list as normal NPCs for now. Possible VHS
covers, posters, or a subtle doppelganger cameo were discussed but left
for later.

**Guideline:** Great Frontier already has a distinctive in-universe
lane. Avoid over-integrating it to the point that it competes with
Starborn.

## No Charge / The Wayfarer

**Context:** Two names for the same core band from Jason\'s youth; No
Charge was the name for most of its history and The Wayfarer was the
later name. Chris played bass, Andrew lead guitar, and Cheno ran sound
for many shows.

**Implementation concept:** Treat the two names as different eras of an
in-universe music history without explicitly writing \'formerly No
Charge.\' They can appear as separate artifacts with subtle continuity.

**Strong artifact idea:** A text-described Wayfarer tour poster/flyer
that the player can inspect through Starborn\'s exploration UI. It can
list futuristic tour dates and actual Starborn locations. No new art
asset is required. A nearby old No Charge album/tape or other artifact
could represent the earlier era.

**Tone:** The music references should feel like genuine in-universe
culture, not a museum exhibit about Jason.

## Chris + Andrew Side Quest

**Placement:** Chris and Andrew will likely be in the same general area,
perhaps the same room or within a couple of screens. No Charge/The
Wayfarer references should be physically or narratively linked to them.

**Character treatment:** They should be intelligent, capable people with
legitimate roles in Starborn\'s world, not joke \'space rocker\'
caricatures.

**Quest purpose:** Give them a real optional side quest with an
emotional subtext: the three friends made things together, life
scattered them to different places, and the time they shared still
mattered.

**Jason\'s role:** Jason does not need to appear physically in this
quest. Nova becomes Jason\'s emotional proxy.

**Ending beat:** After Chris and Andrew thank Nova/the party for
helping, Nova turns around and thanks them. To a normal player it is a
sincere character moment; to Chris and Andrew it is Jason speaking to
them through Nova. The core message: some things do not stop mattering
simply because people move on.

# 6. Places, Work, and Life References

## VIMS / CCRM

**Meaning:** Jason\'s professional home. A place where he gets to work
alongside people he deeply respects and admires, with a noble mission.

**Identity:** Science in service of something worthwhile: coastal
science, wetlands, flooding, habitats, resilience, environmental
stewardship, and work that can help communities and the environment.

**Jason\'s role:** Often the technology person among scientists:
building and maintaining infrastructure and tools that help other
people\'s expertise reach the world.

**Implementation possibility:** A small research station populated by
extremely smart researchers studying an ecosystem/environmental problem
while a technology person keeps their equipment and systems running. It
should feel affectionate and mission-focused rather than like a literal
VIMS copy.

## Coastal Virginia / Williamsburg / Newport News / Yorktown

**Meaning:** Home. Jason grew up in Newport News and built his adult
life around Williamsburg/Coastal Virginia.

**What he loves:** Trees, water, coastal nature, and the unusual mixture
of rural, suburban, and urban environments sitting close together.

**Implementation possibility:** Rather than naming real places, allow a
Starborn location to carry Coastal Virginia DNA: heavy trees opening
onto water, marsh/coastal environments, modern infrastructure close to
woods, and a blend of developed and natural spaces.

**Specific sub-reference:** Yorktown Beach is separately meaningful
because of Kodi and the phrase \'go beach.\'

## Cox Communications / Call Center

**Context:** Jason previously worked in a Cox Communications call
center.

**Implementation possibility:** An abandoned or rundown futuristic call
center/comms hub, but only if such a place naturally fits the story. A
very quick joke could have Nova grateful she never had to work there,
perhaps implying it seems worse than her mining work.

**Guideline:** Keep it quick and do not force it into the game if the
story never provides the right location.

**Zeke connection:** Zeke\'s corporate-supervisor schtick is a natural
place to fold in Jason\'s Cox Communications / telecom / call-center
history. Corporate jargon, metrics, scripts, escalation chains,
productivity pressure, and soul-draining communications-company
bureaucracy can become part of Zeke\'s humor and background without
requiring a separate call-center location.

## Other optional life touchstones

**Possible sources:** Programming/computers, AI/automation, guitars and
music-making, retro games/emulation, event-lighting/DJ work, and
property-management history.

**Guideline:** These are secondary. Do not interview/catalog every part
of Jason\'s life merely to create Easter eggs. Use them only when they
naturally strengthen a person, place, joke, or story moment.

# 7. Design Principles for the Easter Eggs

-   People and pets are the priority. Places, jobs, and hobbies are
    supporting material.

-   Do not make every reference a framed picture, named NPC, or memorial
    plaque. Variety is part of the point.

-   References should work inside Starborn even for a player who knows
    none of the real-world context.

-   Specific personal details are more valuable than generic name drops:
    \'go beach,\' Bella\'s elephant, Tyson\'s handmade plushies,
    Charlie\'s \'Baby Steps,\' Oliver\'s schedule, Penny herding the
    family, etc.

-   Deceased loved ones should be treated with warmth and care, but
    there is no rule that they must all become NPCs.

-   Living relatives connected to a memorial should not accidentally be
    framed as deceased or as part of an in-memoriam marker.

-   Do not force negative or sensitive traits into references simply
    because they are true. Prefer details that the person would
    recognize and appreciate.

-   Copyrighted interests (Mario, Frozen, Ninja Turtles, Ted Lasso,
    etc.) are useful for understanding the person but generally should
    inspire an original Starborn analogue rather than appear directly.

-   Jason himself is already present throughout Starborn as its creator.
    His explicit Easter egg should therefore be optional, playful, and
    meta-adjacent rather than self-important.

-   Some references can remain unresolved until the right room, quest,
    item, or character naturally appears during development.

# 8. Strongest Implementation Ideas So Far

**Hidden Jason Workshop ---** Optional secret room with an endlessly
creating Jason-like builder surrounded by unfinished projects, music,
technology, games, mementos, and another new idea already underway.

**Chris + Andrew Quest ---** A sincere side quest tied to No Charge/The
Wayfarer. Nova ultimately thanks them on Jason\'s behalf: the time they
shared still mattered.

**Cheno NPC / Memorial ---** A substantial, warm, dependable character
inspired by Cheno rather than a generic static memorial.

**Charlie / Baby Steps Creature ---** A shy creature whose trust grows
across repeated visits until it finally approaches Nova.

**Penny Party-Herder ---** A large loving creature that wants the entire
group together and tries to befriend smaller creatures that are
intimidated by its size.

**Oliver the Supervisor ---** A social creature/character who quietly
keeps everyone and everything on schedule.

**Wayfarer Tour Poster ---** Inspectable text-only poster listing
futuristic dates and real Starborn locations, with a nearby No Charge
artifact.

**Kodi + Bella ---** Still needs a final implementation. Kodi\'s \'go
beach\' and Bella\'s elephant are the strongest personal anchors.

**VIMS-inspired Research Station ---** Smart, mission-driven researchers
doing worthwhile environmental work, supported by a tech person keeping
the operation running.

**Zeke + Corporate Telecom / Call Center ---** Use Zeke\'s
corporate-supervisor schtick as the primary home for
Cox/telecom/call-center satire: scripts, metrics, escalation chains,
corporate jargon, and productivity pressure. A brief abandoned
call-center/comms location can still appear if the story naturally
supports it.

as Starborn develops.*
