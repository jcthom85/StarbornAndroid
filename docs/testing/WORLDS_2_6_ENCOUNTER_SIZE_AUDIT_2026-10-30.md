# Worlds 2–6 authored encounter-size audit

October 30, 2026. Counts describe static authored parties, not difficulty, mandatory fights, event-spawned encounters, or balance certification. When `enemy_parties` is absent, the controller treats each entry in `enemies` as a separate single-enemy party.

| World | Singles | Pairs | Three or more |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 13 | 0 | 0 |
| 3 | 6 | 3 | 0 |
| 4 | 36 | 2 | 0 |
| 5 | 39 | 3 | 0 |
| 6 | 14 | 2 | 1 |

World 2's static catalog does corroborate the single-opponent observation. The Source Beast is also a single enemy, but that is an event-driven boss. Later worlds have authored groups. Changing World 2 encounter composition, aggressiveness or rewards requires a design/balance decision; this audit makes no such changes.

## Subsequent approved World 2 changes

The table above records the pre-change audit. The approved follow-up regrouped the same 13 World 2 enemies into **seven singles and three pairs**. See [implementation and seeded balance evidence](WORLD2_ENCOUNTER_AND_SALVAGE_PASS_2026-10-30.md). Worlds 3–6 remain unchanged.
