**Commit Reviewed:** `a3a1501c2df68c01062fd6ea8fcc11bee5de2550` (`main`, clean working tree)

## 1. Executive Summary

Starborn's six-world progression currently runs on **two broken structural axles** and **one severe archetype divergence**:

1. **All Tier-2 and Tier-3 weapons (8/8) and nearly all character armors (9/12) are unreachable in the live campaign.** `weapon_shop` (*The Armory*), `armor_shop` (*The Aegis*), `accessory_shop` (*Trinkets & Tech*), and `mechanic_shop` (*Mechanic's Wares*) exist in `shops.json` but have **zero openers** in `rooms.json`, `npcs.json`, `dialogue.json`, `events.json`, or Kotlin code. After Nova upgrades from `mining_pistol` (4–7 dmg) to `nova_laser_blaster` (7–10 dmg) by defeating `the_iron_warden` at the very end of World 1 (`W01-08`), **no party member receives another weapon for the remaining five worlds**.
2. **Level-ups grant zero base stat growth and zero AP (`LevelingManager.kt`, `PartyHealth.kt`, `CombatController.kt:3120-3170`).** Of the 12 levels in `leveling_data.json`, **8 levels (2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 11) are completely empty**; only Levels **6, 9, and 12** unlock skills (`progression.json`). Every point of HP, Strength, Vitality, Agility, Focus, and Defense after `W01-02` comes exclusively from gear, mods, accessories, snacks, prepared meals, and the shared AP skill trees.
3. **Massive power divergence between player archetypes — paired with a World 5 difficulty peak and a World 6 boss inversion.**
   - A **Main-Path** player reaches World 5 (`compliance_avatar`: 780 HP, 64 single-target / 49 AoE skill damage) with **World 1 weapons**, **base HP (95–150)** on 3 of 4 crew members, **5,019 gross credits** (unable to afford mid/late shop gear after healing spend), and **43–50 AP** (after a 3-world AP drought where W4/W5/W6 mid-world main quests award 0 AP). Basic attack damage decays *downward* from W1 (`18`) to W5 (`6–10`) as enemy DEF outscales static player STR.
   - An **Explorer** who talks to side NPCs and clears side rooms gets **free W2–W3 accessories** (`+30` to `+50` Max HP, `+3–5` DEF, `+4–5` FOC) that dwarf 1,890c–2,160c shop accessories, reaches **Lv 12 at the end of World 4 (`W04-04`)** (zero level growth across all of Worlds 5–6), and grosses **13,523 credits** (2.7× Main-Path).
   - A **Tinkerer** can craft late-game mods early (`mod_thermite_core` +8 STR/+5 LCK, `mod_orbital_deflector`, `mod_astral_weave` have `requires_schematic = null`), and can pick up `thermite_core_mod` (`+8 STR, +5 LCK`) inside World 1's `mine_sealed_munitions_vault` (`W01-06`) — nearly doubling Nova's W1 attack power — yet hits hard zero-sum bottlenecks on **unbuyable, undroppable** materials (`ballistic_weave`: 4 total copies; `nano_filament`: 4 one-time pickups + 2–4.5% W5 fishing).
   - **Cooking** (specifically **Well-Fed** `ActiveMealBuff` with Zeke's `+25 Max HP, +3 Stability` party-wide serving perk for 3 battles) is the single highest-leverage stabilizer for Main-Path survival, and unintentionally triggers on ordinary `ration_pack` items because `provision_field_ration_press` outputs `ration_pack` with default `category = "meal"`.
   - **World 6** locks out Astra return until `ms_game_complete`, has **zero shops**, drops enemy credits to **0**, and drops its final bosses (`ascended_vale` 470 HP / 21–25 dmg; `ascended_god` 660 HP / 29 dmg) **below** World 5's `compliance_avatar` (780 HP / 49–64 dmg) and World 4's `titan_walker_boss` (860 HP). Meanwhile, the 6th fishing zone (`singularity_ether_well`), its two exclusive fish (`void_ray`, `chronos_guppy`), their two dishes, and both Master Angler mastery rewards are locked behind `ending_complete = true` (post-game only).

## 3. Methodology, Archetypes & Explicit Assumptions

### 3.1 Core Runtime Rules Verified in Code
- **Leveling (`leveling_data.json`, `CombatController.kt:3375-3424`, `PartyHealth.kt`):** Cumulative XP thresholds: `Lv1=0, Lv2=100, Lv3=350, Lv4=900, Lv5=1,800, Lv6=3,000, Lv7=5,000, Lv8=7,500, Lv9=11,000, Lv10=15,000, Lv11=20,000, Lv12=26,000`. New party members (`zeke`, `orion`, `gh0st`) inherit Nova's current XP and level on join (`GameSessionStore.kt:649-650`) and receive + auto-equip their starter weapon and armor (`ExplorationController.kt:1268-1282`). **Leveling grants 0 stat points and 0 AP**; only Lv 6, Lv 9, and Lv 12 unlock skills (`progression.json`).
- **AP Skill Trees (`NovaBuildPrototype.kt`, `CrewBuildPrototype.kt`):** Shared `playerAp` pool across 4 characters × 2 branches × 4 nodes (`1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10 AP` per branch; **80 AP** to max all 4 characters). All Tier-4 capstones (4 AP each) require `ms_w4_mq20_complete` (end of World 4). Sources: 16 main quests (`35 AP`), 6 AP-bearing side quests (`w1_sq03` [mandatory], `w2_sq01`, `w3_sq12`, `w4_sq19`, `w5_sq22`, `w6_sq26` = `12 AP`), Orion + Gh0st recruitment (`+4 AP`), and enemy `ap_reward` (`administrator_boss` 2, `titan_walker_boss` 3, `compliance_avatar` 4, optional `boardroom_bailiff` 4, `ascended_vale` 5, `ascended_god` 8 = `26 AP`). **Total finite campaign AP = 77 AP** (plus 3 AP from `w6_mq30` post-final-boss = **80 AP** exact cap).
- **Equipment & Mod Slots (`GearRules.kt`, `ModEquipmentRules.kt`, `BaseEquipmentRules.kt`):** Physical inventory (`physicalGearInventory = true`: 1 copy = 1 character). Mod slot unlocks: `weapon_mod1` (`W01-03`), `armor_mod1` (`W01-04`), `weapon_mod2` (`W01-06`), `armor_mod2` (`W01-08`).
- **Quest Rewards Architecture (`QuestRuntimeManager.kt:513-537`, `ExplorationController.kt:6910`):** `quests.json` `rewards` arrays are **UI display only**; actual XP, items, and credits are granted by `events.json` and `dialogue.json` triggers. Cross-verification confirmed all 62 quests match their actual event/dialogue grants (no missing quest rewards once `dialogue.json`'s `"trigger"` strings are included), and **0 of 62 main/side quests award credits in `quests.json`** (only 1 side quest, `w4_sq_crucible`, grants 350c via Pitmaster Brutus's dialogue trigger).
- **Enemy Persistence vs. Repeat Farming (`ExplorationController.kt:1849-1858, 5510-5524`, `EnemyMovementManager.kt`):** All static room encounters and roaming patrols (`w1_deep_mine_pressure_patrol`, `spire_rooftop_assassin`) stay **permanently defeated** once cleared. Outside the **Astra Simulation Deck** (`W02-04` onward, where defeated encounters replay with full standard XP, credits, drops, and **non-deduplicated boss `ap_reward`**), all campaign combat XP and credits are finite.
- **Resting (`ExplorationController.kt:1068-1088`):** Free (`0c`) full-HP party recovery at rest stops in every world (W1: 3, W2: 5, W3: 2, W4: 2, W5: 2, W6: 2, Astra: 1).
- **Access & Craft Rules:** Tinkering is available **anywhere** via `Menu → Field Kit` (`ExplorationScreen.kt:3477-3485`); Cooking requires a **cooking station** (`CraftingService.kt`), and 8 recipes require visiting specific `discovery_room` stations.

## 5. System-by-System Quantitative Audits

### 5.1 Levels, Skills, AP Economy & Enemy Scaling

1. **8 of 12 Levels Are Completely Empty (`leveling_data.json`, `progression.json`, `CombatController.kt:3120-3170`):**
   - `Player.toCombatant` reads static base stats from `characters.json` (`nova`: 120 HP / 8 STR / 6 VIT / 9 AGI / 6 FOC; `zeke`: 150 HP / 6 STR / 7 VIT / 5 AGI / 3 FOC; `orion`: 95 HP / 4 STR / 4 VIT / 8 AGI / 8 FOC; `gh0st`: 105 HP / 7 STR / 5 VIT / 10 AGI / 7 FOC).
   - Leveling provides **zero HP, zero stat growth, and zero AP**. Levels 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, and 11 produce a celebratory level-up popup with zero gameplay effect.
   - **Pacing mismatch across archetypes:** Main-Path unlocks Lv 6 at `W02-03`, Lv 9 at `W04-03`, and Lv 12 at `W06-01` (even spacing). Explorer unlocks Lv 6 at `W02-01`, Lv 9 at `W03-03`, and **caps at Lv 12 at `W04-04`** (27,670 XP vs. 26,000 cap), leaving **zero level-up rewards for the entire final third of the game (Worlds 5 and 6)**, finishing at 66,140 XP (2.54× the Lv 12 cap).
2. **Severe Mid-to-Late AP Drought + Repeatable Sim-Deck AP Exploit (`NovaBuildPrototype.kt`, `CombatController.kt:3333, 3425`):**
   - Main-Path AP arrives front-loaded in W1–W2 (28 AP by `W02-05`, 2–4 AP almost every quest), then **crashes in W3–W6**: `w3_mq11`, `w3_mq12`, `w4_mq16–19`, `w5_mq21–24`, and `w6_mq26–29` all award **0 AP**. Main-Path goes **6 consecutive main quests (`W04-01`–`W04-03`) with 0 AP**, gets +6 AP at `W04-04` (just as 4-AP Tier-4 capstones unlock), then goes **4 more main quests (`W05-01`–`W05-03`) with 0 AP**, then **4 more main quests (`W06-01`–`W06-03`) with 0 AP** until after Vale/God.
   - Furthermore, **16 of Main-Path's 66 total AP (24%)** arrive at `W06-03b`–`W06-04` (`ascended_vale` +5, `ascended_god` +8, `w6_mq30` +3) — *after* the fights where AP builds actually matter!
   - Conversely, `ExplorationController.kt:1849-1858` launches Astra Simulation Deck encounters through standard combat reward code (`CombatController.kt:3425`), which **re-awards `enemy.apReward` on every repeat win**. Once `administrator_boss` (+2 AP) is defeated at `W03-05`, a player can max all 80 AP in ~15–20 minutes of Simulation Deck farming.
3. **Enemy Scaling Spikes, Zero-Reward Roadblocks & World 6 Inversion (`enemies.json`, `CombatActionProcessor.kt:1109-1212`):**
   - **Basic attack decay:** Player basic physical damage is `(STR×2 + avg_weapon_dmg) − floor(enemy_DEF / 2)`. Because Main-Path STR stays near `6–8` and weapons freeze at `W01-08` (`7–10`), while enemy VIT climbs from `6` (W1) → `12` (W2) → `15` (W3) → `18` (W4) → `20` (W5), **Main-Path basic attacks deal *less* damage in World 5 (`6–10` dmg) than in World 1 (`17–20` dmg)**. Only skills (which skip target `DEF` in `resolveSkillDamage`) scale via weapon + Focus potency.
   - **Enemy basic attacks get an unintended `unarmedBonus` (`CombatActionProcessor.kt:1118`):** Because enemies have `weapon = null`, every enemy basic attack adds `unarmedBonus = max(3, STR)`, making enemy basic attacks effectively `3× STR − floor(player_DEF/2)`! That makes **enemy basic attacks hit HARDER than many enemy signature skills** (e.g. `titan_walker_boss`: 62 basic vs. 40 `foundry_titan_cut` / 23 `foundry_titan_stomp`; `ascended_god`: 50 basic vs. 29 `source_great_silence`!).
   - **Zero-reward high-HP walls:** `hk_droid` (`deep_anchor_chamber`, W5) has **1,200 HP, 48 ATK, 60% physical resist, and `nature_heal`**, yet drops **0 XP, 0 Credits, 0 AP, and 0 items**. `conveyor_crusher` (W4 hazard, 510 HP, 80% physical/burn resist) also yields 0/0/0.
   - **World 6 stat drop-off:** Common enemies plunge from W5's 220–440 HP down to 120–130 HP (`source_shadow`, `glitch_hound`, `distorted_sentinel`), while the two finale bosses (`ascended_vale` 470 HP, 21–25 skill dmg; `ascended_god` 660 HP, 29 skill dmg) sit well below W5's `compliance_avatar` (780 HP, 49–64 skill dmg) and W6's own Nightmare elites (`manager_projection` 720 HP, `silent_shore_wraith` 680 HP, `2× endless_war_echo` 1,120 combined HP at 50 ATK). Note also that `AstraCatalog.kt:56` advertises Ascended God as *"1,800 HP"*, contradicting `enemies.json` (660 HP, or 1,220 with both 280 HP anchors).
4. **Untaught Mechanics & Tutorial Gaps (`tutorial_scripts.json`, `CombatTutorialTracker.kt`):**
   - **Taught well:** Basic attack vs. Weakness (`faulted_loader` at `W01-03`), Guard Break (`acoustic_bulwark` + `nova_hydraulic_kick` at `W01-05`), Tinkering (`repair_cryo_inductor` at `W01-03`), Gear equipping (`gear_equip_basics` at `W01-02`).
   - **Untaught before fights demand them:**
     - **Momentum (1/2/3 stacks → `1.25× / 1.50× / 1.80×` damage, `-1 CD` at 2, guaranteed crit at 3):** No tutorial teaches spending Momentum on skills, yet W3–W5 bosses expect burst windows.
     - **Prepared Meals (`Well-Fed` 3-battle party buff + chef serving perk) vs. Snacks (permanent equippable reusable combat skill):** Never tutorialized before W2/W3 spikes; players routinely hoard meals as single-use battle heals and miss the `+25 Max HP` Zeke serving buff entirely.
     - **Status cleanses & Silence/Meltdown/Corrosion:** W2 `spore_spitter` applies Acid/Weak, W4 spams Meltdown, and W5–W6 spam **Silence** (blocking all skills — the only way Main-Path deals real damage!). Nothing teaches that `w5_sq24`'s optional `data_shield` grants party-wide Silence/Jam immunity, or that **no shop consumable cleanses Silence** (`herbal_poultice` cleanses `weak`, `burn_salve` cleanses `meltdown`, `antidote_flask` cleanses `corrosion`/`poison`/`erosion` — **zero Silence cleanse items exist in `items.json`!**).

### 5.4 Exploration, Pickups, Drops & Recovery

- **Guaranteed vs. Random Drop Economy:** Enemy material drops are unreliable for main-path progression because encounters are finite and never respawn. Key material drop rates: `scrap_metal` (50–100%), `wiring_bundle` (30–60%), `power_cell` (25–70%, plus 100% ×2/×3 on W3/W4/W5 bosses), `beast_meat` (20–75%), `vial_of_venom` (30–40%), `battery` (40–50%), `armor_plate` (20–50%). Notably, **`nano_filament`, `circuit_board`, `ballistic_weave`, `composite_plate`, and `astral_thread` have a 0% drop rate from all enemies**.
- **Single-Pickup Cliff Risks:**
  - Missing `w1_mq01_receive_starter_kit` is impossible (main quest), and `the_iron_warden`'s `nova_laser_blaster` is a 100% drop.
  - Missing **`w2_sq03` (*Tideglass Day*)** locks the player out of `schematic_source_resin`, cutting available `source_resin` from infinite (46c each) to **1 single drop** (`the_beast`), permanently blocking 3 of the 4 recipes that require it (unless farming `the_beast` on the Simulation Deck).
  - Missing **`pit_kitchen` container (`starbar_crunch`)** leaves a Main-Path player with **0 snacks** through Worlds 1 and 2.
  - Missing **`foundry_lava_tube_bypass`** hides World 4's **only shop** (`foundry_welder_shop`), cutting off `medkit_plus`, `burn_salve`, and extra `heat_liner` purchases until World 5 (though players can backtrack via Astra to W2/W3 shops).
  - **Recovery Paths:** Free rest stops prevent exploration HP soft-locks in every world (including World 6). Prior-world backtracking via the Astra (`W02-04`–`W05-04`) provides recovery for missed shops/pickups — **until `W06-01`, where Astra return is hard-locked until post-game**, removing all shop/recovery access before the final bosses.

## 6. World-by-World Progression Walkthrough

- **World 1 (`W01-01`–`W01-08`):** Tightest, best-paced opening stretch, with **two structural glitches**: (1) Main-Path has **0c** when `general_store` unlocks at `W01-04` (still only 40c at `W01-05`), making early shops window-shopping until `W01-06` (125c); (2) `mine_sealed_munitions_vault` (`W01-06`) hands Explorers/Tinkerers `thermite_core_mod` (`+8 STR, +5 LCK`), which roughly doubles Nova's damage and breaks W1–W2 balance. Optional patrol `pressure_hauler` (300 HP, 18 ATK) is a steep solo-Nova ambush spike in `W01-06`. Main-Path heals smoothly thanks to 12+ free quest/ground `medkit_i`/`ration_pack` items before `the_iron_warden` (310 HP), who finally drops `nova_laser_blaster`.
- **World 2 (`W02-01`–`W02-05`):** Introduces fishing, campfire cooking, Orion (`W02-03`), Gh0st (`W02-04`), and the Astra (`W02-04`). **First major archetype split:** Explorers pick up 3 massive free accessories (`pennys_herder_bandana` +40 HP/+4 DEF, `kodi_and_bellas_sunstone` +30 HP, `olivers_supervisory_seal` +30 HP) and can even craft World 6's `astral_weave_mod` (+8 AGI/+8 FOC) by `W02-03` using W2 vault `astral_thread`! Meanwhile Main-Path hits its **first boss wall** at `the_beast` (`W02-04`, 640 HP, `beast_hunt_claw` 45 dmg — 2-shots Orion's 95 HP unless protected by Zeke's Cover/Well-Fed meal).
- **World 3 (`W03-01`–`W03-05`):** Sharpest enemy jump in the campaign (`riot_guard` 480 HP, `corporate_assassin` 370 HP / 32 ATK / 39.6 SPD, `administrator_boss` 570 HP + 40 AoE / 51 single-target). **Main-Path enters a critical danger zone here:** still at Lv 7–8 (missing Lv 9 skills that Explorers already have at `W03-03`), still holding W1 weapons and 0–1 accessories, with Orion (95 HP) and Gh0st (105 HP) vulnerable to 2-turn AoE wipes from `spire_lattice`. Buying `iron_biscuit` (+80 HP, 368c) at `noodle_row_stall` and eating a Zeke-served `Stellarium Eel Skewer` (+25 HP, +5 DR) completely changes W3 survival. Also opens the **Sim-Deck AP farm** after `W03-05`.

### 8.2 What the Numbers Prove vs. What Must Be Felt in Play

- **Proven by Data (Fix Regardless of Playtest Feel):**
  - 4 shops (`weapon_shop`, `armor_shop`, `accessory_shop`, `mechanic_shop`), 8 weapons, 9 character armors, 8 snacks, and 8 mods are completely unreachable (`P-01`).
  - 8 of 12 levels grant zero stats/AP/skills (`P-02`).
  - World 6 PONR blocks Astra return with 0 shops, locks Zone 6 fishing post-game, and makes `Source Astral Feast` uncookable unless pre-stocked (`P-03`).
  - Simulation Deck re-awards boss `ap_reward` infinitely (`P-06`), `item_arcade_token` is never spent (`P-14`), and `stability`/`tech` equipment mods are silently dropped (`P-10`).
  - `ballistic_weave` (4 total) and `nano_filament` (4 pickups vs. 7 demand) create permanent crafting deadlocks, while W1 `thermite_core_mod` and W2 `astral_weave_mod` break tier gating (`P-07`, `P-08`).
- **Must Be Felt in Live Play (Validate in Section 8.1 Playtests):**
  - **Timed-hit offense/defense windows (`TimedWindowType`):** How much real mitigation skilled vs. average mobile touch players get from defensive timing prompts against W2 Beast, W3 Administrator, and W5 Compliance Avatar.
  - **ATB real-time pressure & turn cadence:** Whether high-SPD enemies (`corporate_assassin` 39.6, `orbital_fighter` 46.4, `glitch_hound` 56.4) overwhelm mobile menu selection speed even when per-hit numbers look survivable.
  - **Stability Break loop & Momentum cadence:** Whether breaking bosses (e.g., W4 Titan 260 Stability, W5 Avatar 250 Stability) creates satisfying burst windows that offset low basic-attack damage, or whether bosses recover before Momentum 2–3 skills fire.
  - **Silence attrition feel in W6:** Whether Silence on `ascended_vale` / `ascended_god` turns an otherwise numerically undertuned finale into a frustrating wait-out for Main-Path players without `data_shield`.
