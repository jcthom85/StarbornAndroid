# Mara Thorne and Arden Vale campaign plan

Mara Thorne is the campaign’s principal antagonist through the Foundry. Arden Vale uses her infrastructure and delegated authority to seize command, then uses the Source to reconstruct reality. World 3 gives Thorne a visible win or loss of her own, World 4 shows the machinery of her project and ends with the betrayal, and Worlds 5–6 follow the consequences.

Thorne wants people, labor, and access to Harmony to remain scarce enough to sell and controllable enough to own. Vale thinks independent voices inevitably create conflict and pain. He needs Thorne’s system to reach the Source, then intends to replace the world that makes her business possible. Their alliance holds while both need the same machinery. It breaks when Thorne wants to contain the crew and preserve the asset, and Vale decides that the people carrying the signal are expendable.

## Campaign beats

| World | Scene or location | What changes for the player |

| 2 | Facility records or existing crew reaction | Audit existing experiment lore. If it supports the link, add one optional record showing that someone approved continuation despite the human cost. Keep the local survival and discovery story in front. |
| 3 | Archive approach, before taking the Lens | Give Thorne an order the player actually sees enforced. She directs the Administrator to keep the Lens intact, recover the crew’s access, and close the freight route. | 3 | Archive Vault after the Lens | Thorne initiates the audit and calls Vale in under a limited recovery mandate. Vale uses that access to seal the roof and authorize lethal force. Thorne tries to countermand the order and warns the crew when she cannot. The Administrator fight remains the physical climax. |
| 4 | Foundry records and final audit | Connect existing production records to Thorne’s project. Let a worker record or crew reaction explain what the quota meant for people. At the final audit, Rylos invokes Thorne’s authority. Vale revokes it and takes command in the existing departure sequence. Make this the moment the player can name as the betrayal. |
| 5 | Mainframe Nave, Anchor Chamber, and Throne Room | The Thorne trace shows how Vale took control of her permissions. Thorne admits what she approved and gives one concrete piece of help that costs her something, such as surrendering a route credential that exposes her own records. Elara’s reunion stays centered on Elara and Gh0st. Vale demonstrates his plan in the Throne Room through an existing system or memory already rewritten to remove dissent. |
| 6 | The Center and the Silence | Give Vale one specific example of what his reconstructed reality removes. Let Nova answer through a crew memory the player has already experienced. The Soloist and the Silence carry the argument into the existing finale. |

The World 3 override and World 4 betrayal need different consequences. In the Archive, Vale exceeds the authority Thorne lent him. In the Foundry, he removes her authority altogether. World 5 reveals how he made the takeover stick. This gives Thorne time to remain responsible for the harm done in her name after the Archive scene.

## Vance and the salon

Keep the murder investigation optional and preserve the two deductions. The access log proves Vance lied about where he was. The missing coolant core links his equipment to the poison. The auditor’s datapad can deepen his motive without becoming a new requirement.

Break the confession into three beats. Vance sees that blaming the log no longer works. He admits what Malick’s audit would expose and why he chose to stop it. Security then takes his credentials and holds him for transport. Give Lyra a short post-case conversation in which she says what she’ll do with Malick’s unfinished audit. She should also have a reason to worry that filing it will make her a target. Dax or Selene can get a reaction later if the scene still feels empty on replay.

Vance owns his choice. Thorne does not need to know about the murder. The connection is the pressure both stories put on an audit: Vance tries to bury one to protect his career, while Thorne can use one to shut down an entire district. Players who skip the salon still need to understand Thorne and Vale from the main route.

One naming check belongs in this pass. World 1 calls the protagonist Nova Vance, and the salon suspect is Director Vance. Confirm whether that shared surname is intentional. If it is, decide how Nova recognizes it on screen. If it is accidental, rename the director’s display name and keep his internal IDs and save flags.

## Implementation order

1. **Write the mandatory handoff first.** Draft the pre-Lens order, the Archive override, and the Foundry final audit together. For each scene, identify the speaker who controls the system, the order they give, the immediate effect, and what Nova learns. Read the three scenes back to back before editing assets.
2. **Implement World 3 and the salon.** Reuse the Archive approach, Lens sequence, confession, and existing quest gates. Add a completion-gated Lyra follow-up. Confirm players who already solved the case can read the follow-up without receiving a second reward. Preserve the rooftop encounters and departure prerequisites.
3. **Implement the Foundry and Ring.** Reconcile the Foundry’s existing references to Vale’s factory work with Thorne’s ownership of the project. Let Vale have designed or modified parts of the machinery under her authority where existing lore supports it. Use the final audit for the transfer and the Mainframe/Throne Room for evidence and consequences. Check that the trace, maze, Anchor, and Tear still follow their current order.
4. **Make the bookend pass.** Read World 1’s disposal order, World 2’s facility evidence, and World 6’s finale against the new spine. Add only the lines needed to establish or pay off something the player can remember.
5. **Verify and update guides.** Run the existing quest tests and play the revised mandatory route with the salon skipped, then replay with the salon solved. Check leave-and-return behavior, save/reload around the confession and Lens, and continuation from saves made after the Foundry audit. Update walkthroughs after scene and interaction names are stable.

## Playtest questions

Ask these without supplying the answers. After the Archive, who started the lockdown, and whose orders made it lethal? At the end of the Foundry, who is in charge, and what did they do to take over? After the salon, what happens to Vance and Malick’s audit? At The Center, what would Vale’s reconstructed world take from someone the player knows?

The pass succeeds if players can answer the main-story questions without the optional records or murder quest, and if a returning player can see the salon aftermath without replaying the deduction. Existing bosses, rewards, and routes give us enough to stage this pass through dialogue, records, and reactions.
