# Airlane to the Spire storyboard

Draft illustrated expansion of scene_w2_astra_departure. Sample lines are proposed dialogue. Geography and actions follow the existing scene and quest.

## Scene intent

Give the crew a moment to feel the Astra rise, demonstrate how they work together, then make the Spire's interference force a hard landing. End with the crew physically present and ready to act beneath the city.

The Astra remains below the planetary Shield. The World 1 pod and World 2 ship have separate journeys. The departure begins after the player confirms at the restored bridge and ends at spire_sewers_landing.

## Proposed art

1. Astra above Sector 9 with the planetary Shield distant above the horizon.
2. Bridge interior that supports several crops, with all four established character appearances and physically plausible positions.
3. Spire approach containing the high city and a lower service route that can support both wide and descending crops.

Reuse the destination room for arrival if its composition works. A fourth landing image is optional after the first animatic review. The bridge shot should not contain baked-in text or UI.

Reference the cold open's actual illustrations for palette, rendering and camera motion. Reference existing Astra and character assets for design continuity. Do not derive a new ship or redesign the crew for these shots.

## Integration

Keep the existing departure event's prerequisites and owning on_complete handoff. Expand the existing scene and remove or shorten the final message that currently repeats its route and landing. Check both the owning event and any follow-up dialogue for duplicate exposition.

Scene art should convey the crew's work without implying the player failed a flight minigame. Their chosen low route explains survival and destination. The music resolves into the first Spire room's soundscape after the landing reveal.

## Review questions

Does the opening allow relief before the warning? Can a player explain why the ship stays below the Shield? Is the guidance failure visibly imposed from outside? Does each crewmate have a readable contribution? Does the landing lead smoothly into exploration without a second arrival summary?
