Starborn • Mobile Game

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Junewire Games ("we", "our", or "us") develops and publishes Starborn ("the Game"), a single-player narrative sci-fi role-playing game.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with Starborn on Android devices.

1. Information Collection and Use

Starborn does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or sensitive user data.

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, or any personal identifiers.
  • No Device Identifiers or Tracking: We do not track Advertising IDs, Android IDs, IMEI numbers, IP addresses, or device fingerprints.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: We do not integrate third-party analytics SDKs, crash trackers, or behavior profiling tools.
  • No Account Creation: Starborn does not require an account, sign-in, or registration to play.

2. Device Permissions

Starborn requests only the minimum necessary system permissions to operate:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide haptic feedback during in-game physical impacts, explosions, and tactile user interface interactions.

No camera, microphone, location, external storage, or network permissions are requested or used by the game.

3. Local Game Saves

All game progress, inventory, character statistics, dialogue choices, and user settings are stored locally on your device within the application's private sandboxed data storage.

  • This data never leaves your device.
  • You can permanently delete this data at any time by clearing the application storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the application.

4. Advertising and In-App Purchases

  • No Advertising: Starborn contains no ads, banners, interstitial popups, or rewarded video advertisements.
  • No Microtransactions: Starborn does not contain in-app purchases, loot boxes, or commercial transactions.

5. Children's Privacy

Starborn does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. Because the game collects no personal data of any kind, it complies fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international privacy standards.
