## Hub Layout
The foundation of the Spire. It never sees the sun, only the neon glow of the city above and the constant rain.

**Progression Model:** Arrival → Information Gathering → Ascent.

## Stage 1
### Node 1: Vent Output
*   **Icon:** 🗑️
*   **Type:** Start / Arrival
*   **Description:** You drop into a pile of wet cardboard in a dead-end alley. The rain is acidic enough to sting.
*   **Internal Content:** Alley.
*   **Purpose:** Arrival.
*   **Unlocks:** Stage 2 (The Static).
...
### Node 2: The Static
*   **Icon:** 🍸
*   **Type:** Story / Base
*   **Description:** A dive bar carved into a subway car. Loud, dark, and shielded from sensors.
*   **Internal Content:** Bar, Zeke's Apartment.
*   **Purpose:** Base Unlock.
*   **Unlocks:** Stage 3 (Night Market AND The Sewers).

## Stage 3 (Choice)
### Node 3A: Night Market
*   **Icon:** 🍜
*   **Type:** Choice A / Shop
*   **Description:** A sensory overload. Vendors shout over sizzling woks. Lanterns string across the alley.
*   **Internal Content:** Stalls.
*   **Purpose:** Shop access / Breather.
*   **Unlocks:** Stage 4 (Transit Plaza).

### Node 3B: The Sewers
*   **Icon:** 🚇
*   **Type:** Choice B / Combat
*   **Description:** Knee-deep water. Pipes marked with resistance graffiti.
*   **Internal Content:** Pipe.
*   **Purpose:** Combat Route.
*   **Unlocks:** Stage 4 (Transit Plaza).

## Stage 4
### Node 4: Transit Plaza
*   **Icon:** 🔼
*   **Type:** Gate / Mini-Boss
*   **Description:** A wide, open square dominated by the base of the Great Elevator. Riot barriers funnel crowds.
*   **Internal Content:** Elevator.
*   **Purpose:** Mini-Boss: Sentinel.
*   **Unlocks:** Hub 3.2 (Upper City).
