# World 5 — The Void / The Cradle: complete step-by-step walkthrough

Rebuilt November 12, 2026 against the current authored rooms, actions, quest prerequisites and puzzle settings. Full spoilers. This ordered route includes all five main quests, all five side quests, all 81 Ring rooms, optional caches, arcade discovery, fishing, relic tuning, recovery instructions and a complete Journal checklist. Source-checked; this newly ordered itinerary still needs a full phone playthrough. The November 12 readiness pass renames the service rooms and restores quest aftermath text; install a build containing that pass to see the updated names.

## 1. How to use this guide

Start at **Executive Dock** (`orbital_executive_dock`) after leaving the Foundry, with **Docking Procedure** active. Follow the numbered steps in order. Bold text names rooms and controls; room IDs disambiguate repeated titles and are not commands. Each route arrow is one NSEW swipe. Finish dialogue and feedback before checking Journal. A visible control may require an earlier task; gray inspection links are completed/read details, not an instruction to earn the reward again.

Keep a manual arrival save, a rolling progress save, a separate save before choosing the guardian ending, and a predeparture save before entering the reality breach. Use normal campaign equipment and combat for balance feedback. A stocked debug save can already contain rewards or progress.

The five optional quests are integrated into the route: **Employee of the Month, Solar Maintenance, Vacuum Seal, Ghost in the Shell, Admin Privileges**. Ghost in the Shell has two exclusive endings: complete one on your main save and use a separate save to compare the other. Do not expect both rewards on one completed quest.

The pending playtest-fixes build restores the service rooms’ optional inspections and adds Charles’s public-queue comparison. The new comparison applies to pending favors; already completed favors stay complete. The directions, quests and step numbers are unchanged. If using 1.3.120 or an earlier build, some of these details are absent: read the room prose and continue; they are not quest requirements.

### Recovery, combat and services

**Customs Lounge** provides Rest, Cook and the **Orbital Dispensary** shop. Later, **Thaw Gallery** provides Rest and Cook. Use the dedicated service pills; the equipment names in this guide identify the location. Review gear, AP, consumables and recovery before bosses. No fixed level or shopping quantity is assumed. Ordinary patrol fights can provide resources, but a passive object description is not a loot promise.

The **Orbital Fighter**, **Security Hub patrol**, **Firewall Construct** and **Compliance Avatar** victories credit main-quest tasks. The Construct also opens both Anchor entrances. Do not substitute tapping a control for actually winning these fights. The Anchor Chamber Hunter-Killer is the story Stasis threat; taking the Anchor credits the scripted relic beat. This guide does not require grinding that enemy.

### Navigation

The route uses NSEW walking throughout. Two reciprocal named Paths controls are optional alternatives: **Service Shaft ↔ Server Farm** by cables, and **Firewall Gamma ↔ Anchor Chamber** by lift after the Construct. These are tapped travel controls, not vertical swipes. Local branch-return shortcuts are optional; walking returns are supplied below.

Service-branch rooms have distinct location names in this readiness pass. Follow the starting room, directions and room IDs; the walking routes have not changed. The optional avionics lab needs the magnetic clamp engaged. The Anchor entrance needs the Construct defeated; Firewall Gamma alone is insufficient. The reality breach is the actual World 6 departure: finish Ring business first.
