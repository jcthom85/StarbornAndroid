# World 5 — The Void / The Cradle: complete step-by-step walkthrough

Rebuilt November 12, 2026 against the current authored rooms, actions, quest prerequisites and puzzle settings. Full spoilers. This ordered route includes all five main quests, all five side quests, all 81 Ring rooms, optional caches, arcade discovery, fishing, relic tuning, recovery instructions and a complete Journal checklist. Source-checked; this newly ordered itinerary still needs a full phone playthrough.

## 1. How to use this guide

Start at **Executive Dock** (`orbital_executive_dock`) after leaving the Foundry, with **Docking Procedure** active. Follow the numbered steps in order. Bold text names rooms and controls; room IDs disambiguate repeated titles and are not commands. Each route arrow is one NSEW swipe. Finish dialogue and feedback before checking Journal. A visible control may require an earlier task; gray inspection links are completed/read details, not an instruction to earn the reward again.

Keep a manual arrival save, a rolling progress save, a separate save before choosing the guardian ending, and a predeparture save before entering the reality breach. Use normal campaign equipment and combat for balance feedback. A stocked debug save can already contain rewards or progress.

The five optional quests are integrated into the route: **Employee of the Month, Solar Maintenance, Vacuum Seal, Ghost in the Shell, Admin Privileges**. Ghost in the Shell has two exclusive endings: complete one on your main save and use a separate save to compare the other. Do not expect both rewards on one completed quest.

### Recovery, combat and services

**Customs Lounge** provides Rest, Cook and the **Orbital Dispensary** shop. Later, **Thaw Gallery** provides Rest and Cook. Use the dedicated service pills; the equipment names in this guide identify the location. Review gear, AP, consumables and recovery before bosses. No fixed level or shopping quantity is assumed. Ordinary patrol fights can provide resources, but a passive object description is not a loot promise.

The **Orbital Fighter**, **Security Hub patrol**, **Firewall Construct** and **Compliance Avatar** victories credit main-quest tasks. The Construct also opens both Anchor entrances. Do not substitute tapping a control for actually winning these fights. The Anchor Chamber Hunter-Killer is the story Stasis threat; taking the Anchor credits the scripted relic beat. This guide does not require grinding that enemy.

### Navigation

The route uses NSEW walking throughout. Two reciprocal named Paths controls are optional alternatives: **Service Shaft ↔ Server Farm** by cables, and **Firewall Gamma ↔ Anchor Chamber** by lift after the Construct. These are tapped travel controls, not vertical swipes. Local branch-return shortcuts are optional; walking returns are supplied below.

Several service-branch rooms still share display titles. Follow the starting room, directions and room IDs so you do not mistake one Ring Service Alcove for another. The optional avionics lab needs the magnetic clamp engaged. The Anchor entrance needs the Construct defeated; Firewall Gamma alone is insufficient. The reality breach is the actual World 6 departure: finish Ring business first.

## 15. Recovery: find the missing step

- **Docking controls only describe the collar:** win the Orbital Fighter first; then force dock, then use sealed airlock.
- **Solar mirrors or garden priority do not advance:** trace false sun path at Mirror Walk, realign mirrors, then redirect garden priority.

- **Shaft traversal does not credit:** win the Compliance Officer / Null-G Drone quest encounter while Zero G is active. Use zero-g cables or patrol timing afterward.
- **Eastern lab blocked:** engage the magnetic clamp at Zero-G Junction; switching it off closes the crossing again.
- **Thorne scene missing:** access the mainframe terminal during Zero G, then use thorne trace at Mainframe Nave and finish the dialogue.
- **Guardian ending unavailable:** trace the purge at Server Farm, recover the routines at Purged Backup Rack, then return to Server Farm. A completed ending prevents the opposite payout.
- **Anchor entrance or lift blocked:** map the maze, operate Alpha/Beta/Gamma in order, then win the Firewall Construct at Gamma. Gamma’s control alone is insufficient.
- **Anchor cannot be taken:** enter during The Anchor, use elara, and finish the reunion before anchor relic.
- **Armory has no reward:** obtain the key from dead sysadmin first. Gray completed inspection should not pay again.
- **Avatar victory does not credit:** witness the Soloist at Throne Room first. The authored Avatar battle is at The Tear. Finish pending dialogue before fighting.
- **Only a quest banner appears:** verify Journal, skills and inventory. Missing narrative feedback is a separate playtest note from missing quest progress; do not expect repeated rewards from a completed control.
