# World 1: Complete Step-by-Step Walkthrough

**Reviewed against the repository on October 2, 2026. Version 1.3.84 (168).**

Full spoilers. This is a route you can follow from a fresh New Game through the crash into World 2, including all five World 1 side quests, room interactions, collectibles, and optional puzzles. Directions and action names come from current assets; checks describe what should happen, not proof that every interaction has been played on a phone.

## How to use this guide

Follow sections 1-11 in order. Each numbered instruction gives you a destination or action. The **Check before leaving** paragraphs are stop signs: resolve missing progress before advancing. Section 12 is the exhaustive room reference; consult the entry for your current room when you want every inspection, connection, gate, and authored reward. Section 13 lists journal objectives verbatim.

- Directions are map directions: north/up, south/down, east/right, west/left. Use the arrows or directional movement controls.
- Room names are followed by internal IDs so you can identify the exact location in screenshots or reports.
- **Talk** means select the NPC and advance through the whole conversation until exploration controls return. Rewards often fire on the final line.
- Use **Menu > Journal** to check quests and **Menu > Items / Party** to inspect supplies, equip compatible gear, and heal. Open **Tinkering** from the field menu when instructed.
- To return to a hub map, walk to that node's entrance first. The game only permits returning from designated entrance rooms. A disabled return control elsewhere is not evidence of a broken quest.
- Never sell or scrap the damaged Cryo-Inductor, Functional Cryo-Inductor, quest proof, badge, Fork, or Chime. Protected items should be excluded, but this route never depends on disposing of them.
- Do not use a stocked debug scenario to judge the campaign economy. For this complete route, start a normal New Game or the Opening scenario.

### Save plan

Keep a **pre-Fork save** until you finish World 1. Use a second slot for your latest progress and Quick Save for temporary checks. Save after Wake Up Call, after the shield drill, before the Fork, and before the Warden. Saving to one slot repeatedly destroys your older checkpoint; choose slots deliberately.

### What is actually mandatory?

All five main quests are mandatory. **Heavy Lifting** is filed as a side quest, but its shield certification is required for Boggs' mine authorization. Scavenger's Stash, System Flush, Protocol Override, The Lost Shift, the ore-cart vault, and the mine Riot Control Post detour are optional for the main route; this guide completes them.

The lift descent is **not a demonstrated permanent point of no return**. The authored lift has a return connection. Finish upper-colony content first for convenience, rather than because this guide claims it becomes permanently inaccessible. Completing the Fork starts the escape story; finish your completionist sweep before that transition. The navigation console at the end launches the World 2 transition.

## 1. Wake up, meet Jed, and sweep The Pit

**Main quest: Wake Up Call (`w1_mq01`).** Start: Nova's Bunk (`pit_nova_bunk`).

1. Watch or skip the intro, then wait for the bunk fade to finish. Turn on **bunk light**.
2. Inspect **door control panel** while the light is on. This isolates the conduit and opens the exit. If the panel is unavailable, confirm the light is on; do not turn it off yet.
3. Inspect **bunk** if you want to rest. Resting is optional and restores the party; dismiss its prompt fully.
4. Go **west** to Pod Row (`pit_L2_corridor`). Inspect **sleeping pods** and **posters**.
5. Go **west** to Supply Closet (`pit_storage`). Turn on **emergency breaker** before attempting the **loose floor panel**. Inspect the panel for the first VHS tape; also inspect **filter units**. Return **east** to Pod Row.
6. Go **south** to Lift Shaft (`pit_shaft`). Inspect **ladder** and **pipe**.
7. Go **west** to Jed's Bunk (`pit_jed_bunk`). Talk to **Jed** until he has told you to visit the workshop and the conversation ends. Inspect **tool case** for spare supplies and **manuals** for lore.
8. Open Items / Party and review the starter kit. The kit event grants Mining Pistol, Nova's Flux Liner, damaged Cryo-Inductor, scrap metal, and a ration. Equip suitable gear through Party if needed; keep the damaged inductor and at least one scrap for its repair.
9. Go **east** to Lift Shaft, then **south** to Pit Landing (`pit_L1_landing`). The colony map exit becomes available after Jed's briefing. Inspect **bulkhead**, but do not leave yet.
10. Go **east** to Mess Hall (`pit_mess`). Inspect **broken Hyperion cabinet** to discover the arcade cabinet; this is discovery, not its later repair/install. Inspect the other listed room interactions for lore.
11. Go **east** to Ration Sink (`pit_kitchen`). Inspect **ingredient hopper** for its one-time supplies and **thermal cooker** for lore. Return **west** to Mess Hall.
12. Go **south** to Vent Crawl (`pit_vents`). Inspect **maintenance crawl** for the hidden supplies and **grates** for lore.
13. Go **east** to Shared Showers (`pit_showers`). Inspect **steam valves first**, then **drainage grate**. The valves make the drain loot available. Inspect **tiles**.
14. Return **west** to Vent Crawl, **north** to Mess Hall, **west** to Pit Landing. Return to the hub map and choose **Jed's Workshop**.

**Check before leaving:** Jed's workshop instruction has fired; your kit is present; you still have the damaged Cryo-Inductor and scrap. Side quest introductions from Scrapper and Doc are not available until Wake Up Call is complete, so do not divert to them yet.

## 2. Repair the cutter and complete Wake Up Call

Start: Scrap Yard (`workshop_yard`).

1. Inspect **loader diagnostic strip** before fighting. It identifies the Faulted Loader's exposed Shock relay.
2. Defeat the **Faulted Loader**. Use an available Shock action when possible; read the current action description rather than relying on an old guide's damage numbers. Heal before a lethal hit, not after one. Clear the victory and tutorial prompts.
3. Go **north** to Jed's Shutter (`workshop_floor`). Talk to **Jed** if his workshop briefing is available, and finish the conversation.
4. Open **Menu > Tinkering**. Select **Functional Cryo-Inductor**. The authored repair needs **1 damaged Cryo-Inductor + 1 scrap metal**. Select/insert the damaged base and scrap as requested by the interface, then craft once. Check that the functional device appears and Cryo Vent unlocks.
5. Close Tinkering. Inspect **Flux Liner** to patch the suit link and clamp the ground strip.
6. Inspect **mining cutter** to confirm the governor bypass. These actions appear after the inductor repair.
7. Inspect **live cutter test** only after the repair, liner patch, and governor confirmation. Watch the surge and let its callback finish. Talk to Jed afterward if prompted; his directions identify Transit Checkpoint and Zeke's Booth.
8. Open Journal. **Wake Up Call must be complete** and **Shift Clearance must be active**. If not, check the three preparation actions and finish any open cinematic/dialogue.
9. Sweep the workshop before leaving: go **north** to Jed's Office (`workshop_office`), inspect **diagnostic bench drawer** for VHS tape 2, then return **south**.
10. Go **west** to Parts Loft (`workshop_loft`). Loot **mod bench**; inspect **parts** and **shipwright's slide rule**. Return **east**.
11. Go **east** to Tool Shed (`workshop_shed`). Loot **toolbox**. Go **south** to Flooded Basement (`workshop_basement`), turn on **workbench power switch**, then loot **components chest**. Return **north**.
12. Go **east** to Loading Dock (`workshop_dock`), then **east** to Back Alley (`workshop_back`). Loot **salvage dolly**; inspect the remaining lore actions. Return **west**, **west**, **west** to Jed's Shutter, then **south** to Scrap Yard.
13. The Loading Dock's **loader / cargo rail** actions are optional Heavy Lifting practice, not the cutter's main quest actions. Do not expect those to certify you before the training quest is active.
14. Save now. Return to the hub map from Scrap Yard and choose **Trade Row**.

**Check before leaving:** repaired inductor owned, Cryo Vent available, Wake Up Call completed. The new tinkering interface may show fewer components after crafting because you consumed them; do not craft the repair again to chase a missing dialogue step.

**If Scrapper gives ordinary chatter:** check Wake Up Call is complete. If the stash is open but the quest remains active, finish the return conversation rather than reopening the container.

## 4. Complete System Flush and sweep Med-Bay

Start: Triage Hall (`medbay_hall`).

1. Go **north** to Doc's Exam Room (`medbay_exam1`). Talk to **Doc** until **System Flush (`w1_sq02`)** starts.
2. Go **east** to Med Supply Closet (`medbay_storage`). Turn on **emergency terminal**, then loot **cryogenic locker**. Return **west**, then **south** to Triage Hall.
3. Heal if needed, then go **east** to Ventilation Hub (`medbay_vents`). Defeat the **two Siren Skimmers**. Let victory processing finish.
4. Inspect **vent first** to identify the cracked housing. Then use **fans** to reroute airflow. Then clear **toxic blockage**. The order matters; repeatedly tapping the blockage before inspection and airflow does not advance the intended chain.
5. Go **east** to Exhaust Mouth (`medbay_exhaust`), inspect **vent pipe** and **grate**, then return **west**.
6. Go **west** to Triage Hall, then **north** to Doc. Finish the turn-in: **90 XP + Corrosive Rounds mod**. Inspect the mod in Party/Items and equip it in a compatible unlocked slot if desired.
7. Return **south** to Triage Hall, go **west** to Decon Lock (`medbay_decon`), then **north** to The Morgue (`medbay_morgue`). Loot **bio-containment canister** and read **recycling terminal**; inspect **drawers**.
8. Return **south**, then **east** to Triage Hall. Inspect **bio-scanner** and other remaining interactions, then return to the hub map.
9. If you want to heal at Nova's bunk before the next stretch: enter The Pit at Pit Landing, go **north, north, east** to Nova's Bunk, rest, then retrace **west, south, south** to the map exit.
10. Save. Choose **Transit Checkpoint** on the hub map.

**Check before leaving:** System Flush is Completed. If Doc only reminds you about fumes, inspect the vent, reroute fans, and clear blockage in that order, then talk again.

## 5. Get Zeke's temporary clearance

**Main quest: Shift Clearance (`w1_mq02`).** Start: Shift Queue (`checkpoint_queue`).

1. Inspect the queue's listed interactions, then go **north** to Search Bay (`checkpoint_bay`). Talk to **Guard Hank** until Nova's clearance rejection is logged.
2. Go **east** to Zeke's Booth (`checkpoint_booth`). Talk to **Zeke**, advance to the paperwork choice, and choose **Grid Instability**. Operator Error and Unauthorized Modification are wrong choices; read their feedback and return to the correct choice if offered.
3. Finish the conversation. Check you received the **Mine Access Badge** and Shift Clearance completed.
4. Go **north** to Holding Cell (`checkpoint_cell`). Loot **mortar seam**, read **graffiti**, and inspect **bench**. Return **south** to Zeke's Booth.
5. Go **west** to Search Bay, **north** to Blast Door A (`checkpoint_door`), then **north** through Transit Tunnel (`checkpoint_tunnel`) to **Concourse Lobby (`admin_lobby`)**. Inspect each intermediate room's interactions as you pass.
6. Entering Logistics starts **The Echo (`w1_mq03`)** if it has not already started. The Transit Checkpoint and Admin Concourse remain connected; you do not need to wait for a new-node animation at every doorway.

**If the gate rejects you:** finish Hank's rejection first, choose Grid Instability with Zeke, and finish Zeke's final lines. Having visited the booth without completing the override is not enough.

## 6. Certify Guard Break and get the lift authorized

Start: Concourse Lobby (`admin_lobby`).

1. Talk to **Foreman Boggs**. His initial review sends you to the shield drill and starts **Heavy Lifting: Required Training (`w1_sq03`)**. Finish all lines so registration fires.
2. Go **east** to Customer Service (`admin_window`), then **east** to Security Post (`admin_security`). Complete the registered **Acoustic Bulwark shield-training fight**. Hydraulic Kick should be unlocked by the drill registration; read its Guard Break description and use it to pressure the shield.
3. If no training fight/ability appears, return **west, west** to Boggs and finish his training reminder, then revisit the Security Post. Inspecting the decorative shield trainer alone is not the certification reward.
4. After victory, check Heavy Lifting is Completed and the training has logged. Its authored reward is **100 XP, Heavy Gear, and Hydraulic Fluid**.
5. Return **west** to Customer Service, then **west** to Concourse Lobby. Talk to **Boggs again**. This second full conversation authorizes the Deep Mine and reveals/unlocks its node. Training victory alone is not the final authorization conversation.
6. Sweep the Concourse: go **north** to Observation Deck (`admin_deck`), then **north** to Foreman's Office (`admin_office`); inspect its room actions and return **south**.
7. Go **east** to Information Kiosk (`admin_kiosk`), then **north** to Staff Lounge (`admin_lounge`). Use **executive dispenser** and read **datapad**; inspect **chairs**. Return **south** to the kiosk, then **south** to Customer Service and **west** to the lobby. Inspect **call queue terminal** while passing Customer Service.
8. You can optionally revisit Loading Dock via the Homestead map with Heavy Lifting active to try **loader**, then **cargo rail**. This practice route is not required when the Security Post certification is already complete. Do not spend time chasing a second certification payout.
9. Before descending, complete Protocol Override in section 7. Return to a valid map entrance (Admin Concourse's Concourse Lobby) and choose **Server Room** when available. If its node remains inaccessible, inspect the node's lock message and ensure Shift Clearance and Boggs' conversations are complete.

**Check before leaving:** Heavy Lifting is Completed; Boggs has discussed Sector 4; Deep Mine is revealed/unlocked. The lift still refusing authorization means the Boggs assignment conversation has not fully completed.

## 7. Complete Protocol Override and sweep Server Room

Start: Air-Lock Entrance (`server_airlock`).

1. Go **east** to Crawlspace 4 (`server_crawl`). Use **service junction**, inspect **cables**, then return **west**.
2. Go **north** to Aisle 01 (`server_aisle1`). Defeat the **two Resonance Buoys**. Inspect the aisle's listed actions afterward.
3. Go **east** to Cooling Unit (`server_cooling`). Inspect **pipes** and loot **thermal case**; inspect other actions. Return **west**.
4. Go **north** to The Core Hub (`server_hub`). Use **terminal first**. This starts **Protocol Override (`w1_sq04`)** and identifies the rebel output warning.
5. Use **console** to thaw/stabilize its controls. This is an exploration interaction; you do not need to fire a combat ability at the console.
6. Read **output ports**. Then use **terminal again** to open the routing choices.
7. Choose **Write to the removable circuit board**. The maintenance network and supervisor-account choices are audited wrong routes; they do not complete the correct extraction. If you chose one, reconnect to the terminal and choose the board.
8. Finish the resulting message/cinematic. Check Protocol Override is Completed and you received **Circuit Board + 110 XP**.
9. Go **west** to Backup Storage (`server_backup`). Turn on **backup breaker box**; read **archive terminal** and loot **cases**. Return **east**.
10. Go **east** to Admin Office (`server_office`). Read **executive terminal** and **audit datapads**, then return **west** to the Core Hub.
11. Return **south** to Aisle 01 and **south** to Air-Lock Entrance. Return to the map, then return to the Concourse Lobby.

**If nothing happens at the terminal:** first contact starts the quest; the console must then be thawed; a second terminal interaction opens routing. The reward is on the board choice, not on merely locating or warming the terminal.

## 8. Descend, restore power, and complete The Lost Shift

Start: Concourse Lobby (`admin_lobby`).

1. Verify sections 3, 4, 6, and 7 are complete. Heal and save.
2. Go **west** to Elevator Lobby (`admin_elevator`). Inspect **warning signs**, **scanner**, and **cables**. Choose **Plan mine descent** (the named north travel interaction). On the first authorized descent, the Logistics map reveals **Deep Mine**. Select Deep Mine and enter it. On a later descent the named action may instead say **Enter Deep Mine** and use the continuous room route.
3. You should arrive at **Elevator Landing (`mine_landing`)**. Inspect **lift**, **beacons**, and **machinery**. **Drain pool fishing is locked in the normal World 1 route**: it requires the World 2 pod-examination fishing kit. Do not hunt for a World 1 rod to clear this gate.
4. Go **north** to Main Tunnel Alpha (`mine_alpha`). Defeat its **two Echo Borers**. The mine's first-encounter objective is checked here, not by a made-up fight at the empty landing.
5. Go **north** to Cavern Junction (`mine_junction`). Use **junction breaker box > Restore power**. Leave it on. This lights the deeper mine and exposes the Side Shunt datapad.
6. Backtrack **south** to Main Tunnel Alpha, then **east** to Side Shunt 4 (`mine_shunt`). Entering after power is restored starts **The Lost Shift (`w1_sq05`)**.
7. Read **crew datapad**, finish the letter/cinematic, and check the quest completes. The authored reward is **Recoil Dampener + Crew Datapad + 120 XP**.
8. Inspect **resonant vein** for its one-time resource, plus **crates** and **support beams** for lore.
9. Return **west** to Main Tunnel Alpha, then **north** to Cavern Junction. Continue with its optional branches below.

**If the datapad is missing:** return to the junction and turn power on, then leave/re-enter Side Shunt. Its quest starts on entry with power restored. Inspecting the shunt before the breaker does not satisfy that entry condition.

## 9. Breach the ore-cart vault and finish the mine sweep

2. Use **track diverter lever > Divert Track West**. Do not toggle it back to Reset Track Switch.
3. Use **wheel brake** to release the cart. The breach requires the west track setting; the cart does not roll just because you moved the lever.
4. Go **west** into Sealed Munitions Stash (`mine_sealed_munitions_vault`). Open **dominion munitions cache** for **Thermite Core Mod, Scrap Metal, and Pure Iron**; inspect **shattered ore cart**. Return **east** to the junction.
5. For the completionist detour, go **north** to Riot Control Post (`mine_checkpoint`). Defeat **Acoustic Bulwark + Dominion Dampener**. Pressure the Guard and deal with the support enemy rather than spending every heavy attack into an intact shield.
6. Go **west** to Toxic Pocket (`mine_gas`), inspect **pipe**, then return **east**, **south** to the junction. This fight is optional; the main route goes east.
7. Go **east** to Conveyor Belt (`mine_conveyor`), inspect **conveyor belt** and **motor**, then **north** to Ore Sifter (`mine_sifter`). Inspect **machinery**, **dust**, and **strobes**.
8. Go **north** to Deep Shoring (`mine_shoring`), inspect **support beams** and **debris**, then **north** to Ancient Threshold (`mine_threshold`). Inspect **geometry** and **walls**.
9. Go **west** to The Long Slope (`mine_slope`), inspect **pillar**, then **north** to Echo Antechamber (`mine_antechamber`). Inspect **ceiling** and **stone**.
10. Go **north** to Geometric Gap (`echo_gap`), inspect **stone** and **line**, then **north** to Silent Walkway (`echo_walkway`). Inspect **walls** and **fractals**.
11. Go **north** to The Heart (`echo_heart`). Do not engage the Fork yet. The Harmonic Well to the east and Signal Alcove to the west may be dark before synchronization; leave their inspections until the Fork has lit them in section 10.
12. Inspect **geometry** in The Heart. Make your pre-Fork save in a dedicated slot. Use the checklist below before continuing.

## 10. Counter-Tune the Fork and follow Red Alert

1. In **The Heart**, select **tuning fork**. The panel is **Counter-Tune the Fork**, with one active control at a time and a visual waveform. Sound is helpful atmosphere, not required for the solution.
2. **Find the pulse:** adjust Phase Sweep until the cutter peaks align with the Fork's repeating peaks. Select **Hold sweep** when enabled. The authored target is **87 kHz**, tolerance **2 kHz**; the control may emphasize visual feedback rather than showing a numeric value.
3. **Balance the cold loop:** adjust the wave height to match the reference. Select **Hold cold loop** when enabled. Target **68%**, tolerance **3 percentage points**.
4. **Quiet the feedback:** turn Ground Phase until the two traces oppose and the lower combined trace becomes flat. Select **Engage handshake** when enabled. Target **180 degrees**, tolerance **6 degrees**.
5. If a confirmation button is disabled, adjust the current control into tolerance; tapping a disabled button cannot advance. Earlier held steps stabilize at their accepted center. Use Previous Step if you need to revisit a stage.
6. Let the sync cinematic and reward processing finish. **The Echo (`w1_mq03`)** completes, **Red Alert (`w1_mq04`)** begins, and the Fork is recovered. Its completion reward is **150 XP, the Tuning Fork, 2 Medkit I, and 2 rations**, plus **Blast Wave**. The calibration consumes the **Functional Cryo-Inductor**; its disappearance here is intentional, not lost loot. Before leaving The Heart, sweep **east** into The Harmonic Well and inspect **mist**, return **west**, then **west** into Signal Alcove and inspect **walls/static**, and return **east**. These rooms are lit by the successful sync. Do not assume the emergency exit is open before the handshake succeeds.
7. Go **north** to Emergency Exit (`echo_exit`). Inspect **maintenance tunnel** and dismiss Zeke's comms narration.
8. Go **north** to Maintenance Access (`launch_access`). Defeat **Siren Skimmer + Echo Borer**; dismiss victory and narrative prompts.
9. Before proceeding north, take the optional room sweep: **east** to Gantry Level 1 (`launch_gantry`), inspect **gantries/drums**, **south** to Refueling Bay (`launch_fuel`), inspect **propellant/hose**, then return **north, west** to Maintenance Access.
10. Go **north** to Cargo Lift (`launch_lift`). Defeat **Acoustic Bulwark + Resonance Buoy**. This is the fight that advances the escape gauntlet stage.
11. **Talk to Jed after clearing the lift.** Finish his sacrifice/handoff conversation. It grants **Jed's Chime (Ghost Signal Cell)**, a Medkit, rations, and **150 XP**; Red Alert completes and **The Launch (`w1_mq05`)** begins.
12. Inspect **override panel** and **hydraulics** for context. Use **cargo lift** rest interaction to recover before the checkpoint. Save in your current-progress slot, keeping the pre-Fork slot intact.

**If the lift stage does not advance:** the Cargo Lift encounter must be cleared, and Jed's conversation must finish. The Chime is a handoff from Jed; it is not obtained by inspecting the override panel. Do not leave without it.

## 11. Defeat the Warden, splice the Chime, and launch

1. From Cargo Lift, go **north** to Security Checkpoint B (`launch_checkpoint`). Defeat **Dominion Dampener + Resonance Buoy**. Inspect **checkpoint** and **floodlights** after the encounter.
2. Heal, then go **north** to Pod Bay (`launch_bay`). The Iron Warden's arrival advances the main quest to its boss stage.
3. Talk to **Zeke before the fight** if you want to check his contextual warning. He should talk about the Warden/Pod Bay, not warn you about the old booth's audio recording. This conversation does not splice the Chime while the Warden is undefeated.
4. Save before committing to the fight if the menu is available. Fight **The Iron Warden**. Cryo Vent's Brittle setup followed by physical attacks and Guard-pressure actions is a useful plan; use the actual action descriptions. Keep healing ready and avoid wasting heavy actions into an intact Guard. Treat numeric damage/HP in older guides as unverified.
5. Finish victory processing. Check the Warden is defeated and the quest now asks for the launch-pod steps.
6. **Talk to Zeke in Pod Bay after victory.** Finish the directions so he sends you into The Pod Core. This is the first conversation of a two-room handoff.
7. Go **north** to **The Pod Core (`launch_pod`)**. **Talk to Zeke again here** and finish the splice conversation. It consumes the Ghost Signal Cell, records the Chime splice, and adds Zeke to the party. Merely moving into the room does not splice it.
8. Inspect **pod** and **speaker** if desired. Use **navigation console** only after the splice. This is the launch action; it is not a second puzzle and talking to Zeke alone does not launch.
9. Watch or skip the launch/crash cinematic, then allow transition processing to finish. You should arrive at **Sector 9 Crash Site (`sector9_crash_site`)**, World 2.
10. Check **The Launch is Completed**, **A Strange Coast (`w2_mq01`) is active**, and Zeke is in the party. Save after arrival. Stop here for the World 1 walkthrough.

**If the console says locked:** return to Pod Bay for Zeke's post-Warden directions if necessary, then talk to him in The Pod Core. Verify the Chime splice objective, not just possession of the Chime. If the world transition stalls, finish/dismiss the cinematic rather than repeatedly selecting the console.

## 12. Exhaustive room reference

Use this section to finish every side inspection, cache, and lore interaction. Connections below are authored map links, not a guarantee that every link is currently unlocked. Gate requirements and action prerequisites are included. A repeated loot interaction should not pay again.


### The Pit - entry: Pit Landing (`pit_L1_landing`)

#### Pit Landing (`pit_L1_landing`)

- **Exits:** North to Lift Shaft (`pit_shaft`); East to Mess Hall (`pit_mess`).
- **bulkhead**: Inspect/select this action. In-room feedback: Standard Dominion seal. It won't open until the shift-change siren sounds.

#### Lift Shaft (`pit_shaft`)

- **Exits:** South to Pit Landing (`pit_L1_landing`); North to Pod Row (`pit_L2_corridor`); West to Jed's Bunk (`pit_jed_bunk`).
- **ladder**: Inspect/select this action. In-room feedback: Rusted and slick with condensation. Watch your step.
- **pipe**: Inspect/select this action. In-room feedback: The steam is hot and smells like old copper.

#### Pod Row (`pit_L2_corridor`)

- **Exits:** South to Lift Shaft (`pit_shaft`); East to Nova's Bunk (`pit_nova_bunk`); West to Supply Closet (`pit_storage`).
- **sleeping pods**: Inspect/select this action. In-room feedback: Most doors carry a name, a shift code, and a repair tally.
- **posters**: Inspect/select this action. In-room feedback: The faces of the 'Founders' are faded and covered in grease stains.

#### Nova's Bunk (`pit_nova_bunk`)

- **Exits:** West to Pod Row (`pit_L2_corridor`).
- **West gate:** Nova needs the bunk light on and the door opened before she can leave. Required state: pit_nova_bunk: light_on = True; pit_nova_bunk: conduit_isolated = True.
- **bunk light**: Toggle: Turn on / Turn off. Leave the relevant light or power on while exploring. In-room feedback: The wall switch is cold under your fingers.
- **door control panel**: Inspect/select this action. Requires: `ms_w1_mq01_fault_visible`. Appears with room state: `light_on`. Hides after room state: `conduit_isolated`. In-room feedback: Turn on the bunk light to see the door controls.
- **bunk**: Rest/recovery interaction; finish or dismiss its prompt before moving.

#### Supply Closet (`pit_storage`)

- **Exits:** East to Pod Row (`pit_L2_corridor`).
- **emergency breaker**: Toggle: Flip switch / Flip switch. Leave the relevant light or power on while exploring.

## 14. Quick recovery: where am I stuck?

- **Cannot leave the Pit:** turn on the bunk light, inspect the door controls, finish Jed's bunk conversation, and return to Pit Landing for the map exit.
- **Scrapper or Doc has no quest:** finish Wake Up Call first.
- **Workshop live test refuses:** craft the inductor, patch Flux Liner, confirm mining cutter, then use live cutter test. Let the cinematic finish.
- **Checkpoint rejects me:** talk to Hank, then choose Grid Instability with Zeke and finish the conversation.
- **Boggs will not authorize me:** complete the registered Security Post shield drill, then return to Boggs and finish the assignment conversation.
- **Cannot return to the map:** walk to the node's designated entrance listed in section 12.
- **Server terminal does not award Circuit Board:** terminal to discover quest, console to thaw, output ports to read warning, terminal again, then removable-board choice.
- **Mine rooms are dark / datapad missing:** turn on Cavern Junction power, then re-enter Side Shunt 4.
- **Cart does not breach the vault:** divert track west, then release wheel brake. Leave the diverter west until the breach occurs.
- **Fork confirmation disabled:** align the active waveform/control; hold each earlier stage before the next; final cancellation is opposite phase.
- **Emergency exit refuses:** complete the Fork handshake and its sync cinematic first.
- **No Chime at launch:** clear Cargo Lift and finish Jed's handoff.
- **Pod console locked after Warden:** Zeke in Pod Bay first, then Zeke in The Pod Core; console last.
