# World 2: Complete Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Reviewed October 2, 2026 against version 1.3.84 (168). Full spoilers. Exact route, all five main quests, all five side quests, every node room, puzzle solutions, recovery instructions and optional content. Repository verified; device playtesting remains necessary.


## 1. How to use this guide

Start at the World 1 crash transition, or a save at Crash Site with A Strange Coast active. A later debug scenario can already complete tasks and stock equipment; use a campaign save to evaluate ordinary progression and economy.

1. Make a manual starting save and keep it. Use a second slot for rolling progress. Make a separate predeparture save before leaving Sector 9. Do not overwrite your only recovery point with the next chapter.

2. Follow numbered instructions in order. Bold names identify actual rooms and actions. IDs in backticks are for reporting/recovery, not input. Each route arrow is a separate directional tap. Clear entry encounters before using room actions.

3. For companion conversations, use the character conversation control in exploration and finish the dialogue. Repeat until the stated offer or turn-in appears. Accepting a side quest changes tracking; select the main quest again if you want its journal guidance.

4. Reserve the three quest recipes: damaged cutter + wire + scrap; herb + meat; transmitter core + wire + scrap. Learn each found blueprint before crafting. Keep the repaired Thermal Cutter for shortcuts and preserve the Chime and Bridge until ship installation.

5. Visible actions can still have event prerequisites. An object description is not a loot promise. The appendix distinguishes containers, dialogue, puzzles and direct rewards. Random battle rewards and arrival levels are not fixed benchmarks.

6. Use normal campaign gear and disable OP/instant victory for combat balance feedback. If you use those controls to check flow, record that separately. Source review and fast victories cannot establish that the Beast is fun or fair.

**Check before moving on:** You have a starting save, know your rolling slot, and are at Crash Site with the opening quest active.


## 2. Crash Site: Zeke, the Chime and emergency supplies

Main quest: A Strange Coast. The emergency medkit action is inside Escape Pod Interior. Botanist becomes available only after this main quest completes.

7. At **Crash Site**, talk to **Zeke** and finish the crash conversation. Its last line credits Check on Zeke and establishes his party membership. Read the navigation-pair explanation before inspecting the console.

8. At **Crash Site**, inspect **navigation console**. Recover Chime (`ghost_signal_cell`), Medkit, Ration Pack, Wooden Rod and Basic Lure. This ordinary campaign kit enables later fishing.

9. From **Crash Site**, go **north** to **Escape Pod Interior** (`sector9_pod_interior`).

10. At **Escape Pod Interior**, use **emergency medkit** to stabilize Zeke. It consumes the opening Medkit and credits stabilization. Inspect **locker** for context; its description does not grant a second emergency kit.

11. At **Escape Pod Interior**, inspect **waterlogged survival console** for optional arcade discovery. Record cabinet status separately; restoration is not required for the World 2 main quest.

12. From **Escape Pod Interior**, go **south** to **Crash Site** (`sector9_crash_site`).

13. At **Crash Site**, read **wreckage** and **glow-water**. Leave scan moss for after Botanist starts; its event requires that active side quest.

14. From **Crash Site**, go **west** to **Overgrown Glade** (`sector9_landing_glade`).

15. At **Overgrown Glade**, harvest **luminous mushrooms** for Herb, then inspect **broadleaf canopy** and **mossy hollow**. Use **Charlie -> Interact** for his optional conversation.

16. From **Overgrown Glade**, go **north** to **Security Perimeter** (`sector9_landing_perimeter`).

17. At **Security Perimeter**, inspect **drone** and **scanner lens**. This is a dead end; return through Overgrown Glade.

18. From **Security Perimeter**, go **south** to **Overgrown Glade** (`sector9_landing_glade`) -> **east** to **Crash Site** (`sector9_crash_site`) -> **east** to **Stream Bank** (`sector9_landing_stream`).

19. At **Stream Bank**, let the entry event complete **A Strange Coast** for **150 event XP**, start **The Signal**, and reveal Tideglass Beach and Razor-Vine Path. Open **river silt cache** for Herbal Poultice and Scrap Metal. Read **glow-water current** and **smooth pebbles**.

20. At **Stream Bank**, talk to **Zeke** again until he offers **Botanist**. Accept **Sure, I'll scan them.** If the offer is absent, finish any earlier conversation and verify MQ01 is complete.

21. From **Stream Bank**, go **west** to **Crash Site** (`sector9_crash_site`).

22. At **Crash Site**, use **scan moss** with Botanist active. Confirm the moss objective is checked.

23. From **Crash Site**, go **east** to **Stream Bank** (`sector9_landing_stream`) -> **south** to **Scorched Brush** (`sector9_landing_brush`).

24. At **Scorched Brush**, harvest **charred starleaf** for Herb and read **muddy clearing**.

25. From **Scorched Brush**, go **east** to **Pod Fragment** (`sector9_landing_drop`).

26. At **Pod Fragment**, inspect **cargo panel**. Note **salvage conduits** for the late Astra repair, but collection needs Liftoff and Astra inspection. Merely finding this room now does not collect them.

27. From **Pod Fragment**, go **west** to **Scorched Brush** (`sector9_landing_brush`) -> **north** to **Stream Bank** (`sector9_landing_stream`).

**Check before moving on:** MQ01 complete, The Signal and Botanist active, Zeke stabilized, moss scanned, and Chime/rod/lure recovered. If stream completion failed, finish the three opening tasks and re-enter Stream Bank.


## 3. Stream, submerged vault and first camp

Explore the coast before the temple. Tideglass Day is a later Orion quest; visiting now does not credit its future beach-entry task. Ordinary food pickups can be gathered now.

28. From **Stream Bank**, go **east** to **Mist Pools** (`sector9_stream_pools`).

29. At **Mist Pools**, clear the Siren Skimmer encounter, harvest **reed bulbs**, and read **still basins**. Reeds are at Mist Pools, not Resonant Sedge.

30. From **Mist Pools**, go **south** to **Resonant Sedge** (`sector9_stream_wetlands`).

31. At **Resonant Sedge**, clear Spore-Spitter if encountered, read **spore warning** and **wind-sung reeds**, and open **submerged emergency locker** for Antidote Flask and Pure Iron.

32. From **Resonant Sedge**, go **north** to **Mist Pools** (`sector9_stream_pools`) -> **east** to **Crystalline Flats** (`sector9_stream_flats`).

33. At **Crystalline Flats**, read **half-sunken causeway** and **crystalline sand**, then take the north cave detour.

34. From **Crystalline Flats**, go **north** to **Cavern Entrance** (`sector9_stream_cave_entrance`).

35. At **Cavern Entrance**, read **cave glyphs** and **waterfall curtain**.

36. From **Cavern Entrance**, go **north** to **Glow-Moss Cavern** (`sector9_stream_cave_depths`).

37. At **Glow-Moss Cavern**, clear Echo-Borer, harvest **echo nest** for Beast Meat, read **glowing moss**, and interact with **waterproof cargo case** for VHS discovery. Check its collectible feedback rather than expecting food.

38. From **Glow-Moss Cavern**, go **east** to **Waterlogged Tunnel** (`sector9_stream_sunken_passage`).

39. At **Waterlogged Tunnel**, read **drowned console** and **warm current**. Use **ancient sluice wheel** / **Turn Sluice Wheel** to drain the passage. The south gate checks current room state, not simply whether it was drained once in the past.

40. From **Waterlogged Tunnel**, go **south** to **Submerged Aethel Vault** (`sector9_sunken_aethel_vault`) after the water drains.

41. At **Submerged Aethel Vault**, open **precursor reliquary** for Tidal Resonator Mod, Astral Thread and Bioluminescent Lure. Inspect **living coral**. This equipment detour is optional.

42. From **Submerged Aethel Vault**, go **north** to **Waterlogged Tunnel** (`sector9_stream_sunken_passage`).

43. At **Waterlogged Tunnel**, leave the sluice engaged for revisits. If testing its release, the passage should flood and the south entrance block again; turn it back afterward.

44. From **Waterlogged Tunnel**, go **west** to **Glow-Moss Cavern** (`sector9_stream_cave_depths`) -> **south** to **Cavern Entrance** (`sector9_stream_cave_entrance`) -> **south** to **Crystalline Flats** (`sector9_stream_flats`) -> **east** to **Resonant Falls** (`sector9_stream_falls`).

45. At **Resonant Falls**, use **recess shelter** to rest and finish its camp sequence. **Sheltered cookfire** provides cooking, **provisions counter** opens the shop, and **penny camp bed** is an optional inspection. Talk to **David** and use **Penny -> Greet** for their camp conversations. Check actual party recovery rather than assuming every campfire heals.

46. At **Resonant Falls**, open the cooking source to discover regional recipes if evaluating cooking. Cook one affordable meal if desired, while preserving quest materials. Eating outside combat and using a meal in combat have different effects; keep observations separate.

47. Save at Resonant Falls. Load once if checking persistence, and confirm party, completed opening quest, moss scan, containers and fishing gear survive. Return to this same point before continuing.

**Check before moving on:** Vault and stream rooms explored, first camp visited, supplies recovered, cooking access checked. No required level or credit budget is assumed.


## 4. Tideglass Beach: fishing and the prism

Botanist weeds are at actual Tide Pools. The prism grants Bioluminescent Focus, not the Focus Conduit from Ancient Echoes. Fishing is optional for main progression.

48. From **Resonant Falls**, go **north** to **Beach Transition** (`sector9_stream_tidepools`).

49. At **Beach Transition**, harvest **tidepool crawler** for Beast Meat and inspect **star crystals**.

50. From **Beach Transition**, go **east** to **Coastal Resilience Station** (`sector9_coastal_research_station`).

51. At **Coastal Resilience Station**, inspect **wetland telemetry array**, **forensic research slate** and **solar coffee press**. Use **Megan -> Consult** and finish her local conversation. This is optional environmental writing.

52. From **Coastal Resilience Station**, go **south** to **Beach Cliffs** (`sector9_beach_cliffs`).

53. At **Beach Cliffs**, read **signal reflection**, **basalt cliffs**, **driftwood marker** and **weathered elephant toy**. The patrol reflection does not replace alpha, beta or gamma.

54. From **Beach Cliffs**, go **north** to **Coastal Resilience Station** (`sector9_coastal_research_station`) -> **north** to **Sandy Cove** (`sector9_beach_cove`).

55. At **Sandy Cove**, read **warm sand** and open **campfire** for cooking if desired. Talk to **Scout Kaelen** and finish his optional beach conversation. Cooking-source access is separate from a named rest event.

56. From **Sandy Cove**, go **west** to **Tide Pools** (`sector9_beach_pools`).

57. At **Tide Pools**, use **scan weeds** for Botanist; read **pools** and **luminescent anemones**. Leave **gather tideglass** until Orion gives its quest.

58. At **Tide Pools**, use **tide fishing**, set up the Wooden Rod and Basic Lure as its interface requires, and complete a catch. Follow fight feedback: ease reeling when strain rises, then resume when the fish settles. Confirm catch inventory and journal discovery. The vault lure is an optional upgrade.

59. At **Tide Pools**, return for missing native species if pursuing the whole fishing journal. Follow the displayed journal milestones and claim its actual rewards. Random species are not guaranteed on a particular cast or required for World 2 main-quest completion.

60. From **Tide Pools**, go **west** to **Beached Cargo Wreck** (`sector9_beach_cargo`).

61. At **Beached Cargo Wreck**, open **crates** for Scrap Metal, Composite Plate and Hydraulic Fluid; read **rusted hull**.

62. From **Beached Cargo Wreck**, go **north** to **Star-Sand Dunes** (`sector9_beach_dunes`).

63. At **Star-Sand Dunes**, harvest **burrowing grazer** for Beast Meat and read **wind-sculpted ridges**.

64. From **Star-Sand Dunes**, go **south** to **Beached Cargo Wreck** (`sector9_beach_cargo`) -> **east** to **Tide Pools** (`sector9_beach_pools`) -> **north** to **Sea Cavern** (`sector9_beach_cave`).

65. At **Sea Cavern**, read **mineral shelter** and open **tide-worn chest** for Bioluminescent Lure, Tideglass Delight and Burn Salve. Receiving that prepared meal does not complete Tideglass Day.

66. From **Sea Cavern**, go **north** to **Sunken Obelisk** (`sector9_beach_pillar`).

67. At **Sunken Obelisk**, read **resonant pillar**, then return south to Sea Cavern.

68. From **Sunken Obelisk**, go **south** to **Sea Cavern** (`sector9_beach_cave`) -> **east** to **Hidden Grotto** (`sector9_beach_grotto`).

69. At **Hidden Grotto**, read **sea-worn arches** and **cache**. The cache explains that Source Resin comes from living materials, not a finished relic on a pedestal.

70. At **Hidden Grotto**, open **bioluminescent prism**. Aim the beam into the receiver, tighten the colored fringe, then balance glow until the receiver is steady green. The current beam presentation does not show numeric target answers; judge visual clue quality first.

71. At **Hidden Grotto**, reference solution: **angle 135 degrees +/-5; lux 64% +/-4; frequency 92 kHz +/-3**. Submit when ready. Success grants **Bioluminescent Focus** (`relic_mod_biolum_focus`); the current event grants neither Focus Conduit nor an extra 150 XP.

72. At **Hidden Grotto**, if you cancel/reopen, local controls reset and you align again. A failed submission should describe beam/fringe/brightness trouble without a reward. After success check one mod award and solved state.

73. From **Hidden Grotto**, go **south** to **Waterlogged Tunnel** (`sector9_stream_sunken_passage`) -> **west** to **Glow-Moss Cavern** (`sector9_stream_cave_depths`) -> **south** to **Cavern Entrance** (`sector9_stream_cave_entrance`) -> **south** to **Crystalline Flats** (`sector9_stream_flats`) -> **west** to **Mist Pools** (`sector9_stream_pools`) -> **west** to **Stream Bank** (`sector9_landing_stream`) -> **west** to **Crash Site** (`sector9_crash_site`).

**Check before moving on:** Coast sweep complete, moss and weeds scanned, fishing access checked, prism solved. Tideglass Day waits for Orion. Sanctuary entry later has physical return links; it is not itself permanent departure.


## 5. Razor-Vine Path and Lost Patrol

Both fern and spore scans are in Resonant Canopy. Reach gamma before recovering the patrol cache. The outer loop avoids locked cutter shortcuts until you repair the tool.

74. From **Crash Site**, go **south** to **Resonant Canopy** (`sector9_canopy`).

75. At **Resonant Canopy**, clear Stalker-Vine. This can credit The Signal canopy-clear task and reveal ridge/temple nodes. Use **Scan ferns**, **Scan spores**, read **ferns**, and use **Scavenge remains** to start/advance Lost Patrol alpha.

76. From **Resonant Canopy**, go **south** to **Spore Thickets** (`sector9_wilds_thickets`).

77. At **Spore Thickets**, clear Razor-Vine and use **distress signature alpha** if its journal task remains missing. The same trace also exists at the canopy remains.

78. From **Spore Thickets**, go **east** to **Hound Nest** (`sector9_wilds_nest`).

79. At **Hound Nest**, clear Shard-Hound if encountered, harvest **shard-hound remains**, and open **trampled supply pack** for Beast Meat, Wiring Bundle and Energy Core.

80. From **Hound Nest**, go **west** to **Spore Thickets** (`sector9_wilds_thickets`) -> **south** to **Poison Hollow** (`sector9_wilds_hollow`).

81. At **Poison Hollow**, use **distress signature beta** and inspect **ancient stump**.

82. From **Poison Hollow**, go **south** to **Patrol Graveyard** (`sector9_wilds_grave`).

83. At **Patrol Graveyard**, inspect **transceiver** for context. Its event aliases beta; final cutter recovery is at Burned Glade, not this room.

84. From **Patrol Graveyard**, go **south** to **Canopy Edge** (`sector9_wilds_overlook`).

85. At **Canopy Edge**, read **canopy route** and **canopy vista**.

86. From **Canopy Edge**, go **east** to **Hanging Root Bridge** (`sector9_wilds_canopy_walk`).

87. At **Hanging Root Bridge**, read **razor-vine shortcut**. East needs repaired Thermal Cutter. Use the south outer loop now.

88. From **Hanging Root Bridge**, go **south** to **Architect Gateway** (`sector9_wilds_archway`).

89. At **Architect Gateway**, clear Shard-Hound and read **Aethel boundary stones**.

90. From **Architect Gateway**, go **east** to **Beast Run** (`sector9_wilds_dense_brush`).

91. At **Beast Run**, read **toxic bloom** and open **lost surveyor pack** for Antidote Flask, Medkit Plus and Ration Pack.

92. From **Beast Run**, go **north** to **Burned Glade** (`sector9_wilds_clearance`).

93. At **Burned Glade**, read **shattered trunks**. Note **patrol transceiver**, which needs gamma before recovery. You will return.

94. From **Burned Glade**, go **north** to **Hunter's Blind** (`sector9_wilds_lookout`).

95. At **Hunter's Blind**, use **ridge campsite** for rest and **heat ring cookfire** for cooking. West is still blocked; retrace south through Burned Glade rather than forcing it.

96. From **Hunter's Blind**, go **south** to **Burned Glade** (`sector9_wilds_clearance`) -> **south** to **Beast Run** (`sector9_wilds_dense_brush`) -> **west** to **Architect Gateway** (`sector9_wilds_archway`) -> **north** to **Hanging Root Bridge** (`sector9_wilds_canopy_walk`) -> **north** to **Vine Climb** (`sector9_ridge_climb`).

97. At **Vine Climb**, read **medic satchel**. It gives stabilizer lore, not a promised Medkit drop.

98. From **Vine Climb**, go **north** to **Windy Ledges** (`sector9_ridge_ledges`).

99. At **Windy Ledges**, clear Echo-Borer and harvest **blue-frond cluster** for Herb.

100. From **Windy Ledges**, go **north** to **Beast Lair Approach** (`sector9_ridge_overhang`).

101. At **Beast Lair Approach**, read **weathered mural**.

102. From **Beast Lair Approach**, go **north** to **Canopy Ridge** (`sector9_canopy_ridge`).

103. At **Canopy Ridge**, use **Look at Shield**. This is scouting before Sleeping Giant completes; Confront stalker and the Beast come later. The boss is not an automatic entry encounter.

104. From **Canopy Ridge**, go **east** to **Shield Viewpoint** (`sector9_ridge_viewpoint`).

105. At **Shield Viewpoint**, read **facility sightline**.

106. From **Shield Viewpoint**, go **north** to **Sniper Lookout** (`sector9_ridge_sniper_perch`).

107. At **Sniper Lookout**, use **distress signature gamma**. Verify all three patrol traces before doubling back.

108. From **Sniper Lookout**, go **south** to **Shield Viewpoint** (`sector9_ridge_viewpoint`) -> **west** to **Canopy Ridge** (`sector9_canopy_ridge`) -> **south** to **Beast Lair Approach** (`sector9_ridge_overhang`) -> **south** to **Windy Ledges** (`sector9_ridge_ledges`) -> **south** to **Vine Climb** (`sector9_ridge_climb`) -> **south** to **Hanging Root Bridge** (`sector9_wilds_canopy_walk`) -> **south** to **Architect Gateway** (`sector9_wilds_archway`) -> **east** to **Beast Run** (`sector9_wilds_dense_brush`) -> **north** to **Burned Glade** (`sector9_wilds_clearance`).

109. At **Burned Glade**, use **patrol transceiver**. Receive Damaged Thermal Cutter, its schematic, Wiring Bundle, Scrap Metal and Corrosive Rounds mod. The supplied parts cover the repair; reserve them.

110. At **Burned Glade**, open **Menu -> Tinkering**, learn the found Thermal Cutter schematic (or use the schematic in Items), and craft **Thermal Cutter** from **Damaged Thermal Cutter x1 + Wiring Bundle x1 + Scrap Metal x1**. Confirm Lost Patrol completes for **100 event XP**. Owning an old cutter does not replace its craft task.

111. From **Burned Glade**, go **north** to **Hunter's Blind** (`sector9_wilds_lookout`).

112. At **Hunter's Blind**, rest if needed, then take **west** through the previously blocked vines. Confirm the repaired cutter is retained.

113. From **Hunter's Blind**, go **west** to **Hanging Root Bridge** (`sector9_wilds_canopy_walk`) -> **north** to **Vine Climb** (`sector9_ridge_climb`) -> **north** to **Windy Ledges** (`sector9_ridge_ledges`) -> **north** to **Beast Lair Approach** (`sector9_ridge_overhang`) -> **north** to **Canopy Ridge** (`sector9_canopy_ridge`) -> **east** to **Shield Viewpoint** (`sector9_ridge_viewpoint`) -> **north** to **Sniper Lookout** (`sector9_ridge_sniper_perch`) -> **east** to **High Pass** (`sector9_ridge_crevice`).

114. At **High Pass**, inspect **razor-vine cache** and **fault line** at High Pass. Descriptive cache feedback is not a separate item grant.

115. From **High Pass**, go **east** to **Sanctuary Outlook** (`sector9_ridge_plateau`).

116. At **Sanctuary Outlook**, use **plateau camp** for rest, **stone cookfire** for cooking, and **ridge overlook cache** for VHS discovery.

**Check before moving on:** Lost Patrol complete and cutter retained; The Signal canopy-clear task done. Botanist moss, weeds, ferns and spores done; roots remain. Ridge scouting is not the Hunter quest completion.


## 6. Temple Gate and Sanctuary entry

122. At **Resonator Chamber**, talk to Sentinel-3 / open **Sentinel Scraps** for optional supplies. Check actual prices and retain materials rather than purchasing to meet speculative budget numbers.

123. From **Resonator Chamber**, go **north** to **Left Tuner Column** (`sector9_temple_left_wing`).

124. At **Left Tuner Column**, inspect **western resonance crystal** for context. Ancient Echoes is not yet active, so it cannot credit that quest.

126. At **Right Tuner Column**, inspect **eastern resonance crystal** for context. Note both wing locations for later.

127. From **Right Tuner Column**, go **north** to **Resonator Chamber** (`sector9_temple_lock_chamber`) -> **east** to **Temple Gate** (`sector9_temple_gate`).

128. At **Temple Gate**, use **stone lock** after Clear canopy path and Locate Temple Gate are checked. The Signal completes for **200 event XP**, Sleeping Giant starts, and you warp to Hall of Echoes.

**Check before moving on:** MQ02 complete, Sleeping Giant active, current room Hall of Echoes. Keep the Chime for later authentication. Gate opening is not Astra departure.


## 7. Sleeping Giant: four clues, three decisions, Orion and the Bridge

Waking the pod, talking to Orion and recovering the separate Bridge are three different progression boundaries. Finish them all before his optional errands.

129. At **Hall of Echoes**, inspect **murals** for the memory/pressure/return clue. **Central relief** is the later optional chorus puzzle, not the stasis wake control.

130. From **Hall of Echoes**, go **south** to **Stasis Chamber** (`sector9_stasis_chamber`).

131. At **Stasis Chamber**, inspect **stasis pod** for the pressure-shell clue and **root lattice** for Botanist's fifth scan. Talk to **Zeke** until his completion line awards **100 event XP + Painkillers x2**. Scanning five alone is not the turn-in.

132. From **Stasis Chamber**, go **east** to **Ring Array** (`sector9_stasis_ring_array`).

133. At **Ring Array**, leave **ring console** until the remaining overview and coolant clues are read.

134. From **Ring Array**, go **north** to **Observation Deck** (`sector9_stasis_observation`).

135. At **Observation Deck**, read **stasis mural overview**. Note the three sockets and separate memory bands.

136. From **Observation Deck**, go **south** to **Ring Array** (`sector9_stasis_ring_array`) -> **south** to **Coolant Lines** (`sector9_stasis_coolant_lines`).

137. At **Coolant Lines**, use **coolant garden** to stabilize the flow.

138. From **Coolant Lines**, go **north** to **Ring Array** (`sector9_stasis_ring_array`).

139. At **Ring Array**, use **ring console** with all four clues complete. Advance the console explanation to the first choice.

140. At **Ring Array**, choose **Divert pressure around the memory shell.** Pressure goes around Orion, not through his memories.

141. At **Ring Array**, choose **Keep each identity on its own band.** Do not merge the voices into a stronger signal.

142. At **Ring Array**, choose **Reconnect return after the outer bypass settles.** This wakes Orion and credits ring alignment. Wrong choices explain the unsafe route and retry; Step away lets you reopen without completing early.

143. From **Ring Array**, go **west** to **Stasis Chamber** (`sector9_stasis_chamber`).

144. At **Stasis Chamber**, talk to **Orion**, finishing the whole awakening exchange and Nova/Zeke responses. His final invitation recruits him, credits Talk to Orion and reveals **sealed Bridge cradle**.

145. At **Stasis Chamber**, open **sealed Bridge cradle**. Receive **Bridge Echo** (`bridge_relic`); Sleeping Giant completes for **250 event XP** and The Hunter starts. Check inventory.

146. At **Stasis Chamber**, talk to **Orion** again and accept **Tideglass Day** with **Let's go.** Repeat and accept **Ancient Echoes** with **I can help with that.** Both require MQ03 complete; finish any higher-priority conversation first.

147. Save here. Confirm Orion in the party, Bridge in inventory, Botanist complete, and both Orion side quests active. This is a useful checkpoint before optional sweeps and Beast.

**Check before moving on:** Orion awake AND recruited; separate Bridge recovered; MQ03 complete; Hunter active; Botanist complete; Tideglass Day and Ancient Echoes accepted.
