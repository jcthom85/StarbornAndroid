# World 6 — The Source: complete step-by-step walkthrough

Rebuilt November 12, 2026 against the current authored room connections, actions, quest requirements and tuning settings. Full ending spoilers. This ordered route covers all 65 rooms, all five main quests, all five side quests, branch caches, the final Great Frontier film, arcade discovery, the ending and the postgame shore. Source-checked; the newly ordered itinerary still needs a full phone playthrough.

## 1. How to use this guide

Start at **The Campfire** (`source_campfire`) after World 5’s reality breach, with **Fractured Minds** active. Follow numbered steps in order. Bold text names actual rooms and controls; IDs are for reporting, not input. Each arrow is one NSEW swipe. Finish dialogue, feedback and cinematics before expecting subsequent tasks. Some controls remain gray for completed inspections.

Keep an arrival save, a rolling save, a separate HR Record choice save and a pre-ending save. The five side quests are **Jed’s Echo, The HR Record, Elara’s Song, The Aethel Grave, The Final Scavenge**. Both HR endings grant Unshackled; only the worker-broadcast ending records the worker release. One ending completes the quest. Use separate saves to compare them.

### Builds, recovery and navigation

Rest and Cook use dedicated service pills at The Campfire; **Shared Memory Span** later provides Rest through **final meal**. Spend earned AP and check equipment and consumables between major encounters. Use actual combat for balance testing. This route does not assume an arrival level or fixed number of healing items.

Every room has NSEW walking returns. Named memory shortcuts are optional conveniences. Four forward story locks require the crew anchors, the shared promise, all three refusals, and the memory-defense victory; none requires replaying those objectives on the return. The New World stays locked until the ending completes. Reassembly, the mine elevator, the singularity action and the ending move the party through scripted transitions; the route explicitly accounts for these moves.

Read optional inspections as you pass. Descriptive memories and passive details are not extra quest tasks or guaranteed loot. A branch patrol can share an enemy type with a required encounter; check the Journal receipt rather than assuming that every duplicate enemy must be fought.
