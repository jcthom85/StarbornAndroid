# World 3 — Integrated completionist route (separate edition)

November 6, 2026. Full spoilers. Written for release **1.3.110 (194)** and the World 3 design additions preceding it.

This is a separate walkthrough. The existing **World 3 completionist playtest guide** remains unchanged, with its original numbered route and browser checklist. You do not need to restart your game or abandon that guide. This edition interleaves all five main quests and six side quests, nearby discoveries and final checks into one itinerary.

## Already halfway through? Join here

Keep using the original guide if that is easiest. Use this edition only for the missing side-quest directions, then return to your existing numbered step. Its browser reader has independent checkmarks; it does not import or clear your original progress.

Check **Journal** before choosing a starting point. A completed quest stays completed: skip its actions and rewards rather than trying to replay it. Compare the task names if a quest is partly complete. Keep your current save and make a second manual save before any catch-up trip.

- **Before the planning-table departure (original steps 6–18):** continue your heist preparation. If you missed Neon Fix or Night Market Run, use “Lower City favors and heist preparation” below. Join “Upper City access and cover story” when ready to depart.
- **Upstairs, before the Lens (original steps 19–33):** finish guest access and the scanner/chute objectives if still pending. Then use “Upper City favors: collect the core and case number,” “One trip downstairs: finish Neon Fix and Cold Case,” “Corporate Espionage,” and “Murder in the Skyline Salon.” Return to the Lens chapter afterward. If Night Market Run is unfinished, finish it during the downstairs trip.
- **Lens already taken (original steps 34–45):** keep a predeparture save. Use the city lift return described below to finish outstanding market/core/case quests, and finish the ledger, murder and prototype objectives before launching. Lockdown does not itself complete or erase those quests. Clear actual blocking encounters and heal as needed; this is a catch-up option, not a requirement to repeat the main story.
- **Already holding the Neon Sign Core:** skip the Sentinel collection and turn it in to Jax.
- **Already chose a ledger outcome:** keep that outcome. Publication and private leverage are mutually exclusive; you do not need both for a completionist run.

### City-level return from an existing save

From **Laundry Service**, go **south** to **Uniform Sorting**, then **east** to **Service Lift**. Its **lift panel** descends to **Transit Plaza** after The Plan. From Transit Plaza, **north** reaches The Static/Jax; **south** reaches Security Kiosk. Return to Transit Plaza and use **elevator controls** to ride back to Laundry Service. These are repeatable physical lift trips, not regional-map travel. Your quest progress is preserved.

## How to follow this route

Start the ordered steps below at Sewer Landing Pad on a new World 3 arrival, or join the chapter matching your Journal. Complete dialogue before expecting quest credit. The fixed **Leave** button cancels a conversation without executing its unfinished final-line reward; reopen and finish it when ready. Inspect gray clues again when you need their information.

NSEW instructions are swipes. Named stairs and inspection-lift buttons are optional bidirectional shortcuts; this itinerary uses the walking network so the directions remain unambiguous. The cleared city lift and story departures use their named controls. No diagonal exits are used.

Take rest and shop opportunities according to your actual build. No level, credit balance or prescribed number of purchased Medkits is assumed. Keep a separate save before the Lens and before departure. Side quests are optional to finish the story but included here for a completionist playthrough.

The numbered route covers every quest. The room sweep at the end covers the rest of the world's 70 rooms; those detours are for full exploration and are not extra story prerequisites.


## Arrival: Under the Radar

1. At **Sewer Landing Pad**, let the World 3 arrival finish. Confirm **Under the Radar** is active; keep your World 2 arrival save.

2. From **Sewer Landing Pad**, go **north** to **Pump Gallery** (`spire_sewers_scale_01`).

3. Defeat the **Sewer Crawler** encounter at **Pump Gallery**. Other enemy parties are separate encounters; heal and clear victory notices before continuing.

4. From **Pump Gallery**, go **south** to **Sewer Landing Pad** (`spire_sewers_landing`) → **east** to **Runoff Lock** (`spire_runoff_lock`) → **east** to **Sewer Passage** (`spire_sewers_passage`) → **north** to **Vent Output** (`spire_vent_output`).

5. From **Vent Output**, go **east** to **Underrail Platform** (`spire_underrail_platform`) → **east** to **The Static** (`spire_the_static`).

6. Talk to **Jax** at **The Static**. Finish his introduction and shelter offer. This establishes the borrowed apartment; it does not collect the later heist schedules.

7. From **The Static**, go **north** to **Signal Stairwell** (`spire_signal_stairwell`) → **north** to **Static Safehouse** (`spire_zekes_apartment`).

8. Talk to **Zeke** at **Static Safehouse**. Finish the wiring/shield conversation through Orion’s final line. Confirm **Under the Radar** complete and **The Plan** active.

From **Night Market**, go **west** to **Checkpoint Queue** (`spire_checkpoint_queue`) → **west** to **Transit Plaza** (`spire_transit_plaza`) → **north** to **The Static** (`spire_the_static`) → **north** to **Signal Stairwell** (`spire_signal_stairwell`) → **north** to **Static Safehouse** (`spire_zekes_apartment`) → **north** to **Safehouse Roof** (`spire_safehouse_roof`).

Identify **his badge entering the Syndicate Booth at 21:14**, then connect **coolant in the glass with the missing core in his comms rig**. Wrong arguments receive rebuttals and permit retry or leaving. Finish his confession.

90. At **Landing Pad Roof**, use **shield window**. Use the Lens/Scan timing to identify the local security-curtain opening. This is not the removal of the planetary Shield.


## Completion checkpoint: stop before launch

91. Check Journal for all six completed side quests: **Neon Fix, Cold Case, Night Market Run, Corporate Espionage, Prototype Testing, Murder in the Skyline Salon**. Confirm **Under the Radar, The Plan, Social Engineering, The Lens** complete, with **Burn Notice** awaiting departure. Skip already-completed objectives; use the earlier chapter for any missing quest.

92. If pursuing every room and collectible, finish the room sweep below before leaving. Check fishing/arcade/optional-puzzle discoveries against their actual journals and inventory. You do not need both ledger branches. Save in a separate slot and retain it after departure.

93. At **Landing Pad Roof**, use **launch window**. Depart only after the completion checkpoint. Finish the escape sequence and confirm **Burn Notice** completes on arrival in World 4. The Foundry is below the outer Shield; high-orbit hardware is still missing.

## Full room sweep and reference

Use this reference during the exploration stops above. Each node heading groups nearby rooms. Enter its first room using the named route/map location, follow the listed NSEW exits into each unvisited branch, and retrace the opposite direction. Every local branch has a walking return. Check the room name and your current Journal stage before using an action: some controls retire after completion, while clues remain readable. Named stairs/inspection lifts are optional alternatives.

Read ordinary inspections, collect available caches/VHS tapes, sample resident conversations, and use the available cooking/rest/shop services according to your needs. A named action is not automatically an unfinished quest objective. The prototype replay and open stalls appear only after their results exist; receipt reading never awards a second payout. The black-site lab requires its existing laser-console access, so operate the authored bypass rather than treating the north doorway as freely open. Optional tuning puzzles are extra discoveries, not the Lens’s containment sequence.

### Vent Output

#### Vent Output (`spire_vent_output`)

The vent output coughs the crew into acid rain and neon exhaust. [action:trash pile|Trash piles] clog the curb, and Rain Gutter Alley vanishes west between buildings that lean like they are tired.

Exits: south → Sewer Passage, west → Rain Gutter Alley, east → Underrail Platform, north → Condensate Run.

Interactions: trash pile.

#### Rain Gutter Alley (`spire_rain_gutter_alley`)

Rainwater sheets through Rain Gutter Alley in oily ribbons. A patrol mirror hangs above a cracked stairwell, giving the alley eyes even when no guard is present.

Exits: east → Vent Output.

Interactions: patrol mirror, cracked stairwell.

#### Underrail Platform (`spire_underrail_platform`)

An abandoned underrail platform trembles whenever the city trains pass overhead. The underrail map is scratched with routes, curfews, patrol timing, and warnings written in marker. Platform stairs descend to Transit Plaza and remain open for the climb back.

Exits: west → Vent Output, east → The Static.

Named travel control: **Take the platform stairs to Transit Plaza** (down → Transit Plaza). Works in both directions.

Interactions: underrail map, patrol timing.

#### Condensate Run (`spire_vent_output_scale_01`)

A condensate run channels warm rainwater down the back of the Vent Output. The scan drip under the pipe maps footsteps from the street above, while the rust initials prove lower-city kids have used this route for years.

Exits: south → Vent Output, north → Maintenance Crawl.

Interactions: condensate run, scan drip.

#### Maintenance Crawl (`spire_vent_output_scale_02`)

A maintenance crawl squeezes between coolant pipes and old warning paint. The old stencil says unauthorized workers become infrastructure, and the pipe gap is just wide enough for a desperate crew.

Exits: south → Condensate Run, north → Rain Grate Overlook.

Interactions: maintenance crawl, old stencil.

#### Rain Grate Overlook (`spire_vent_output_scale_03`)

A rain grate opens above the lower street where upper runoff falls clean before hitting rust. The upper runoff and patrol reflection make the route feel watched from both directions. Tucked behind the grating sits a hidden smuggler stash. An optional return chute leads directly back to the entrance. The room corridor also remains open for walking back.

Exits: south → Maintenance Crawl.

Interactions: rain grate, upper runoff, smuggler stash, chute.

### The Static

#### The Static (`spire_the_static`)

The Static is a dark dive bar built around an old subway car. Neon signs pulse erratically, the sign outside keeps failing, and the signal stairwell behind the bar hums with pirate broadcasts. The subway car lies east through the back-bar passage; the signal stairwell remains north.

Exits: west → Underrail Platform, east → Back Bar Ledger, north → Signal Stairwell, south → Transit Plaza.

Interactions: sign.

#### Old Subway Car (`spire_old_subway_car`)

The Static spills into an old subway car bolted beside the bar. Torn booth seats, stickered windows, and a silent jukebox make the car half studio. Chris tunes an audio board while Andrew rests his guitar. A reinforced door opens to the rigging deck. A Wayfarer tour poster curls above a No Charge tape beside the audio board. The back-bar passage returns west to The Static.

Exits: west → Back Bar Ledger, east → Bridgman Rigging Deck.

Interactions: silent jukebox, stickered windows, Wayfarer tour poster, No Charge tape.

#### Signal Stairwell (`spire_signal_stairwell`)

A signal stairwell climbs behind The Static, each landing crowded with antenna wire and old mail slots. The whole shaft hums with pirate broadcasts. A chrono coil console hums on the landing, monitoring temporal flux lines.

Exits: south → The Static, north → Static Safehouse.

Interactions: antenna wire, mail slots, chrono coil console.

#### Static Safehouse (`spire_zekes_apartment`)

The abandoned apartment above The Static smells of dust and cold pipes. Jax has offered it to the crew as a safehouse. A workstation hums beside a planning table under bad light, and roof access opens toward the impossible skyline.

Exits: south → Signal Stairwell, north → Safehouse Roof.

Interactions: workstation, planning table.

#### Safehouse Roof (`spire_safehouse_roof`)

The safehouse roof is barely larger than the apartment below it. A skyline of corporate towers rises beyond laundry lines full of patched coats and disguise materials, and the shield shimmer turns the clouds into a visible cage.

Exits: south → Static Safehouse.

Interactions: skyline, laundry lines.

#### Back Bar Ledger (`spire_the_static_scale_01`)

The Static's back bar hides cracked glasses, ration labels, and a favor slate no customer admits to reading. A chipped tumbler sits beside the slate, marked with names Jax only says after closing. The main bar is west, the subway car east, and the roof stairwell north.

Exits: west → The Static, east → Old Subway Car, north → Roof Stairwell.

Interactions: back bar, favor slate.

#### Roof Stairwell (`spire_the_static_scale_02`)

Concrete roof stairs climb past curfew chalk and old burn marks from drone sweeps. The curfew chalk changes every night, but the scorch line never moves.

Exits: south → Back Bar Ledger, north → Tenant Shrine.

Interactions: roof stairs, curfew chalk.

#### Tenant Shrine (`spire_the_static_scale_03`)

A tenant shrine of folded notices, dead neon, and cheap candles turns eviction paperwork into a memorial wall. The dead neon still flickers when transit drones pass overhead. Interactions: tenant shrine, folded notices, courier satchel, fire escape.

### Night Market

#### Night Market (`spire_night_market`)

The Night Market insists on being heard. Lanterns swing over food steam, Noodle Row shouts through rain, and a lantern bridge crosses the drain like stubborn stars. A market registry broadcast crackles from a speaker above the stalls.

String lights cross the lantern roof in careful knots above the Night Market. The anchor knots keep them up when patrol drones skim too low, and the lantern shadows hide handoffs under color.
