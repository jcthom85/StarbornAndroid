# Worlds 1–2 gear and ability progression

The earned main route reached the World 3 handoff at level 6 with all four crew members and 16 unspent AP. Nova and Zeke get opportunities to try equipment before departure. Orion has a distinct support role as soon as he joins, and Gh0st joins too late to try his kit against the Beast.

| Crew | Story equipment and abilities | Optional choices available by departure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nova** | Starts with **Arc Tether**. Jed supplies the **Mining Pistol** and **Flux Liner**. Cutter surge unlocks **Cryo Vent**, certification unlocks **Hydraulic Kick**, and The Fork unlocks **Blast Wave**. The Warden is the first boss where these early build decisions can be tried. |

## Where upgrades come from

- **Exploration** gives equipment worth checking even on a low-combat route. World 1 offers accessories including the Parts Loft’s Precision Sight, Holding Cell’s Digital Watch and Backup Storage’s Focus Conduit. Optional rooms and quests offer Recoil Dampener and Thermite Core. World 2 adds Tidal Resonator in the Submerged Aethel Vault, Focus Conduit from Ancient Echoes, and Bioluminescent Focus from the grotto or reliquary. The latter is a weapon mod whose element applies if the weapon and earlier mod leave the element open.
- **Combat** guarantees the Laser Blaster from the Warden, Stability Gyro from the Canopy pair, Beast Meat and Ion Poppers from the Hound Nest pair, and Source Resin from the Beast. The Canopy and Hound Nest rewards give those optional encounters a reason to exist alongside XP. The Hound Nest’s supply pack remains guarded.
- **Tinkering** can turn early materials into Power Lens, Ergonomic Grip, Armor Plating or Insulated Lining when the recipes and ingredients are available. World 1 unlocks the first weapon mod slot after the first main quest, first armor after the second, second weapon after the third and second armor after the finale. In World 2, Brawler Rig gives Zeke an equipment trade, Tideglass Day teaches a second source of Source Resin, and Stolen Tech teaches the Rapid Capacitor recipe. The premade capacitor at Breaker Deck works immediately. The crafted one is the quest objective.
- **Snacks** are equipped and have a passive stat bonus and a reusable combat action. Starbar gives +6 Speed and an Evasion action. Ion Poppers give +2 Luck and an Accuracy action. Each active effect lasts two turns and takes a turn to use. Recipes already offer Mineral Trail Mix, Pulse Citrus and Comet Gummies, but the two new rewards let players discover the slot through ordinary play before they find those recipes.

## Encounter comparison

Three deterministic seeds per encounter and loadout. Each run used the earned checkpoint’s HP, party and consumables, the same basic-attack and emergency-healing policy, and no combat assistance. The upgraded runs equipped items obtainable by that checkpoint. The third run for each seed added one snack use at its first eligible turn. Values are medians across seeds.

| Fight | Upgrades equipped | Party actions, starter / equipped / snack used | HP damage taken, starter / equipped / snack used |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Iron Warden** | Starbar on Nova | 19 / 19 / 19 | 54 / 74 / 32 |
| **Resonant Canopy** | Laser Blaster and Starbar on Nova | 19 / 19 / 19 | 19 / 5 / 11 |
| **Source Beast** | Laser Blaster on Nova, Ion Poppers on Zeke | 46 / 42 / 46 | 202 / 244 / 212 |

All 27 runs won. The Warden’s damage varied sharply by seed. The Canopy pair cost zero healing items in all nine runs under this policy. The upgraded Beast kit finished four or five party actions sooner in each seed when the snack was left equipped. Spending a turn on Accuracy at the first opportunity erased most of that action advantage. Damage varied with turn order, so the Beast numbers cannot isolate the contribution of the weapon or the snack. The two snack effects, their cooldowns and keeping the item after battle were verified.

Separate combat runs exercised reactive play. The Warden’s shield drop gives Nova a reason to time pressure. The Canopy pair supported either opening target, and both target orders won across the tested seeds. The Beast still went through Frenzy in the guard and direct-attack runs. These runs show that reacting is possible and that the main route has room to experiment. They leave open whether players notice the opening and whether taking it feels better on a phone.

## Carry forward

- Keep the early route clearable in story gear. Use fights with a recognizable threat to give spending AP, slotting a mod, activating a snack, or running a guard break a reason to matter.
- Start Worlds 3–4 with a focused job for each recruit’s next tool. Give Gh0st a chance to use his precision or Disarm early in Spire. Look for an Orion weapon or armor alternative that changes how he divides turns between Acid pressure and healing. Check what the player has actually earned before the rooftop and Foundry bosses.
- When a fight guards something, tie it to the console, supply pack or passage the enemies are actually defending. One guaranteed, usable reward gives an optional fight a purpose without turning the route into a checklist of compulsory clears.
