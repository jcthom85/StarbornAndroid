Patch Flux Liner ground strip, confirm cutter governor bypass, fire live cutter test surge. Sweep workshop for Film 02 (VHS 2) in Jed's Office, mod bench in Parts Loft, and Flooded Basement chest.
  • Trade Row Sweep (The Scavenger's Stash — w1_sq01)
    Meet Scrapper at his stall to start quest. Loot Cold Locker and open rebel cache in The Hidden Stash. Turn in to Scrapper for 80 XP + 2 Pulse Grenades. Explore Recyc Bar (talk to Miner Bill, inspect jukebox), Card Den, and balance Pump Passage valve.
  • Med-Bay Sweep (System Flush — w1_sq02)
    Meet Doc in Exam Room 1 to start quest. Loot cryogenic locker in Med Supply Closet. Defeat 2 Siren Skimmers in Ventilation Hub. Clear vents in strict sequence: Inspect Vent → Reroute Fans → Clear Toxic Blockage. Turn in to Doc for Corrosive Rounds mod. Sweep Decon Lock and The Morgue.
  • Transit Checkpoint & Clearance (Shift Clearance — w1_mq02)
    Pass Shift Queue to Search Bay; talk to Guard Hank to trigger clearance rejection. Enter Zeke's Booth; select "Grid Instability" to spoof paperwork. Receive Mine Access Badge. Step through Blast Door A into Logistics.

⚠️ Critical Playtest Notes & Implementation Gotchas

🔒 Locked Drain Pool Fishing

In Elevator Landing (mine_landing), the drain pool mentions fishing. This is strictly locked until you examine the pod in World 2. Do not hunt for a fishing rod in World 1.

💾 Pre-Fork Save Discipline

Tuning the Fork at the end of Session 2 triggers Red Alert lockdown, permanently changing room states. Keep your pre-Fork save file in a separate slot so you can reload upper colony content if needed.

🗣️ Two-Step Zeke Chime Dialogue

After the Warden dies, talk to Zeke in Pod Bay first, then follow him into The Pod Core and talk to him second. Touching the navigation console before the second talk will display a locked error.

🚪 Hub Map Return Rule

The "Return to Map" button only works from designated entrance rooms (Pit Landing, Scrap Yard, Trade Row Gate, Triage Hall, Shift Queue, Concourse Lobby). Walk to the entrance room first.
