# World 1 phone acceptance

Use a fresh production route through the World 2 crash site, with no debug grants or instant-win controls. Use additional fresh/earned saves for alternate-order and completionist checks. Record results in [the run template](../testing/run-template.md); see [setup and automation](../testing/WORLD1_PLAYTEST.md).

## Record first

- [ ] Device/model, Android/API, APK version and hash, commit/dirty state, tester/date.
- [ ] Fresh/existing save, starting room, resources, equipped gear and purchased skills.
- [ ] Tutorial, sound/music, text size, large-target and accessibility settings.
- [ ] Screenshots/video and save-slot references for failures. Distinguish game, fixture, instructions and environment issues.

## Fresh main route

- [ ] Play the full intro once; review text speed, fades, portraits and the opening reveal.
- [ ] Find bunk light, door panel, movement and Jed without guessing every object.
- [ ] Accept equipment teaching and manually equip both slots. On a separate start, defer and verify the offer does not nag during travel.
- [ ] Try both loader orders: inspect then fight, and fight before reading its strip. Mira appears after victory; no duplicate Flux Liner is granted.
- [ ] Craft the Cryo-Inductor, patch the suit, confirm governor bypass and run the cutter test. Confirm the journal clearly orders these steps.
- [ ] Let Hank reject the badge; complete Zeke's override. Check the badge reward once and onward routing.
- [ ] Complete Boggs' Guard Break certification using Hydraulic Kick. Workshop loader/cargo practice is optional; deep-mine authorization follows certification and return dialogue.
- [ ] Take the mine descent from the Logistics map. Fight actual mine enemies, restore junction power and reach The Heart.
- [ ] Explain the counter-tune clues before solving. Try a wrong value, leave/return midway, and finish all three stages. Confirm useful feedback, consumed Cryo-Inductor, Fork/Blast Wave and the post-acquisition background.
- [ ] Follow lockdown instructions, clear Cargo Lift security and receive Jed's Chime. Check backtracking dialogue and urgency.
- [ ] Talk to Zeke before and after the Warden. Defeat the boss, authorize the pod with the Chime and reach World 2 with The Launch completed and A Strange Coast active.
- [ ] On a separate earned escape save, defeat the Warden before Jed, return for the handoff, and verify onward progress without a second boss/reward.
