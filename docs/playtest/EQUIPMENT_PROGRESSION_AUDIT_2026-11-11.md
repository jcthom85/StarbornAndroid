# Equipment progression audit — November 11, 2026

## Verdict

The concern about World 3 is correct: it awards **no new base weapons or armor**. It has a substantial optional mod/accessory layer, but that does not replace the satisfaction or tactical variety of earning new weapons. This is a campaign progression gap, not an isolated missing World 3 chest.

The catalog contains **14 weapons, 17 armor items, 36 mods and 45 accessories**. The 31 base-gear entries include retired Ollie equipment. Normal campaign rewards do not expose most of the current four-character weapon/armor catalog. The weapon and armor vendors are defined, but have **no authored campaign room/NPC entrance**. Their gated stock is therefore not a reliable player acquisition route. Debug shop scenarios and bundled sample saves are not campaign acquisition.

This audit reviews authored rewards and production runtime behavior. It is not a new six-world phone playthrough or a combat balance simulation. Existing saves can contain gear from older content or debug scenarios. Reward availability below does not mean every optional branch is necessarily visited on a main-story playthrough.

## Highest-priority findings

1. **Seventeen current-character base-gear choices are stranded behind the unconnected weapon/armor shops.** Nova's Laser Blaster is among them; Nova actually receives the Mining Pistol from Jed. The other three characters have their default equipment prepared by GameBootstrap. Eight alternative weapons and eight alternative armor pieces are also only in those shops.
2. **One Heat Liner can be equipped by several characters simultaneously.** Into the Fire grants one inventory item, not four, and does not auto-equip it on everyone. Generic armor is compatible with all four fighters. equipArmor checks inventory ownership OR the permanent unlocked-armors set, then assigns an owner without reserving a copy. This makes the same item look available to everyone. Crucible Forge-Plate has the same issue.
3. **Equipment ownership is inconsistent across systems.** Mods enforce physical copies and require transfers. Base armor/weapons use permanent unlock sets. Accessories use equippedItems without a comparable copy-count check. Selling and crafting reserve actual equipped copies, which conflicts with the more permissive equip behavior. Once an armor/weapon has entered an unlock set, selling the last unequipped inventory copy does not remove that unlock; equip can still accept it. This needs an explicit ownership policy and save migration, not a special Heat Liner patch.
4. **Tinkering has zero base-weapon or base-armor recipes.** It has twelve equippable-mod recipes. The words “gear” and “armor plating” can suggest broader progression than the recipes actually provide.
5. **Enemies have no direct base-weapon or base-armor drops.** The Beast directly drops Source Resin, a mod. Combat otherwise feeds gear progression mainly through components and room-specific extra drops. These are useful incentives, but currently do not culminate in new base equipment through crafting.
6. **The full shop catalog is gated only by World 1 progress.** Even after connecting shops, simply exposing all stock after the World 1 finale would exhaust eight alternative weapons and eight armor options too early. Distribution needs deliberate pacing.

## Heat Liner, precisely

- Main story: `w4_mq16_enter_airlock` gives **one** Heat Liner and completes Into the Fire. quests.json also describes that reward; QuestRuntimeManager does not independently pay the descriptive reward list.
- Optional: Rylan's favor trades one Heavy Gear and one Hydraulic Fluid for **another** Heat Liner, plus XP/credits.
- Shop: the Welder Bench at Lava Tube Bypass sells Heat Liner for its listed 1,200-credit buy price, subject to shop pricing.
- Stats: defense 6, flat HP +20, vitality +2. Despite its thermal flavor, its equipment definition has no explicit Burn resistance or ambient-heat immunity mechanic.
- Compatibility: all four active fighters. Receiving it does not replace their armor automatically. It is neither four distinct rewards nor a party-wide passive.
- Recommendation: treat generic armor as physical inventory copies. One liner should outfit one fighter; show who is wearing it and offer a transfer. More copies can come from Rylan, purchases or a future recipe. Keep a story route that makes traversing the Foundry plausible without requiring all four to wear the same armor.

### World 3 reward interpretation

Neon Band is an accessory. Phase Rounds is also an accessory (+2 Agility/+2 Focus), **not a weapon and not an attack-element conversion**. It has several legitimate acquisition routes: Prototype Testing, private-leverage Corporate Espionage, an optional executive safe, the Spire arcade and gated lounge stock. Multiple copies are therefore not automatically a reward-duplication bug. The Corporate Espionage publication outcome gives evidence instead of Phase Rounds.

Cyano-Coolant Injector is a mod from Sorrell's confession. The chrono puzzle gives Tachyon Overclock Core. Optional side rooms hold Corrosive Rounds, Precision Core, Stability Gyro Mod, Impact Node, Bulwark Layer, Focus Matrix, Cyber Visor and Syndicate Overclock. A player following mostly the main story can miss much of this layer. Exact sources appear below.

## Recommended design direction — not applied in this change

Use the existing catalog before creating a large new equipment tier. Its single-target, area and charged attacks are different play styles, not a clean “higher number is always better” ladder.

1. Fix ownership consistently for generic armor and accessories, including visible wearer/transfer information. Preserve existing saves and legitimately earned duplicate items. Decide how to migrate armor equipped on more people than the inventory supports.
2. Give Nova a connected Laser Blaster acquisition in World 1 or 2. Keep the Mining Pistol as the opening weapon.
3. Spread the eight alternative weapons and eight alternative armor pieces across Worlds 2–5 through story rewards, exploration/combat caches, side quests and vendors. Do not release the entire catalog through a single early shop.
4. Give World 3 at least one unmistakable base-weapon reward on a required route, plus a fitting armor choice. Nova's Plasma Shotgun and Gh0st's Splitter Edge are existing candidates; decide the exact recipient and encounter context before changing rewards. Do not award every character a full new set at once.
5. Reserve a few charged/heavy weapon choices for Worlds 4–5 and let World 6 refine established builds through Source mods. This adds meaningful choices without an asset-heavy overhaul.
6. Make combat supply those choices: distinctive components for crafting upgrades, guaranteed first-clear equipment or schematics at selected encounters, and repeatable resources for additional copies. Keep normal trash drops modest.
7. Add targeted base-gear crafting only where there is a clear fantasy. For example, thermal armor assembly or repairing a recovered prototype would connect components to equipment without duplicating every vendor item as a recipe.
8. Update quest rewards, item wording and walkthroughs together once the distribution is agreed. In particular, do not describe Phase Rounds as a weapon or imply Heat Liner is a four-person payout.

## How these rewards interact with combat and crafting

Base weapon types are character-specific: Nova uses guns, Zeke gloves, Orion jewels and Gh0st swords. Character armor types are similarly restricted; generic armor such as Heat Liner and Forge-Plate is compatible with all four. Mods currently use the same pool for weapon and armor modification slots. The slot needs a base weapon/armor equipped, and the four slots unlock after World 1 MQ01, MQ02, MQ03 and MQ05 respectively. A character cannot stack two copies of the same mod type, and every simultaneously equipped mod needs an owned copy.

Attack-element conversion only matters through weapon-mod slots. Combat gives the base weapon's own element precedence over mod elements, then uses the first applicable weapon-mod element. A Freeze mod does not necessarily turn Orion's inherently Source weapon or Zeke's inherently Shock weapon into Freeze. Acquiring an elemental reward therefore needs clear preview information to communicate its real value.

Required story progress mainly grants access, XP, crew abilities and key items, rather than a continuing stream of base equipment. Cooking and fishing provide recovery, buffs and component supply; tinkering turns that supply into mods. Selected encounters already provide guaranteed extra components, including the World 3 Service Dumbwaiter circuit board, Fuel Bridge wiring, and World 4 Coolant Spillway Pure Iron / Defect Choir circuit board. The missing link is a satisfying base-equipment destination for some of that resource economy.

No balance change should be justified simply by counting catalog items. Area attacks, charged attacks, defenses and accuracy tradeoffs should be tested with earned resources and ordinary combat participation once their acquisition routes are in place. Required story equipment should remain obtainable without arcade high scores or repeated optional grinding.

### Crafting chain and supply constraints

- Power Lens / Ergonomic Grip / Armor Plating / Insulated Lining are the basic component-to-mod path. They do not replace the character's base weapon or armor.
- Source Resin: herb + beast meat. The Tideglass optional quest gives the schematic; the Beast also drops a finished resin. The schematic must be learned from inventory.
- Rapid Capacitor: one Dominion Transmitter Core + scrap + wiring. The recovered transmitter quest supplies its core and schematic. This is a limited core route, so additional capacitors are more naturally supplied by the Foundry shop or Quality Control reward.
- Photon Core consumes a finished Source Resin plus a lens and wiring. Resonance Capacitor consumes a finished Rapid Capacitor plus Source Resin and nano-filament. Those inputs are already usable gear; the player gives up that copy to upgrade.
- Nanite Plating consumes Armor Plating Mk. I + two nano-filaments + scrap. Thermite Core requires two Pure Iron; Orbital Deflector requires two Composite Plates; Astral Weave requires two Astral Threads. These materials can be found before their nominal world in optional caches or shops, so the world flavor is not a hard availability gate.
- Nano-filament is a particularly important shared bottleneck for Nanite Plating, Resonance Capacitor, Orbital Deflector and Astral Weave. There are cache, fishing and arcade sources, not just the single early story pickup.
- Fishing salvage and raw salvage support the crafting economy. In particular, armor_plate salvages into two scrap and one ballistic weave. Raw salvage returns are defined in CraftingService, not recipes_tinkering.json.
