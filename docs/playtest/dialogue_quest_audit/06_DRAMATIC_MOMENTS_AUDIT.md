# Starborn dramatic moments audit

November 14, 2026

## Assessment

The campaign has a clear dramatic progression. Nova's cutter accident, Jed's decision, Gh0st lowering his rifle, and the Memory Stair refusals give later scenes specific actions to recall. The strongest scenes let a character commit to something and give the player time to register the cost. The weakest scenes put that decision in a notification after the crew has already moved.

The highest-priority stretch is **Elara's reunion through the Tear and the ending**. It combines a reunion, Nova's first Stasis, Vale's execution of Thorne, and the decision to follow Elara. The Throne Room now has a proper conversation. The action around that conversation is still largely described, and the mandatory story leaves Elara's fate unstated. Next are **Jed's lift aftermath** and **Gh0st's decision to spare Rylos**. Both need a small, concrete physical beat that pays off dialogue already in place.

I mapped all 30 numbered main quests and screened all 32 authored side quests for character turns, revelations, irreversible choices, and payoffs. I checked their event order, dialogue, cinematic catalog, room variants, combat handoffs, and travel styles against the story reference and the current route. The table covers the beats that carry dramatic weight, with quieter gameplay objectives grouped around them. Previous emulator evidence covers Astra boarding, the Spire Lens, Memory Stair choices, the Choir Lock and the illustrated ending. This audit is source-led. Shot readability, audio balance, and pacing for every scene still need a continuous device playthrough.

**Keep** means the chosen presentation fits the beat. **Polish** means the event is staged and needs a targeted refinement. **Priority** marks a missing action, consequence, or link the main story relies on.

## Production order

1. **Map Elara's last on-screen action and intended fate.** Then stage the Anchor, failed Stasis, and Tear as a continuous escalation. Give one mandatory scene a concrete answer.
2. **Give Jed and Rylos their physical aftermath.** These can use existing spaces, portraits, and sound. Jed gets the separation the finale remembers. Gh0st gets a decision the player watches him make.
3. **Build the handoffs.** Use the existing Airlane callback to establish the illustrated format. Apply it to the pod crash and the outer-Shield/coup flight. Let Spire-to-Foundry be a shorter escape, and give the Tear its own visual language. Keep repeat boarding brief. None of the five current inter-world handoffs has cold-open-style illustrated staging.
4. **Polish the Archive and the last act.** Make authority change visibly at the Archive and Ring. Stage the reunion and Vale's second form. Use short room reactions for optional consequences before committing to more illustrations.

Play acceptance should cover the actual starting room and interaction, presentation order around combat and quest rewards, audio with the screen visible, reading at phone size, reduced motion, and watch/skip/resume where applicable. Check the transition into the next playable state as well as the scene. Capture reactions to the losses and choices that pay off later before making a blanket timing change.
