Audit Deliverable World 1: The Mines Starborn JRPG

World 1 Narrative & Dialogue Audit

Comprehensive developmental edit & evaluation of dialogue, prose, characterization, continuity, and staging across Stellarium Colony 4.

1. Executive Assessment

World 1 of Starborn (Stellarium Colony 4: "The Mines") has an exceptionally strong narrative backbone. Its inciting incident is rooted in labor, maintenance, and physical causality rather than generic chosen-one tropes. Nova deliberately confirms her cutter's governor bypass at 87 kHz to beat quota, sparking a breaker surge (68% cold loop, 180° ground) that flags her file for recycling and wakes the buried Tuning Fork.

The core main-path cast (Nova, Jed, Zeke, Doc, Scrapper) speaks in crisp, subtext-rich blue-collar voices. However, staging and state-gating contradictions during Act 2's lockdown, an orphaned side-quest objective (w1_sq03), and a silent mid-act Zeke placement in Logistics weaken the emotional payoff of the climax.

2. Category Scores (1–10)

A. Dialogue Quality8.5

Main path is crisp, terse, and subtext-rich. Minus points for verbose Easter egg lines and occasional mechanical tooltips in speech.

B. Characterization8.5

Strong distinct voices. Zeke has a missing conversational bridge in admin_window before the escape.

C. Story Structure & Pacing7.5

Act 1 setup is 10/10. Act 2 climax loses tension due to lack of a Red Alert backtrack gate and split w1_sq03 tasks.

D. Emotional Impact7.5

Jed's written farewell and Lost Shift datapad are powerful, but Jed's sacrifice in launch_lift suffers from idle enemy overlap and sudden vanishing.

E. Humor & Personality8.5

Gallows workplace humor (CLOSED: WITHIN QUOTA, Hank's door philosophy, Scrapper's fence talk) lands naturally.

F. Prose & Worldbuilding8.5

82 rooms offer concrete, tactile descriptions with post-surge room reactivity. A few minor POV shifts ("you" vs. "Nova").

G. Narrative Continuity6.5

Core 87/68/180° chain is rock solid, but Orion's prologue portrait spoiler, Gaga's meta talk, and unkept SQ04 admin promises drag continuity down.

4. Detailed Findings & Recommendations
P1 Jed Sacrifice Staging & Contradictory Dialogue in launch_lift
rooms.json:3361-3432 | dialogue.json:191-235, 351-359 | events.json:2619-2786

The Problem: When Nova enters launch_lift during w1_mq04, hostile units (acoustic_bulwark + resonance_buoy) are active on the platform. If the player taps Jed before clearing them, Jed delivers jed_w1_launch_lift_idle: "Go, Nova! I've got the manual bypass wedged with my wrench. If I step away, the lift locks down. Get to the Launch Bay!" This contradicts the fact that North is blocked, he hasn't given her the Chime yet, and he later says "I've tried the wrench already... it bent." Furthermore, after the handoff completes, Jed vanishes from the room while Nova is still standing on the lift platform.

Recommendation: Re-key the idle dialogue to reflect the active fight, and have w1_mq04_complete warp the lift car into launch_checkpoint as the blast doors seal between them so Nova physically leaves Jed behind as the lift ascends.

- Jed: "Go, Nova! I've got the manual bypass wedged with my wrench. If I step away, the lift locks down. Get to the Launch Bay!"+ Jed: "Clear that shield unit off the platform, Nova! The brake pawl's sheared—my wrench bent trying to wedge it, so I'm holding the lever by hand!"
P1 Unrestricted Backtracking to Peacetime Homestead During Red Alert
rooms.json:38-98, 2889-2962 | events.json:2560-2617 | 00_world_story.md:511

The Problem: Once ms_w1_mq03_complete fires, event narration announces that containment blast doors are sealing in sequence behind Nova. Yet none of the backward room exits or hub node travel are locked. A player backtracking during Red Alert or after Jed's death finds miners eating soup in the mess hall and Guard Hank calmly checking shift badges.

Recommendation: Enforce the Point-of-No-Return at echo_heart (south to echo_walkway) and launch_access (south to echo_exit) with a locked message: "The containment bulkhead is sealed behind Nova. The only open route is forward through the maintenance strobes toward the Launch Bay."

7. Future Worlds Guidance (Worlds 3–6)

Principles to Carry Forward

  • Physical Causality Over Relic Magic: Future Echo Relics (The Lens, Anvil, Anchor, Key) should derive puzzle tuning parameters from concrete heist/engineering actions earlier in that world, just like 87 kHz / 68% / 180°.
  • Systemic Horror via Paperwork: Retain Dominion's evil as spreadsheets, quotas, and transit passes before boss fights occur.
  • Restraint in Affection: Characters show love through their craft (Zeke spoofing clearances, Orion regulating coolant, Gh0st watching blind spots).

What NOT to Duplicate

  • Do not repeat the "accidental fugitive" arc: In Worlds 3–5, Nova should be a deliberate instigator, not an accidental breaker-surger.
  • Do not keep Zeke panicked: By World 3 (The Spire), Zeke should weaponize his insider bureaucracy fluency.
